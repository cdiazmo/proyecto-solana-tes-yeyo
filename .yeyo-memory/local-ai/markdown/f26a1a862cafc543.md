## Purpose and scope
This document is intended to evaluate the implications of replacing the existing TES System plate heat exchangers, for a new set of heat exchangers of the tube and shell. The new proposed heat exchangers will have different pressure drops and will be located at a different location and at heights of the existing ones. To evaluate the consequences of installing this equipment on the existing HTF and Molten Salts circuits, a set of hydraulic calculations were simulated using Pipeflo ®, for both systems and different operating cases.

## Molten Salts Circuit
The plant includes 6 identical and independent molten salts circuits. For simulation purposes the following equipment where considered:
- One (1) hot salts tank (1/../6 -MT-252)
- One (2) cold salts tank (1/../6 -MT-251)
- One (1) HTF-to-Salt Heat Exchanger (1/../6 -MX-251)
- One (1) hot salts pump (1/../6 -MP-252)
- One (1) cold salts pump (1/../6 -MP-251)

## Hypothesis and Assumptions
The following assumptions have been considered for the calculations:
- Pipes length and fittings details based on the 3D Model (Solana_14-06-16) and P&IDs collection.
- Salts tanks operating pressure: 14.5 psia.
- Cold molten salts temperature: Charge mode : 556 °F, Discharge mode: 556 °F and 564 °F for the Normal and Winter Discharge modes, respectively.
- Hot molten salts temperature: 730 °F.
- Pumps height : 3’
- Heat Exchanger Inlet nozzle height: Discharge mode: 36,09 ft, Charge mode: 6,56 ft
- Heat Exchanger Outlet nozzle height: Discharge mode: 6,56 ft, Charge mode: 36,09 ft
- Heat Exchanger pressure drop: based on the Lointek’s tender datasheet (extrapolated from the design flow pressure drop).
- Pumps performance curve: as specified in its datasheet.

## Hypothesis and Assumptions
- Pipes length and fittings details based on the 3D Model (Solana ALL 2015-08-13) and P&IDs collection
- Cold HTF Temperature: ranging from 545 °F to 598°F
- Hot HTF temperature: ranging from 718 °F to 738°F
- Pumps performance curve as specified in its datasheet
- HTF flow to Solar Fields: minimum 50,000 gpm (25,000 gpm per field)
- Pumps suction pressure: 172 psia
- Heat Exchanger Inlet nozzle height: discharge mode 36,09 ft, charge mode 6,56 ft
- Heat Exchanger Outlet nozzle height: discharge mode 6,56 ft, charge mode 36,09 ft
- Temporary strainers at the main pumps suction removed
- Heat Exchanger pressure drop based on Lointek’s tender datasheet (extrapolated from design flow pressure drop)
- Operating flows as indicated on initial heat exchangers datasheets
- Far field pumps operation considerations: 8 out of 10 running with even flows, designed as fixed speed pumps at maximum (1802 rpm)

# Simulation Results and Conclusions

For all cases, the simulation results presented no issues with the existing pumps and heat exchangers control valves, and the system was always balanced.

The new set of heat exchangers and installed pumps can handle all operation cases. The simulations show that for all cases, the pump can run at a speed below its maximum speed (1132 rpm) and the control valve opening percentage bigger than 50% (for some cases in the 90%-95% opening range).
