## Valve Specifications

### TA58V
- **Class:** 300 Buttweld
- **Bore Sizes:** 2” (Sch 80), 3” – 18” (Std. Wt.)
- **Material:** 316 Stainless steel
- **Gate and Bonnet:** Bolted and extended, OS & Y, rising stem
- **Disc:** 13 CR flexible or solid wedge disc, stellited gate and seats
- **Primary Stem Seal:** Bellows seal, replaceable with valve in position, minimum fatigue life of 10,000 cycles
- **Secondary Stem Seal:** Flexitallic brand “Thermiculite” processed vermiculite mineral

### FB58V
- **Class:** 300 Buttweld
- **Bore Sizes:** 2” (Sch 80), 3” – 18” (Std. Wt.), 20” – 30” (X-Stg.)
- **Material:** Steel globe
- **Bonnet:** Bolted and extended, OS & Y, rising stem
- **Disc:** 13 CR Plug disc, stellited seat and disc
- **Primary Stem Seal:** Bellows seal, replaceable with valve in position, minimum fatigue life of 10,000 cycles
- **Secondary Stem Seal:** Alternating layers of Teflon washers and graphite impregnated stainless steel braid
- **Stem Length:** Flexitallic brand “Thermiculite” processed vermiculite mineral
