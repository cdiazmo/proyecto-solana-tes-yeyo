- External painting: Yes - Nitrogen (0.045 barg)

## Construction information
- Manufacturer's paint criteria shall be submitted with the bid for buyer approval before any possible awarding.

## Scope of supply
- Anchor bolts, anchor plate and template for bases (fix and sliding supports) and Cogetherm insulation.
- Insulation clips.
- Nameplate.
- Thermal limits: heating up and heating down ramps, maximum temperature gradient between empty equipment and entering fluid (nitrogen or salts).

## Notes
- To be confirmed by pump supplier
- The tank can suffer temperature step changes close to 212º F up to a maximum of 718º F.
- Manhole to be located on top. If there is not enough space, manhole will not be installed and access to the tank will be through the pump hole.
- Tank will be N2 blanketed during stand-by periods. Also pressurized N2 will be used to empty the tank during drainage operation.
- The weld for transportation caps has to be lower than the use for the hydraulic test and has to protect the nozzle mechanized according ASME 16/25.
- Trace content of salts can remain in the tank after draining after cooling.
- For a more detailed drawing of this tank see Page 4.

## Scheme
- Horizontal design with a cylindrical body, slope of 1%.
- Tank capacity: 5.7 m³.
- Fluid type: Molten Salts (60%NaNO3, 40%KNO3) / Nitrogen.
- Design standard: Korbbogen.
