# Instrument Specification

## General
- **Supplier**: The instrument supply company chosen for the project.
- **Customer**: Abener Teyma.
- **Fabricator**: Any instrument assembly shop subcontracted by Abener Teyma (if applicable).
- **Owner**: Abengoa Solar Inc.
- **Attachments**: Considered part of this specification and Supplier shall be bound to adhere to these specifications.

## Scope
- **Scope of Work**: Supply of all instruments as listed in the request for quote needed for the project, including cables, gaskets, manifolds, etc.
- **Packaging**: To be defined later.
- **Labeling/Tagging**: Specific requirements to be defined later.
- **Delivery**: Supplier shall deliver all material to Abener Teyma selected Fabricator(s) and/or project jobsite. Shipments should be scheduled for full loads to reduce transportation costs.
- **Exclusions**: Certain items are excluded from this subcontract.
- **Contact Person**: Supplier shall provide a single contact person to monitor and track all material purchased under this contract, available for update meetings in Abener Teyma St. Louis office or via teleconference.
