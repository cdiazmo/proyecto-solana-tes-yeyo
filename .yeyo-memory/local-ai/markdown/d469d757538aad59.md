# 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001 Project Design Criteria

## Resumen

- Project: Solana
- Alternate billing cycle allowed by contract
- Confidential information with strict usage restrictions
- Site located in Maricopa County, Arizona
- Design basis is equipment-specific
- Structural design categories include solar collector field, central plant power block, and firewater storage tank
- Wind load calculations based on ASCE 7-05
- Earthquake loads calculated per ASCE 7-05
- Site Class D with coefficients F<sub>A</sub> = 1.6 and F<sub>V</sub> = 2.4
- Design spectral response acceleration coefficients for short period (S<sub>MS</sub>) and 1-sec period (SM<sub>1</sub>)
- Seismic design category C
- Noise level requirement of 85 dBA SPL @ 3 ft.

### Site Information
- Location: Maricopa County, Arizona
- Elevation: 735 ft
- Temperature: 122°F (max), 25°F (min)
- Barometric Pressure: 14.30 psia
- Building Code: IBC-2006
- Basic Wind Speed: 90 MPH
- Prevailing Wind Direction: South West
- Annual Rainfall: 7 inches
- 100 Year Rainfall (24 hour): 0.17 in/hr, 1 hour: 2.5 in/hr

### Design Basis
Design basis is equipment specific.

### Structural Design Basis
#### Occupancy Category for Wind and Seismic Importance Factors (ASCE 7-05 Table 1-1)
- Solar collector field: Category II
- Central plant power block: Category III
- Firewater storage tank and firewater pump building: Category IV

#### Wind Load
- Basic Wind Speed: 90 MPH
- Wind Exposure Category: C
- Wind Importance Factor (IW): 1.00 for Category II, 1.15 for Categories III and IV
- Wind Directionality Factor (KD): 0.85 for buildings/signs, 0.95 for round chimneys/tanks
- Topographic Factor (KZT): 1.0
- Gust Effect Factor (G): 0.85

#### Earthquake Loads
- Seismic Importance Factor (IE): 1.0 for Category II, 1.25 for Category III
