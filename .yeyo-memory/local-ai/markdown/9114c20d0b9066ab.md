# 4521-ESP-ANC-58-61-PS002

## Resumen

- Contract allows alternate billing cycles other than thirty days.
- Project is a concentrated solar power facility in Maricopa County, Arizona.
- Piping Installation Subcontractor installs all pipe lines and components as shown on drawings.
- All welded metallic piping 3” and larger will be shop fabricated.

## 4.1.2 Field Welds for HTF Service Piping
All field welds (FW) for HTF service piping shall receive 100% radiographic examination. Since socketweld joints cannot be radiographed, all socket weld connections shall be examined by visual in-process examination.

## 4.1.3 Other Services Weld Examinations
For all other services, examination will be performed on 5% of all welds. Welds to be examined will be selected by an Abener Teyma representative. Welds will be examined using Ultrasonic Testing (UT). UT testing procedure will employ both Shearwave and Phased Array Ultrasonic Test modes. Any weld defects found will be followed by additional progressive sampling and examinations per B 31.3, paragraph 341.3.4.

Each weld tested shall have the following documentation:
1) Table of welds tested with pass/fail summary
2) Cut plan view field sketch of each weld tested indicating defects, flaws and clean/passed weld portions
3) Ultrasonic data printout generated by UT unit showing defect indication and machine parameters
4) Reviewable electronic file backup of weld scan UT data

# Special Considerations
## Fabrication Tolerances
### Scope
This specification shows tolerances for end preparation, dimensioning and component alignment in the fabrication of metallic piping.

### Reference Standards
The latest approved editions of the following standards apply, except as modified herein: Factory-Made Wrought Steel Building Fittings - ASME B16.9; Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings - ASME B16.5; ASM E B16.47 Series A, only. Fabricating Tolerances - PFI ES-3. (PFI - Pipe Fabrication Institute, Springdale, PA)

### Tolerances
(1) End Preparation: Pipe, fitting and valve ends shall be prepared for welding as specified in the welding procedure specification. Preferred practice is shown in Figure 1.
(2) Linear Dimensions: Tolerances shown in Figure 2 apply to face-to-face, face-to-end and end-to-end measurements of fabricated piping spools and to center-to-center, center-to-face and center-to-end measurements of piping offsets, bands, nozzles and support attachments.
(3) Angular Tolerances: The included angle of a pipe bend shall not deviate more than +0.5 degree from that specified.
(4) Exceptions: Closer tolerances shown on drawings supersede this specification.
