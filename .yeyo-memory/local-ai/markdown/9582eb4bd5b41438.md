# 4521-CAL-AEE-58-11-0002-GAR Rev 02 TES Circuit Hydraulic Calculation

## Resumen

- Document title: TES main pumps hydraulic calculation report
- Client: Arizona Solar One, Project: HEX replacement TES Solana
- Document number: 4521-CAL-AEE-58-11-0002-GAR, Revision: 02
- Purpose: Evaluate implications of replacing existing TES System plate heat exchangers with new Lointek H&MB heat exchangers
- Simulations conducted using Pipeflo® for different operating modes in Molten Salts and HTF circuits
- Assumptions include pipe details, tank pressures, temperatures, pump heights, and heat exchanger specifications
- HTF circuit is a closed system with backpressure from an expansion tank.
- System includes 10 main pumps (2 in standby), 6 HTF-to-Salts heat exchangers, and 2 steam generators.
- Simulation scenarios include pump at optimum speed, maximum speed, and hydraulic limit.
- Pumps operate at speeds adjusted for control valves and Steam Generator operation.
- Overdesign factors calculated for charge and discharge operations.
- 4% overdesign for discharge operation compared to Winter Discharge Mode.

## Purpose and Scope
This document is intended to evaluate the implications of replacing the existing TES System plate heat exchangers, for a new set of heat exchangers of the tube and shell based on Lointek’s H&MB 4521-CAL-LOI-039-062-0002-GAR. The new proposed heat exchangers will have different pressure drops and will be located at a different location and at heights of the existing ones.

## Molten Salts Circuit Description & Considerations
The plant includes 6 identical and independent molten salts circuits. For simulation purposes, the following equipment was considered: one hot salts tank, two cold salts tanks, one HTF-to-Salt Heat Exchanger, one hot salts pump, and one cold salts pump.

## Hypothesis and Assumptions
The following assumptions have been considered for the calculations:
- Pipes length and fittings details based on the 3D latest Model and P&IDs collection.
- Salts tanks operating pressure: 14.5 psia.
- Cold molten salts temperature: Charge mode: 556 °F, Discharge mode: 556 °F and 564 °F for the Normal and Winter Discharge modes, respectively.
- Hot molten salts temperature: 730 °F.
- Pumps suction height : 1 ft
- Heat Exchanger Inlet nozzle height: Charge mode: 16,67 ft, Discharge mode: 37,49 ft
- Heat Exchanger Outlet nozzle height: Charge mode: 37,49 ft, Discharge mode: 16,67 ft
- Heat Exchanger pressure drop: based on the vendor’s Heat & Mass Balances
- Pumps performance curve: as specified in its datasheet.
- Control valve’s Cv based on vendor’s datasheet

# HTF Circuit Description & Considerations

The HTF circuit is a closed system with backpressure ensured by the expansion tank. It consists of 10 main pumps (2 in standby), sending cold HTF to the solar field and through 6 identical HTF-to-Salts heat exchangers, followed by two steam generators.

For simulation purposes:
- Ten (10) HTF Far Field Pumps (MP-200 A/B/C/D/E/F/G/H/J/K) - 8 running, 2 spare
- Pumps suction pressure: 172 psia (set by the expansion tank)
- Six (6) HTF-to-Salt Heat Exchanger (MX-251)
- Two (2) identical steam generators in parallel

### Hypothesis and Assumptions

- Pipes length and fittings details based on the latest 3D Model and P&IDs collection
- Cold HTF Temperature: ranging from 545 °F to 598°F
- Hot HTF temperature: ranging from 718 °F to 738°F
- Pumps performance curve as specified in their datasheet
- Solar field designed with induced pressure drop
- Temporary strainers at main pumps suction removed
- Heat Exchanger pressure drop based on vendor’s Heat & Mass Balances
- Operating flows as indicated on initial heat exchangers datasheets
- Far field pumps operation considerations: 8 out of 10 running (2 spare), equipped with VFDs, speeds adjusted for control valves and Steam Generator operation
