# 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N185 Specification for Field Applied Paint

## Resumen

- This document outlines specifications for field-applied paint on a Solana project.
- It includes notices about alternate billing cycles, extended payment provisions, and certification periods.
- Instructions require initialing on cover sheets and identifying revisions in the body of the specification.
- The scope covers surface preparation and application of coatings and paint in the field.
- General requirements include proper material delivery, storage facilities, labor provision, and adherence to codes.
- Certain materials are excluded from painting, and operations must comply with safety regulations.
- Drop-cloths are used to protect surfaces during painting.
- Contract allows alternate billing cycles, extended payment and certification periods.
- Paint application is restricted to dry surfaces above dew point temperature.
- Surface preparation varies for different materials like carbon steel, stainless steel, and pre-painted steel.
- Stainless steel requires special care during cleaning and sandblasting.
- Uncoated carbon steel surfaces must be prepared according to paint standards.

## Paint Application Conditions
Do not apply paint in rain, snow, fog, or mist, or to any surface that may be wetted before paint has dried. No coating shall be applied unless surface temperature is a minimum of 5°F above dew point.

## Surface Preparation
Surface preparation will be in accordance with the Steel Structures Painting Council per individual paint specification.

### Masking
Surfaces that are not to be painted, such as sight glasses, name plates, identification numbers, instructions, flange faces, and valve gauges, shall be masked with durable materials that will protect against abrasive blast cleaning, paint, and effects of weather. When feasible, Paint Contractor shall touch-up and seal nameplate edges after masking has been stripped.

### Sand Blasting
All sandblasting shall be done on a case-to-case basis so as not to interfere with work of other construction crafts. Paint Contractor must get approval from Construction Superintendent before starting any sandblasting. If necessary, sandblasting shall be done during off-shift hours. Used blasting sand shall be removed from the work areas immediately after completion of surface preparation operations.

#### Carbon Steel Surfaces
Materials used in abrasive blast cleaning shall be 16-30 mesh sand, 40-50 mesh steel grit. Particulate sizes larger or smaller than the ranges designated may be used with the permission of the Purchaser provided they produce the required profile depth. Surface preparation details will be per paint standard or specification.

#### Stainless Steel
Care must be taken to ensure mechanical cleaning methods do not contaminate stainless steel surfaces.

##### Sand Blasting on Stainless Steel
(a) Sandblast equipment must be cleaned before use to assure that all old iron contaminated sand has been removed, (b) sand and mineral grit must be iron and zinc free, and (c) sandblast material must be new and not recovered/re-used. Cleaning brushes and tools used must not contain iron and they must not have been previously used on iron containing materials.

##### Chlorinated Solvents on Stainless Steel
Do not use chlorinated solvents on stainless steel surfaces.

### Uncoated Carbon Steel Surfaces
All steel surfaces that may not have any coating at all, including unpainted carbon steel pipes, shall be prepared for painting per paint standard or specification.

### Pre-Painted Steel
Steel that has been coated in the shop with a prime and seal coat prior to delivery to the project site shall have abrasions, weld seams, and heat-affected zones cleaned for painting by wire brushing or other hand cleaning methods.

### Shop Primed Surfaces with Epoxy Primer
The specified epoxy primer must be seal coated within one year of application. Otherwise, the primer must be brush blasted to provide a proper surface profile prior to application of the seal coat. Unless otherwise indicated, the painter should assume a brush blast using fine (40 mesh) sand according to SSPC-SP 7 is required to get an adequate profile to insure adhesion.

### Shop Primed Surfaces with Inorganic Zinc Primer
Surfaces that have been shop primed prior to delivery to the job site with inorganic zinc primer shall have voids, damaged and defective areas cleaned by wire brushing or other hand cleaning methods before applying the topcoat. If white powder (zinc oxide) substances are visible on the outside surface of the primer, most of it must be high-pressure water washed and scrubbed off the primer prior to application of the seal coat. There is no specified re-coat time for this paint.

### Shop Seal Coated Surfaces with High Temperature Paint
There is no specified re-coat time for this paint. Painted surfaces remain soft until paint has been heat cured. Process heat is used for the curing process. Touch-up paint, prior to cure, can be applied without surface preparation. Cured paint surfaces must be brush blasted prior to application of touch-up paint.

### Inter-Coat Contamination
Surfaces that become contaminated with deposits of any nature shall be cleaned by approved means before application of a succeeding coat of paint.

## 3.9 Mist Coat
When mist coat is requested over the seal coat, surfaces shall be prepared for painting by solvent cleaning, air blast, or brush blast as necessary to remove any visual contaminants.

## 4. Application of Paint
### 4.1 Scheduling
#### 4.1.1 Application of prime coat or finish coat shall be made on the same working day in which surface is prepared.
#### 4.1.2 Application shall be made with a minimum of delay after blast cleaning is completed. Blasted surfaces shall be painted on the same day before rust bloom occurs. If surfaces are allowed to become wet or to stand overnight before priming or finish coating, surface preparation will have to be repeated.
#### 4.1.3 Paint shall not be applied when atmospheric and/or environmental conditions would (1) deter from the quality of the finished product, or (2) be injurious to adjacent property. The subcontractor shall install and maintain a windsock or similar device to monitor wind direction.

### 4.2 Touch-up Painting
Surfaces that have been painted in the shop with a self-priming paint or prime and seal coat prior to the delivery to the project site shall have welds, heat effected zones, abrasions and other damaged areas spot-cleaned per Section 3 of this specification. These areas shall be painted with the same paint as the original coat. The resulting dry film thickness shall be the same as the original coat. Refer to Section 6 of this specification, the Pipeline Schedule, associated piping isometrics, and the Paint & Insulation Schedule for shop-painted items.

### 4.3 Shop Primed Surfaces
Surfaces that have been primed in the shop shall be painted with the seal coat as specified in applicable paint standard. Shop primed surfaces shall be cleaned as specified under Section 3.5 of this specification prior to the application of seal coat. Refer to Section 6 of this specification, the Pipeline Schedule, associated piping isometrics, and the Paint & Insulation Schedule for shop-primed items.

### 4.4 Application Procedure
All painting procedures, workmanship, etc., shall be governed by the paint manufacturer's instructions, the applicable sections of Steel Structures Painting Council Spec. SSPC-PA-1-64, and this specification.
#### 4.4.1 Remove all residues from cleaned surfaces with a clean brush or with dry, oil-free compressed air.
#### 4.4.2 Each application of material shall be complete, full coverage coat that develops the specified dry film thickness as measured on uniform surface areas. Where "touch-up" is specified, complete coverage shall be achieved over the bare area, feathered well back over the sound paint remaining after the cleaning operation.
#### 4.4.3 Paint may be applied by spray apparatus, roller or brush. Painting techniques that result in uniform, wet pattern shall be used. All coats shall be well worked into joints, corners, crevices, etc., and evenly distributed over flat surfaces.
#### 4.4.4 Each coat of paint shall be in a proper state of cure or dryness before the application of the succeeding coat unless otherwise directed in the paint manufacturer's printed instructions.
#### 4.4.5 Traffic and other work on painted surfaces must be prevented until the paint has cured.

## 5 Quality Control
### 5.1 Unless otherwise agreed, the purchaser or the inspector as his representative will observe work in progress and will inspect completed work for compliance with specified end conditions.
### 5.2 Surface Preparation Quality
#### 5.2.1 Acceptability of surfaces prepared by hand cleaning or power tool cleaning will be judged on the basis of visual examination.
#### 5.2.2 The end condition of blast cleaning described in Section 3 will be judged by visual comparison with profile panels such as Clemtex, Ltd. surface profile gauges and cleanliness standards such as NACE Standard TM-01-70.

### 5.3 Painted Surfaces
#### 5.3.1 Inspection of each coat of paint may be made at the Purchasers’ discretion. Film thickness and workmanship of the completed system will be examined.
#### 5.3.2 Film thickness specifications must be met by measurement of plane surfaces. Coverage of edges will be judged by visual examination.
