## Resumen

- This document is an engineering specification for the supply of instruments for the Solana project.

- Supplier responsibilities include delivering materials to designated locations and providing a single contact person for tracking material purchases.

- Vendor data and approval drawings required.
- Monthly shipping of 2 units starting January 2012.

- Confidentiality agreement in place.
