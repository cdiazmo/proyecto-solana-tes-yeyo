## Purpose
### 2.1 Scope of Work
This specification defines the scope of work, insulating materials, and guidelines for the application of external insulation and weatherproofed protective cover systems onto heat traced piping and equipment in a solar plant for the purpose of conserving heat and maintaining process temperatures.

### 2.2 Material Basis
Mineral wool is the basis for the design. Details for material can be found in the attachments of this specification.

## Insulation Requirements
1. Surfaces to be insulated shall be clean and dry. The Contractor shall be responsible for the removal of oil, grease, dust, and similar accumulations of surface dirt.
2. Mill scale, rust scale or deposits or similar firmly adhering accumulations are not the Contractor's responsibility but should be reported to the Abener Teyma Activity Manager.
3. All insulating work shall be performed in a neat and workmanlike manner, of high quality, and consistent with current, acceptable standards of the industry.
4. Insulation shall be applied with care considering factors such as expansion, contraction, wind and water damage, frost prevention, breathing of hot insulation, weatherproofing, electrical cabling, and overall appearance and long life.
5. Insulation and jacketing shall be applied to allow for thermal expansion without creating gaps or hot spots in the insulation or damaging the jacketing.
6. Wet or damp insulation shall not be used and should be disposed of properly.
7. Insulation and jacketing shall be protected from mechanical damage during construction, with repairs at Contractor's expense.
8. Finish installation with systems at operating conditions, repairing joints, separations, and cracking due to thermal movement.
9. Damaged insulation shall be repaired by applying the same facing material over damaged areas, extending patches at least 4" beyond damaged areas, adhering, stapling, and sealing similar to butt joints.
10. Abener Teyma Activity Manager shall have final approval and say over the quality and level completeness/approval of all 'Work'.
11. Cut insulation in a manner to avoid compressing insulation more than 75% of its nominal thickness.
12. Equipment nameplates and code inspection plates shall not be covered, with openings finished neatly and edges sealed with a jacket or covering to prevent moisture entry.

## General Application Guidelines
1. Use pre-formed, molded, or shaped insulation segments when available.
2. Insulation up to 4" thick may be applied in a single layer for piping temperatures below 500°F; thicker insulation or higher temperatures require two (2) layers or more.
3. Insulation shall be applied in standard length sections.

### Expansion Joints
Provide expansion joints to accommodate differences in thermal expansion of the insulation and metal.

### Insulation of Valves and Flanges
Valves and flanges shall be insulated with oversized pipe insulation, up to and including hand wheel, and fabricated in two halves when welded in-line. Insulation shall be neatly applied and wired or banded firmly in place. Overlap adjoining pipe insulation by not less than two times the thickness of pipe insulation, or one pipe diameter, whichever is greater.

### Insulation of Valves Using Preformed Fitting Insulation
Insulate valves using preformed fitting insulation or sectional pipe insulation of same material, density, and thickness as used for adjacent pipe. Overlap adjoining pipe insulation by not less than two times the thickness of pipe insulation, or one pipe diameter, whichever is greater.

### Thermal Liquid Leaks Detection
In points with greater risk of thermal liquid leaks (flanged joints, valves, accessories, etc.), the thermal insulation will have to permit the detection of leaks. The impregnation of the insulation with the thermal liquid, supposes a high fire risk that must be taken into account when performing the insulation.

### Insulation of Pipe Elbows
Insulate pipe elbows using preformed mitered fitting insulation made from same material and density as adjacent pipe insulation. Each piece shall be butted tightly against adjoining piece and bonded with adhesive. When preformed fittings are not available, install mitered sections of pipe insulation, to a thickness equal to adjoining pipe insulation. Secure insulation with wire or bands.

### Insulation of Tee Fittings
Insulate tee fittings with preformed insulation or sectional pipe insulation of same material and thickness as used for adjacent pipe. Cut sectional pipe insulation to fit. Butt each section closely to the next and hold in place with tie wire. Bond pieces with adhesive.

### Stainless Steel Wire/Bands for Insulation Securing
Stainless steel wire and/or bands will be used to secure the insulation to the piping, with spacing from 9” to 12”.

### No Borders or Edges of Insulation
No borders or edges of the insulation may remain free. The system of protection against atmospheric agents will be continuous and must be overlapped.

### Instrumentation Equipment Holes
The holes for the instrumentation equipment will be covered provisionally with an easily removable sheet, for the subsequent placement of the instruments.

### Breathing Mastic Protective Covering
Breathing mastic protective covering shall be utilized where aluminum cannot conveniently be used due to the configuration of the fittings and fixtures. If contractor plans to use mastic in his installation, this shall be stated in their proposal.

### 100% Insulation Coverage
Tanks, vessels, equipment, and piping covered within this Specification shall be 100% insulated including, but not limited to, all integral stiffeners, flanges, nozzles, expansion joints, disabled lifting lugs, support connections, manways, access ways, instrument connections, drain/vent connections, inspection ports, etc., unless explicitly noted otherwise.

### Personnel Protection (PP) Insulation
Personnel Protection (PP) insulation shall be installed where called for by the insulation drawings and specifications. It shall be provided to a height of 7 feet above grade, platform and walkway and to a horizontal distance of 2 feet from a walkway or platform. The surface skin temperature will be limited to 140°F.

### Insulation Close to Equipment
Apply insulation close to equipment by grooving, scoring, and beveling insulation.

### Least Visible Locations for Insulation Covers
On exposed equipment, locate insulation and cover seams in least visible locations.

### MX-251 Main Heat Exchangers Insulation Supports
Insulation supports are provided for MX-251 Main Heat Exchangers. These supports shall be put on by Insulation Subcontractor. Details can be found in the attachments to this specification.

### Insulation Application Guidelines
- **Stiffeners**: Insulation over 1-1/2” x 13 ga. Un-flattened expanded, stainless steel metal secured on #10 ga. stainless steel weld pins with 2-1/2” sq. stainless steel speed clips.
- **Surfaces**: Impaled on #10 ga. stainless steel weld pins on 12” centers, secured with 2-1/2” sq. stainless steel speed clips. Excess pin length bent over to at least 1/4” below the surface of the insulation.
- **Fasteners**: Same material as pipe, vessel, or equipment they attach to.

### ASME Coded Items
- **Field Welding**: Not allowed on coded items (e.g., Steam Generation equipment).
- **Cylindrical Surfaces**: Secured with 3/4” x .020” stainless steel strapping bands at 12” centers.
- **Vertical Vessels and Piping**: May require circumferential, “clamp-on”, insulation support rings.
- **Vessel Heads**: Radial bands applied in a uniform pattern with spacing of approximately 12” around the shell circumference. Floating ring made of 1/4”dia. steel rod placed at the center of the vessel head.

### Additional Guidelines
- **Girth Bands**: Two (2) closely spaced bands placed approximately 3” beyond the head tangent line over the insulation to form a girth around the periphery of the vessel.
- **Radial Bands**: Fastened between the floating ring and the girth bands. All bands spring type with possible exception of girth bands which must be tightly secured.
- **Code Nameplates**: Not covered with insulation.
- **Arc-Welding**: Contractor shall not arc-weld any device to ASME coded pressure vessels or ducting, or to stainless steel vessels. Insulation clips as specified and installed in accordance with acceptable standards.

### General Application Guidelines
- **Tanks/Vessels**: Mineral fiber insulation on sidewalls and bottoms of equipment, tanks, and vessels requiring insulation (specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0003).
- **Aluminum Jacketing**: Insulating material protected from spillages, mechanical wear, and other possible damage with Aluminum jacket (specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0004). Minimum overlaps of 3” for horizontal and circumferential seams along with associated piping isometrics. Slit reinforcement with backup piece of plain aluminum and stainless steel sheet metal screws, minimum flashing overlap 3”.

### Section 7.8 Insulation Flashing and Sealing
- All openings and/or gaps, at protrusions of items through the jacket must be flashed and sealed so they are weather proof and will shed water.
- Jacketing shall be applied to the insulated work when it is in the cold condition (at ambient temperature).
- Stainless steel screws with neoprene washers shall be used on longitudinal joints and for securing flashing sheets to provide watertight installations. Spacing of screws shall not exceed 9”.
- Aluminum jacket sheets on piping shall be held in place with stainless steel strapping bands. Install one (1) stainless steel breather/expansion spring for each 25 feet of banding. Banding shall be provided as indicated in attached specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0006 – Installation of Insulation and Jacketing on Piping.
- Application of aluminum jacketing shall allow for the thermal expansion of the unit to which it is applied. Care shall be given to the use of screw fasteners, their location and number.
- Where vapor barriers are indicated, apply vapor-barrier mastic on seams and joints and at ends adjacent to duct/pipe flanges and fittings.
- Draw jacket tight and smooth.
- Cover circumferential joints with 3” wide strips, of same material as insulation jacket. Secure strips with an adhesive along with outward clinching staples along both edges of strip, spaced 4” o.c.
- Overlap jacket longitudinal seams at least 1-1/2”. Install insulation with longitudinal seams at the bottom of duct/pipe. Clean and dry surface to receive self-sealing lap. Staple laps with outward clinching staples along edge at 2” o.c.

### Section 7.8.12 Jacket Lap Joints
All jacket lap joints shall be arranged and positioned to shed water. All longitudinal joints shall be placed on the off weather side of the duct/pipe whenever possible.

### Section 7.8.13 Horizontal Lines
On horizontal lines, longitudinal joints shall be located on the lower half of the duct/pipe and set above the low point to insure runoff of water.

### Section 7.8.14 Silicone Sealant
Silicone sealant shall be applied in a 1/4" bead extending over all possible sources of moisture penetration, such as the intersections of insulation at piers, nozzles, building walls, valve bonnets, supports, hangers, and other parts protruding through the surface of the finish. Silicone sealant shall be applied without feathering the edges.

### Section 7.9 Insulating Cement
- Because of a high "k" factor, the use of insulating cement must be limited. It may be applied only to small complex surfaces where blanket application is impractical. Apply in 1-1/2" layers, reinforced with stainless steel wire mesh, final thickness to match adjacent blanket.
- Cement may also be used to fill in any chinks, cracks, voids or depressed areas in insulation, prior to application of protective coating or jacket.
- Finishing cement, shall be troweled into the stainless steel wire mesh face of the insulation to a uniform thickness, and allowed to dry thoroughly before applying breathing mastic.

## Protection of Insulation Work
At the close of work each day all applied insulation must be either jacketed or covered with plastic sheeting that is banded in place. Under no circumstances should insulation be left uncovered over night.
