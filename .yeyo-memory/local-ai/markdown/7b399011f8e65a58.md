# 4521-PPI-LOI-039-062-0001-GAR Rev00 Inspection Point Program

## Resumen

- The document is an Inspection Points Program for a HEX replacement TES Solana project.
- It includes details on mechanical calculations, drawings, welding procedures, and material preparation.
- The program covers various inspection points such as dimensional control, bevel inspection, and volumetric inspections.
- Approval signatures are provided by Mikel Aguirre, Iñaki Ugarte, and David Veiga.
- Volumetric inspection of process nozzles to head attachment.
- Non-pressure parts and removable welds dimensional checks.
- Welding parameters checking for bundle supports and guides.
- Surface inspection of pressure-boundary attachment welds.
- Visual inspection of impingement plates.
- Final assembly inspections including bundle insertion, girth seam welding, and helium leak tests.
- Post-weld heat treatment with general inspection before PWHT and chart review.
- Inspections after PWHT for hardness survey and surface inspection.

## Observaciones

- The text is in Spanish and contains some abbreviations that may not be universally understood.
