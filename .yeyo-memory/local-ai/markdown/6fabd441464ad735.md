# 4521-HDD-AEE-39-70-0011-GAR Rev.01 Pressure transmitters datasheets

## Resumen

- This data sheet pertains to pressure transmitters for the Solana project.
- It includes specifications, deviations from technical conditions, and attached documents.
- The documentation must be in the format established by Abengoa.
- Quality assurance measures include CE compliance certificates, calibration certificates, and manufacturing dossiers.
- Inspections are scheduled 2 weeks after delivery and include hydrostatic pressure tests and no-destructive welding tests.
- The document contains datasheets for pressure transmitters.
- Transmitters are designed for high temperature and full vacuum service.
- They use a 316 SS capillary tube with a length of 10 ft.
- Electrical connections are made via 1/2" NPT, classified as Class 1, Division 2, Group D.
- The transmitters measure absolute pressure with an accuracy of 0.1% of the adjustable range.
- They operate on a power supply of 24 Vdc and output a signal of 4-20 mA (2 wires) + HART.
- Transmitters are used for monitoring molten salt pressures between A and B shells of TES HEX.

## Observaciones

- Some data points are missing or marked as N/A, which may affect the accuracy of the transmitter specifications.
- Text is in Spanish, which may cause issues with OCR.
- Some terms are abbreviated or unclear (e.g., 'FV', 'RF').
- OCR issues with some numbers and symbols.
- Text is not consistently formatted, making it difficult to read.
- Document number and sheet information are not specified.
