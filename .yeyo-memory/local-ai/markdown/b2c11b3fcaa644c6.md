# BEARING TEMPERATURE TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the measurement of the anti-friction bearing temperature of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**:
- Tests on manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid.
- Pumps tested at rated point under stable conditions.
- Deviations agreed during contract signing, fixed in written form.
- Terms, definitions, calculations per DIN, HI-Standard, ISO.

**Test Procedure**:
1. Reading of measurements by the responsible tester.
2. Every 20 minutes: temperature (electric/infrared).
3. Records and reports: handwritten or computer printed, filed for 10 years.
4. Instrumentation: calibrated equipment per Rheinhuette quality procedures.
5. Test tolerances: max. 82°C after 1 hour operation.

# RUNNING TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the running test of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**:
- Tests on manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid.
- Pumps tested at rated point under stable conditions.
- Deviations agreed during contract signing, fixed in written form.
- Terms, definitions, calculations per DIN, HI-Standard, ISO.

**Test Procedure**:
1. Reading of measurements by the responsible tester.
2. Minimum every 20 minutes: capacity (magnetic flowmeter), suction head (pressure transducers), discharge head (pressure transducers), speed (electronic), power (torque bar/calibrated test motor).
3. Records and reports: handwritten or computer printed, filed for 10 years.
4. Instrumentation: calibrated equipment per Rheinhuette quality procedures.
5. Test tolerances: stable characteristics during running test regarding flow, head, power consumption, vibration values.

# IMPELLER BALANCING PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: Balancing of impellers according to DIN ISO 1940 T1 on a rotational-balancing-machine.

**Balancing Machine**:
- Manufacturer: Schenk Ro Tec GmbH
- Type: ROTEC HM 30U
- Year of manufacture: 2001
- Calibration: Annually by manufacturer

**Sequence**:
1. Finalized impeller brought to the balancing machine.
2. Mounting auxiliary shaft in its seat for impeller mounting.
3. Adjusting and activating the balancing machine, obtaining imbalance points.
4. Eliminating imbalance through milling, grinding, drilling, or welding material at rotor.
5. Obtaining remaining imbalance and checking limits per DIN ISO 1940, Grade 2.5.
6. Signing working papers and releasing impeller.

**Example Impellers**: 1-levels-balancing (single-plane) if D/B ≥ 6; 2-levels-balancing (double-plane) if D/B < 6.

  - Must be carried out by the manufacturer of the penetrant system
  - Certificates (acc. EN 10204-3.1) must be available prior to testing
  - For routine assessment, reference blocks acc. to ISO 3452-3 are used by the level II examiner

## Time and Extent of Testing
- **Time of Testing**: Defined based on component design, fabricating steps, standards, or customer requirements
- **Extent of Testing**: Defined by design, standards, or customer requirements

## Implementation
### Surface Preparation and Pre-Cleaning
- Test parts must be cleaned from grease, oil, or other contaminations with a solvent-based cleaner
- All surfaces must be dry when the penetrant is applied

### Applying of the Penetrant
- Applied by spraying or brushing
- Must be liquid on all areas during dwell time
- Drying of the penetrant is not allowed

### Dwell Time of Penetrant
- Ranges from 10 to 30 minutes, depending on surface condition and material
- Parts must be turned to avoid pooling

### Surface Temperature
- Environmental and surface temperature range between 10 and 50 °C

### Removing Excess Penetrant
- Clean with a jet of water (not sharp, max. 3 bar, temp. 10-38°C), distance min. 30 cm
- Do not wash off the work piece with water

### Drying
- Perform immediately after cleaning with a clean lint-free cloth at room temperature

### Developing
- Apply developer by spraying a thin and smooth film on the workpiece
- Metallic bright spots must still be visible
- Spray can must be shaken to mix fluid
- If layer thickness is too big, all parts must be cleaned and tests redone

### Developer Dwell Time
- Starts immediately after drying, varies from 10 min to 30 min
- Standards or other specifications may force special dwell times (e.g. > 30 min)

## Inspection, Eye Examination
- Developer forms a reasonably uniform thin white coating
- Surface discontinuities indicated by bleed-out of penetrant (deep red color)
- Indications with light pink color may indicate excessive cleaning
- Inadequate cleaning can leave excessive background making interpretation difficult
- Minimum light intensity of 1000 lx required on the surface to ensure adequate sensitivity

## Evaluation of Indications
- Evaluated during developer dwell time (Sec. 8.8) with conditions of Sec. 8.3
- If unacceptable, level II examiner decides if part must be rejected or repaired
- Retest must be done in any case

## Documentation, Test Report
- Test result must be documented on the papers of the part and/or with a separate test report acc. EN 10204-3.1
- Test report should contain:
  - Designation (name, material, dimensions, cast#)
  - Surface preparation/condition
  - Link to this written procedure (No.: 3.00.4642-0613 En, Rev. 3) and applied specifications
  - Penetration System
  - Description of Implementation (Sec. 8)
  - Minimum light intensity, test temperature
  - Indication (type of discontinuities detected, size, location)
  - Test location, date of testing
  - Performance demonstration (Sec. 5.4) if required
  - Name and qualification of testing personnel

## Post-Cleaning
- Clean with a jet of water and clean, lint-free, dry cloth after penetration testing
