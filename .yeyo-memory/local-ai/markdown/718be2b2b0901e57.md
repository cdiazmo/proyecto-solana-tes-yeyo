# PERFORMANCE TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for hydraulic and mechanical performance test of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**: Tests will be carried out on the manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid. Pumps will be tested in accordance with specifications to ISO 9906. Any deviations from this procedure shall be agreed during signing of the contract between the purchaser and manufacturer and shall be fixed as a separate deviation list in written form.

**Test Procedure**: To begin a performance test, the pumps must be fully machined and assembled. The liquid containing parts shall be subject to hydrostatic test prior to assembling. The pump tester must identify the pump by model, size, contract and serial number or other means of identification. The pump will be coupled with the calibrated test motor or torque bar. Rated operating data fed into the test field programme. Starting the test field programme. Reading of measurements by the responsible tester. The following parameters are measured for each operating point: capacity, suction head, discharge head, speed, power, temperature.

**Records and Reports of Test**: Handwritten or computer printed records shall be kept of all information relevant to a test. Such records shall be filed and kept available for a period of 10 years minimum. For performance test a test certificate and a performance curve will be made (see attachment).

# SOUND PRESSURE LEVEL TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the sound pressure level test of centrifugal pumps along with motor and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**: Tests will be carried out on the manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid. The pumps will be tested at rated point with a calibrated test motor under stable conditions.

**Test Procedure**: Reading of measurements by the responsible tester. Measurement will be done in 1 m distance to the pump (see picture 1).

**Records and Reports of Test**: Handwritten or computer printed records shall be kept of all information relevant to a test. Such records shall be filed and kept available for a period of 10 years minimum. For the sound pressure level test a separate test certificate will be provided (see attachment).

**Test Tolerances**: The sound pressure level, measured in dB(A), shall not exceed the guaranteed value at rated point (specified in noise data sheets).

# BEARING TEMPERATURE TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the measurement of the anti-friction bearing temperature of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**:
- Tests on manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid.
- Pumps tested at rated point under stable conditions.
- Deviations agreed during contract signing, fixed in written form.
- Terms, definitions, calculations per DIN, HI-Standard, ISO.

**Test Procedure**:
1. Reading of measurements by the responsible tester.
2. Every 20 minutes: temperature (electric/infrared).
3. Records and reports: handwritten or computer printed, filed for 10 years.
4. Ball bearing temp measurement: test certificate provided.
5. Instrumentation: calibrated according to RHEINHÜTTE Pumpen GmbH quality procedures.
6. Test tolerances: max temperature 82°C after 1 hour operation.

# RUNNING TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the running test of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**:
- Tests on manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid.
- Pumps tested at rated point under stable conditions.
- Deviations agreed during contract signing, fixed in written form.
- Terms, definitions, calculations per DIN, HI-Standard, ISO.

**Test Procedure**:
1. Reading of measurements by the responsible tester.
2. At minimum every 20 minutes: capacity (magnetic flowmeter), suction head (pressure transducers), discharge head (pressure transducers), speed (electronic), power (torque bar/calibrated test motor).
3. Records and reports: handwritten or computer printed, filed for 10 years.
4. Running test: test certificate provided.
5. Instrumentation: calibrated according to RHEINHÜTTE Pumpen GmbH quality procedures.
6. Test tolerances: stable characteristics during running test regarding flow (Q), head (H), power consumption (P), vibration values (rms).

# IMPELLER BALANCING PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: Balancing of impellers according to DIN ISO 1940 T1 on a rotational-balancing-machine.

**Balancing Machine**:
- Manufacturer: Company Schenk Ro Tec GmbH
- Type: ROTEC HM 30U
- Year of manufacture: 2001
- Calibration: Annually by manufacturer company Schenk

**Sequence**:
1. Finalized impeller brought to the balancing machine.
2. Mounting of auxiliary shaft in its seat for impeller mounting.
3. Adjusting and activating the balancing machine, obtaining imbalance points.
4. Elimination of imbalance through milling, grinding, drilling, or welding material at rotor.
5. Obtaining remaining imbalance and checking if within allowable limits (DIN ISO 1940, Grade 2.5).
6. Signing of "working papers" and release of impeller.

**Example Impellers**: 1-levels-balancing for D/B ≥ 6; 2-levels-balancing for D/B < 6.

# PROCEDURE - LIQUID VISIBLE PENETRANT EXAMINATION (PT) according ASME Sec. V, Article 6

## 1 Purpose and scope
This written procedure is valid for liquid visible penetrant examination on components (e.g. sand die castings acc. DIN EN 1371-1, semi-finished products, tubes, round bars (pump shafts), each from steel or stainless steel), for locating of all kind of flaws which are opened to the surface [e.g. cracks, folding, porostity and incomplet fusion].

## 8 Implementation
### 8.1 Surface preparation and pre-cleaning
- Test parts must be cleaned from grease, oil or other contaminations with a solvent-based cleaner.
- All surfaces must be dry when applying the penetrant.

### 8.2 Applying of the penetrant
- Penetrant applied by spraying or brushing.
- Penetrant must be liquid on all areas during dwell time; drying not allowed.

### 8.3 Dwell time of penetrant
- Ranges from 10 to 30 minutes, depending on surface condition and material.
- Parts must be turned to avoid pooling of the penetrant.

### 8.4 Surface temperature
- Environmental and surface temperature range: 10 to 50 °C.

### 8.5 Removing excess penetrant
- Clean with a jet of water (not sharp, max. 3 bar, temp. between 10 and 38°C), distance min. 30 cm.
- Do not wash off the work piece with water.

### 8.6 Drying
- Dry immediately after cleaning with a clean lint-free cloth at room temperature.

### 8.7 Developing
- Apply developer by spraying a thin and smooth film; metallic bright on surface must still be visible.
- Spray can to be shaken during spraying to mix fluid.
- Layer thickness too big requires cleaning and redoing tests.

### 8.8 Developer dwell time
- Starts immediately after drying, varies from 10 min to 30 min; special dwell times possible (e.g. > 30 min).

## 9 Inspection, Eye examination
- Developer forms a reasonably uniform thin white coating.
- Surface discontinuities indicated by bleed-out of penetrant (deep red color).
- Light pink color may indicate excessive cleaning.
- Minimum light intensity of 1000 lx required for adequate sensitivity.

## 10 Documentation, Test Report
- Document test results on part papers or separate report acc. EN 10204-3.1.
- Test report must include: designation, surface preparation/condition, penetration system, implementation description, minimum light intensity, test temperature, indications, test location/date, performance demonstration (if required), testing personnel name and qualification.

## 11 Post-Cleaning
- Clean workpiece with jet of water and clean, lint-free cloth.
