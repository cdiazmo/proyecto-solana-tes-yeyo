# 4521-ESP-AEE-00-70-0501-GAR rev00 General notes & instructions for instruments & valves procurement

## Resumen

- Document pertains to instruments and valves procurement for the Solana project.
- Supplier is responsible for delivering all material to selected Fabricator(s) or project jobsite.
- Quality requirements include adherence to NEC, IEEE, UL standards.
- Supplier must provide a single contact person for tracking material purchases.
- Equipment delivery schedule is subject to final commercial agreement.
- Customer has the right to inspect equipment before, during fabrication, and before shipment.
- Vendor can provide alternative quotes with reduced costs or improved quality.
- Specifications follow accepted engineering practices and safety regulations.
- Equipment must be free of water, dried, and protected during transit and storage.
- All openings must be covered and secure for prolonged exposure.

## 1. Definitions
- **Supplier:** Instrument supply company chosen for the project.
- **Customer:** Abener Teyma.
- **Fabricator/Fabricators:** Any instrument assembly shop subcontracted by Abener Teyma (if applicable).
- **Owner:** Atlantica Yield.

## 2. General
This specification describes the scope of work for supply of all instruments and valves as listed at the attached datasheet for quote needed for the project. The attachments identified in this document are considered part of this specification and Supplier shall be bound to adhere to these specifications. Any specifications attached shall be met and followed by Supplier in full. All drawings and documents included in this package are considered "Contract Documents". The imperative language of this specification is directed to the Supplier, unless specifically noted otherwise. Supplier shall perform all work described unless other parties are specifically identified as performing the work. All directives such as "provide", "install", "perform", "test", etc., are addressed solely to the Supplier, whether or not the word "Supplier" is specifically mentioned. Work included for others will be specifically identified.

English / U.S. units shall be used on all drawings, calculations and any other documentation submitted by Supplier.

## 3. Scope
Scope of this contract is supply of instruments as outlined in the following Instrument Specifications required for the project including all cables, gaskets, manifolds… etc. as identified in the main specification as options or accessories. Shipping schedule to be confirm with purchasing during the final commercial agreement. Packaging, labelling and tagging requirements shall be defined later. Supplier shall be responsible for delivery of all material to selected Fabricator(s) and/or project jobsite. Fabricator(s) will notify Supplier of material required on an as needed basis. Shipments to Fabricator or jobsite should be scheduled to ensure a full load will be delivered in order to reduce transportation costs.

## 4. Quality
All material shall be supplied to meet all dimensional, quality, marking and stamping, and all other requirements for all NEC, IEEE, UL…. etc standards listed in instruments and valves specifications. Any material not meeting these standards will be returned and replaced at Supplier's expense.

**Notice:**
- Supplier commitment to supply all the required vendor data as promised is crucial to the progress of the project. The attached vendor data requirements reflected when client need the documentation. Vendor shall indicate number of weeks after order for date of submission, and include with the proposal. Failure to comply may result in rejection of the bid.
- Supplier quotation must state time required to submit approval drawings and the time required to fabricate after drawing approval is received.
- Equipment is to be delivered to purchaser's manufacturing site with specific locations to be released later, according document 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001, site information.
- Shipment is required by a strict delivery schedule to be finalized at the time the final contract is materialized.
- For the equipment on this quote, a list of recommended spare parts for one year's normal maintenance with price and delivery for each item is required. Also indicate any savings or discounts which may be offered if the spares are purchased for delivery with the equipment order. Customer needs this information with the quotation, in order for the owner to have spares available during start-up and to take advantage of any economies available.
- The equipment and components covered by this quotation, including any field erection that may be involved, will be subject to inspection by customer personnel prior to shipment to assure compliance with specifications.

• Customer reserves the right to perform inspection activities before and during fabrication, and prior to shipment, if desired.
• It shall be solicited recommendations that may improve the price, delivery or performance. The vendor is invited to quote as an alternative, change in physical dimensions, modifications to the design, prefabrication, stock or manufacturer's standard equipment that would reduce the initial or maintenance cost and/or improve overall quality of the premise. Alternate quotations shall be listed as such in the proposal specifying the alternate and price and delivery for that alternate. The purchase will, in general, be made on a basis of adherence to the original design premise, economic value, and best delivery.
• The attached specifications were prepared using accepted engineering practices, approved technical standards (ASME, ASTM, AWS, etc.) as well as meeting EPA Environmental and OSHA safety regulations. It is expected that if, during your review of these specifications, vendor find needed corrections, changes, or alterations that shall bring these to customer attention. Further, if vendor do not clearly understand the specifications, must request clarification. It shall be given priority to pricing standard equipment and components.
• Unless specified to the contrary in the technical specifications, the following preparations for shipment apply:
o After inspection and testing, equipment shall be completely free of water, dried and prepared for shipment. Adequate protection shall be provided against mechanical damage and atmospheric corrosion in transit, and for at least six (6) months outdoor storage.
o All openings shall be covered, securely fastened and suitable for prolonged exposure prior to final installation.
