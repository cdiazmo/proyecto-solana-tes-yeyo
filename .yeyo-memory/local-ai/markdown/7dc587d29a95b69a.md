## Structural Design Basis
### Occupancy Category for Wind and Seismic Importance Factors (ASCE 7-05 Table 1-1)
- **Structures within the solar collector field**: Category II
- **Structures and buildings within the central plant power block**: Category III
- **Firewater storage tank and firewater pump building**: Category IV

### Wind Load: Calculated per ASCE 7-05
- **Basic Wind Speed**: 90 MPH (Figure 6-1)
- **Wind Exposure Category**: C
- **Wind Importance Factor (Table 6-1)**
  - IW = 1.00 for Category II (solar field)
  - IW = 1.15 for Category III (central plant)
  - IW = 1.15 for Category IV (firewater)
- **Wind Directionality Factor (Table 6-4)**
  - KD = 0.85 for buildings and signs
  - KD = 0.95 for round chimneys and tanks
- **Topographic Factor**: KZT = 1.0
- **Gust Effect Factor**: G = 0.85 (for rigid structures)

### Site Class and Coefficients
- **Site Class:** D
- **F<sub>A</sub> = 1.6, F<sub>V</sub> = 2.4**

### Spectral Response Acceleration Coefficients
- **Short Period S<sub>MS</sub> = 0.3681, 1-sec Period SM<sub>1</sub> = 0.2123**
- **Design Spectral Response Acceleration Coefficients: Short Period S<sub>DS</sub> = 0.245, 1-sec Period SD<sub>1</sub> = 0.142**

### Seismic Design Category
- **Governing Seismic Design Category:** C

### Noise Level
Individual equipment shall be designed for 85 dBA SPL @ 3 ft.

### Cooling Tower Water
- **Summer peak design cooling water temperatures: Supply Temperature 82°F, Return Temperature 102.9°F**

### Mechanical & Electrical Equipment Temperature Ratings
- **Outdoor:** Design dry bulb temperature + 1008W/m<sup>2</sup> DNI
- **Indoor Non-cooled Buildings:** Ventilation system to account for ambient design dry bulb temperature + direct sunlight + code calculated heat gains, equipment with temperature ratings not less than 149 degrees F

### Fire Water Available
125 psig

### Materials of Construction
- **Materials of construction shall be Vendor standard unless otherwise stated in the equipment specifications, and suitable for use in an industrial plant environment.**
- **The use of items and materials containing asbestos is not permitted.**

### Proven Design
Vendor shall furnish equipment and/or materials which have been proven by successful operation for at least (5) years.

### Technical Exceptions
All comments and exceptions to the specifications must be clearly stated in the proposal and shall reference the appropriate paragraph.

### Guards for Moving Parts
OSHA guards shall be provided.

### Lifting Lugs
Adequate lifting lugs shall be provided. If the equipment is shipped in sections, provide lugs for each sub-assembly.

### Electrical Power
- **Motors:** 1/2 to 250 hp - 460 Volts, Three Phase, 60 HZ; 250 to 400 hp - 4000 Volts, Three Phase, 60 HZ; 500 to 3000 hp - 4000 Volts, Three Phase, 60 HZ
- **Motors with VSD:** ½ to 450 hp - 460 Volts, Three Phase, 60 HZ; Greater than 450 hp - 4000 Volts, Three Phase, 60 HZ
- **Control/Instrument Power:** 2-Wire loop powered field transmitter: 24 VDC; 4-Wire field transmitters: 115 VAC, 60 Hz; Field contacts for status and alarms: 115 VAC, 60 Hz or 24 VDC; Field contacts for Interlocks: 115 VAC, 60 Hz or 24 VDC; Continuous process variables: 4-20 mA DC
