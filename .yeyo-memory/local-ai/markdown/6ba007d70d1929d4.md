**Specification Form - Solana (Rev. 12/2010)**
- **Application:** This specification is intended for potable water and instrument air.
- **Service Range:** Viega ProPress stainless piping system: 200 PSIG @ 250° F. Max. Victaulic roll-groove piping system: 150 PSIG @ 230° F. Max.

**Piping Materials and Fittings**
- **Pipe:** 304SS pipe, press connect A312 and A554, press connect valves and threaded valves (See note 1)
- **Fittings:** Viega ProPress press connect fittings with Smart Connect feature, Victaulic Style 107H “Quick-Vic”, Style 07 “Zero-Flex”

**Valves**
- **Gate Valve:** TA1 - CL 150 screwed, TA41S - CL 150 RF flanged
- **Ball Valve:** TF6 – CL 600 press connect, TF144L - CL 150 RF flange
- **Globe Valve:** TB41 - CL 150 RF flanged
- **Butterfly Valve:** TH41 - CL 150 flanged (wafer type) or TH30 – groove end
- **Check Valve:** TC6 - CL 600 press connect, TC41 (swing check) CL 150 RF flanged
- **Dble. Door Check Valve:** TC41W - CL 150 wafer

**Notes**
1. Pipe and fittings ½” thru 4” shall be Viega LLC.
2. 316 Viega ProPress is to be NSF listed for potable use.
3. All gaskets for Viega ProPress Stainless System or Victaulic system shall be EPDM.
4. Victaulic grooved joint piping systems shall be installed in accordance with the manufacturer’s guidelines and recommendations.
5. Grooved end shall be clean and free from indentations, projections and roll marks in the area from the pipe to the groove for proper gasket sealing.
6. A Victaulic factory trained field representative shall provide on-site training for contractor’s field personnel in the proper use of grooving tools and installation of grooved piping products. Factory-trained representatives shall periodically review the product installation. Contractor shall remove and replace any improperly installed products.
