# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0008-GAR Rev01 - IV

## Resumen

- The document is a Visual Inspection Procedure for weld seams and base materials.
- It applies to Arizona Solar One's HEX replacement TES Solana project.
- Visual examinations are required before post-weld heat treatment if not specified otherwise.
- Inspection criteria include surface finish, irregularities, cracks, joint geometry, and more.
- Remote visual examination can be used when direct access is not possible.
- A reduction in thickness due to welding process is limited to 0.8mm or 10% of the nominal thickness.
- Weld reinforcement must have a smooth transition with the base material and not exceed specified thicknesses.
- Concavity due to welding on the root side of circumferential butt welds is allowed if it meets certain criteria.
- Alignment tolerance for butt welded sections requires offsets not exceeding values in Tables 3a & 3b.
- Unacceptable indications must be removed or reduced, and repairs may require rewelding by qualified personnel.

### 1.0 OBJECT
The object of the present procedure is to establish the necessary and sufficient conditions for visual examination of weld seams and base materials.

### 2.0 SCOPE
This Procedure will apply to determine surface condition of the part, alignment of mating surfaces, shape, and all welding joints the same to butt welds than fillet welds.

### 3.0 CODES
- ASME Code, Section V – Non Destructive Testing (2015 Edition)
- ASME Code, Section VIII Div1 y Div2 – Pressure Vessels (2015 Edition)
- ASME Section I, Power Boilers (2015 Edition)
- WRITTEN PRACTICE ASME. QCM.PR05
- SNT-TC-1A

### 4.0 RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY
#### 4.1.0 QUALITY MANAGER
He has the authority to determine the items that must be checked using this procedure.

#### 4.2.0 QUALIFIED PERSONNEL
The personnel who will perform the Visual examinations must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR 05. In those cases where the tests are required to meet the EC Pressure Equipment Directive, personnel must be qualified as per ISO 9712/2012 (EN-473) specifies.

### 5.0 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
1. The Inspector performing visual weld examinations shall verify that the item correlates with the paperwork and that all required previous manufacturing operations and inspections have been successfully completed and signed off.
2. If any necessary stamping to identify material or individual performing welding processes is missing, the Inspector will observe such stamping after validation by company records prior to acceptance.
3. The Inspector will assure that the condition of the weld surface and at least 1" on either side of the weld to be examined are free of scale, paint, grease, or accumulation of dirt or excessive irregularities that would mask a deficiency.
4. Visual examination personnel shall be examined annually to assure natural or corrected near distance acuity in at least one eye, such that they are capable of reading Jaeger No. 1 letters at a distance of not less than 12". Personnel shall also demonstrate the capability of distinguishing and differentiating contrast between colors or shades of gray.
5. Visual examinations shall be performed to assure correct weld size, length, and location. Inspection of weld size does not include fit up inspection unless required by implementing procedures and/or process control. Measurements may be achieved by using devices such as rulers, templates, gauges, micrometers, impressions, magnification such as boroscopes or magnifying glass or by comparison with standards such as workmanship samples or photographs, as necessary to evaluate specified physical attributes of welds and weldments prior to verifying the final inspection.
6. Direct visual examinations
Direct visual examinations shall be made when access is sufficient to place the eye within 600 mm of the surface to be examined and at an angle not less than 30 degrees to the surface to be examined. Mirrors may be used to improve the angle of vision and aids such as a magnifying lens may be used to assist examination.

Illumination (Natural or supplemental white light) for the specific part, component, vessel or section thereof being examined is required. The minimum light level shall be 1000 lu x. The light intensity shall be measured/verified with a meter prior to the examination.

7. Remote Visual Examination
In cases where the welds are not accessible for direct visual examination, remote visual examination may be substituted for direct examination. Remote visual examination may use visual aids such as mirror, telescopes, boroscopes, fiber optics, cameras, or other suitable instruments. Such systems shall have a resolution capability at least equivalent to that obtainable by direct visual observation.

## 7.3 Reduction in Thickness Due to Welding Process
A reduction in thickness due to the welding process is acceptable if it does not exceed 0.8mm or 10% of the nominal thickness of the adjoining surface, whichever is less.

## 7.5 Concavity Due to Welding Process
Concavity due to welding process on the root side of a single welded circumferential butt weld is permitted when the resulting thickness of the weld is at least equal to the thickness of the thinner member of the two sections being joined and the contour of the concavity is smooth.

## Unacceptable Indications
Unacceptable indications shall be removed or reduced to an indication of acceptable size. Whenever an indication is removed by chipping or grinding and subsequent repair by welding is not required, the area shall be blended into the surrounding surface so as to avoid notches, crevices, or corners. Where welding is required after removal of indications, the repair shall be rewelded by qualified welders using qualified welding procedures.
