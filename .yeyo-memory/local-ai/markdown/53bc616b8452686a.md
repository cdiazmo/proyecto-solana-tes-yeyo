**Purpose and Scope**
This document evaluates the implications of replacing existing TES System plate heat exchangers with new tube and shell based Lointek heat exchangers. Hydraulic calculations were performed using Pipeflo® for different operating modes.

**System Description**
The plant includes 6 identical molten salts circuits. Simulated equipment: hot and cold salt tanks, HTF-to-Salt Heat Exchanger, pumps, and control valves.

**Hypothesis and Assumptions**
Pipes details based on 3D Model and P&IDs; operating pressures, temperatures, pump heights, and heat exchanger nozzle heights specified.

**Conclusions**
The current pumps control valves are capable of handling Lointek’s heat exchangers for all considered operation modes.
