# Glossary of Terms
Guide to Reading and Understanding Your Laboratory Report
## Abbreviations and Definitions
- **MDL**: Method Detection Limit.
- **RDL**: Reported Detection Limit.
- **Rec. Recovery**: Recovery.
- **RPD**: Relative Percent Difference.
- **SDG**: Sample Delivery Group.
- **Analyte**: Name of the compound or analysis performed.
- **Dilution**: Adjusting sample concentration for accurate reporting.
- **Limits**: Target recovery ranges for successful QC samples.
- **Qualifier**: Additional information about results, with definitions provided in the Glossary and Definitions page.
- **Result**: Analytical final result (corrected for sample characteristics), with MDL or RDL included.
- **Case Narrative (Cn)**: Discussion of sample results and non-conformances.
- **Quality Control Summary (Qc)**: Results of QC analyses to validate reported results.
- **Sample Chain of Custody (Sc)**: Document verifying sample collection, analysis requests, and possession history.
- **Sample Results (Sr)**: Testing results by sample ID and analysis method.
- **Sample Summary (Ss)**: Specific analyses performed for each sample ID, including dates and times.
