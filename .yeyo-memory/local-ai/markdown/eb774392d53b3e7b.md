### Material Specifications
- **Pipe Sizes:** ½, ¾, 1, 1 ½, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24 thru 48
- **Pipe Sch:** 80 (PE) Standard weight (BE)
- **Fittings:** Forged steel 3000# socketweld, ASTM A-105; Carbon steel buttweld fittings, SO and WN flanges and flanged valves
- **Coupling:** Forged Steel 3000# SW
- **Union:** Forged steel 3000# SW STL. to STL. seats
- **Branch Conn.:** See note 1
- **Gasket:** 1/8” thick Garlock Blue Guard No. 3000, ring type or equal, API 601

### Valves
- **Gate Valve:** FA37 - 800# socketweld, FA42 - 150# RF flanged
- **Globe Valve:** FB37 - 800# socketweld, FB43 - 150# RF flanged
- **Triple Offset Valve:** FH45 – CL 300 RF flange
- **Check Valve:** FC36 (inclined piston check), 800# Socketweld; FC41 - 150# RF flanged; Lift Check Valve, Hor. use, FC37 - 800# socketweld

### Notes
1. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in location regardless of size change due to difficulty of realignment of piping after cutting.
2. All pipe & fittings to be internally cleaned per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
3. All pipe shall be installed “clean”. The minimum standard shall be free of mill scale, slag, dirt, construction debris and foreign objects.
4. At a minimum, all shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas ( “TIG” – “Heliarc”) welding process.
