## Calculation Volume Criteria
- Internal volume of fittings calculated with wall thickness, except for fittings without schedule or thickness.
- Fittings with two schedules (inlet and outlet) use the inlet schedule.
- Reducers and Tee fittings consider actual geometry.
- Other fittings treated as tube surface (cylinder).
- No calculation for plastic specifications (PXXX, VXXX)

## Calculation Surface Criteria
- Couplings: 10% added to total surface of fitting.
- Flanges: 100% added to total surface of fitting.
- Reducers and Tee fittings consider actual geometry.
- Other fittings treated as tube surface (cylinder).
- No calculation for plastic specifications (PXXX, VXXX)

## Welding Number
- Two weldings for fittings with inlet and outlet.
- Three weldings for TEE fittings.
- Cuts every 6m for solar field and <=2" diameter, every 11m for >2" diameter.
- Cuts every 6m for other systems and <=8" diameter, every 10m for >8" diameter.
- One welding for CAP fittings unless DRILLED (two weldings).
- Two weldings for OLET fittings.
- One welding for STUB END fittings.
- Two weldings for BEND TUBES, ELBOWS, COUPLINGS, REDUCERS.
- No welding for small pipings (>=2") unless modeled.

## Welding Valves
- Considered in the first tube or nipple of its pipeline.

## Diametral Weld Inches
- All inlets and outlets considered.

## Equivalent Weld Inches
- Not made for plastic specifications (PXXX, VXXX).
