## Stress Calculations
### Average Primary Membrane Stress (SigmaAvg)
- Formula: \( \sigma_{avg} = \frac{f_N + f_S + f_Y}{A_T} \)
- Values:
  - \( f_N = 14.997 \times 11.573 \times 3.481 = 604.2 \) lb
  - \( f_S = 14.997 \times 43.388 \times (5.341 + 0.843)/2 = 2011.8 \) lb
  - \( f_Y = 14.997 \times 43.388 \times 11.157/2 = 3630.0 \) lb
- Result: \( \sigma_{avg} = \frac{604.2 + 2011.8 + 3630.0}{4.782} = 1306.2 \) psi

### General Primary Membrane Stress (SigmaCirc)
- Formula: \( \sigma_{circ} = \frac{P \times R_{xs}}{2 \times t_{eff}} \)
- Values:
  - \( P = 14.997 \) psig
  - \( R_{xs} = 43.388 \) in
  - \( t_{eff} = 0.662 \) in
- Result: \( \sigma_{circ} = \frac{14.997 \times 43.388}{2 \times 0.662} = 491.2 \) psi

### Maximum Local Primary Membrane Stress (PL)
- Formula: \( PL = max(2 \sigma_{avg} - \sigma_{circ}, \sigma_{circ}) \)
- Result: \( PL = max(2 \times 1306.2 - 491.2, 491.2) = 2121.2 \) psi

## Weld Calculations
### Discontinuity Force Factor (ky)
- Formula: \( k_y = \frac{R_{nc} + t_n}{R_{nc}} \)
- Values:
  - \( R_{nc} = 11.157 \) in
  - \( t_n = 0.843 \) in
- Result: \( k_y = \frac{11.157 + 0.843}{11.157} = 1.076 \)

### Weld Length of Nozzle to Shell Weld (Ltau)
- Formula: \( L_{\tau} = \pi/2 \times (R_n + t_n) \)
- Values:
  - \( R_n = 11.157 \) in
  - \( t_n = 0.843 \) in
- Result: \( L_{\tau} = \pi/2 \times (11.157 + 0.843) = 18.850 \) in

### Weld Load Value (fwelds)
- Formula: \( f_{welds} = min(f_y \times k_y, 1.5 \times S_n(A_2 + A_3), \pi/4 \times P \times R_n^2 \times k_y^2) \)
- Values:
  - \( f_y = 78022 \) psi
  - \( k_y = 1.076 \)
  - \( S_n(A_2 + A_3) = 14788.8 \times (2.935 + 0.000) = 41982.4 \) lb
- Result: \( f_{welds} = min(78022 \times 1.076, 1.5 \times 41982.4, \pi/4 \times 322.3 \times 11.16^2 \times 1.076^2) = 36456.828 \) lb

### Weld Stress Value (tau)
- Formula: \( \tau = \frac{f_{welds}}{L_{\tau}(0.49L_{41T} + 0.6t_w1 + 0.49L_{43T})} \)
- Values:
  - \( L_{\tau} = 18.850 \) in
  - \( L_{41T} = 0.278 \) in
  - \( t_w1 = 0.662 \) in
  - \( L_{43T} = 0.000 \) in
- Result: \( \tau = \frac{36456.828}{18.850 (0.49 \times 0.278 + 0.6 \times 0.662 + 0.49 \times 0.000)} = 3622.918 < 37195.203 \) Weld Size is OK
