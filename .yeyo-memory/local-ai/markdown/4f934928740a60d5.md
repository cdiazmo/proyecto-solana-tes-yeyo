## EC-94 – Solana Specific Standard Form (Rev. 03/2010)
### Application
This specification is intended for cold 93% sulfuric acid, 98% sulfuric acid and oleum piping (including vents and drains). For stack drain use, see Note 2.
#### Service Range
- 93% and 98% H 2SO4 = 80 PSIG, 120 F.
- Oleum = 150 PSIG, 200 F.
### Construction
- 316 SS pipe and SW fittings
- Alloy-20 valves
- 316 Stainless steel buttweld pipe and fittings, alloy -20 flanged valves
#### Nominal Pipe Size
| Size | Sch | Material |
|------|-----|----------|
| 1/2  | Sch 80 (plain end) | 316SS seamless in accordance with ASTM A-312, Grade TP316L |
| 3/4  | Sch 40S (bevel end) | CL 3000 SW Type 316 SS ASTM A-182, Grade F316 |
| 1    | Buttweld, Sch. 40S, Type 316 SS, ASTM A-403, Grade WP316L |
| ...  | ...   | ...      |
### Fittings
- CL 3000 SW Type 316 SS ASTM A-182, Grade F316
- Buttweld, Sch. 40S, Type 316 SS, ASTM A-403, Grade WP316L
- Coupling CL 3000 SW Type 316 SS ASTM A-182, Grade F316
- Union CL 3000 SW Type 316 SS ASTM A-182, Grade F316 with 304 SS
### Nuts and Bolts
- CS ASTM A-307 Grade “B” hex head bolts with heavy hex nuts
### Gaskets
- 1/8” thick Garlock Gylon fawn, ring type.
### Valves
- Gate Valve LA42 - CL 150 RF ANSI flanged
- Plug Valve KE40T - CL 150 RF ANSI flanged
- Globe Valve LB42 - CL 150 RF ANSI flanged
- Butterfly Valve KH40 - (insert) CL 150 wafer CD4M
- Check Valve QC45 - CL 150 ANSI flanged
- Needle Valve QN1 scrd.
- Sample Valve QN2 - CL 300 Male NPT
### Notes
1. All screwed connections at specialty items to be made up with TFE thread seal tape (by field).
2. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in locations regardless of size change due to difficulty of realignment of piping after cutting.
3. KE40T (4” - 12”) valves are not to be used on tapped connections due to possibility of valve flange having tapped holes at bonnet area.
4. Pipe spool may be required to prevent interferences between disc and other specialty items upon operation of valve.
5. At a minimum, all shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas ( “TIG” – “Heliarc”) welding process.
