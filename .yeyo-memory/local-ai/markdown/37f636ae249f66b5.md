# 4521-ESP-ANC-68-61-CS78

## Resumen

- This document outlines the material and installation standards for a Class 1500 Carbon Steel piping system in a Solana project. It includes specifications for fittings, valves, and notes on construction and maintenance requirements.

# Material and Installation Standards
## Class 1500 Carbon Steel - Piping System
### Application
- Steam, condensate, boiler blowdown, feedwater discharge, chemical feed to boiler.
### Service Range
- Maximum 775°F (See Note 5)
### Construction
- Carbon steel pipe, socketweld FS fittings and valves, weldneck flanges
- Carbon steel pipe, CS buttweld fittings, WN flanges and flanged valves
### Nominal Pipe Size and Schedule
| Size | Schedule | End Type |
|------|----------|----------|
| ½    | 80       | Plain end |
| ¾    | 160      | Beveled end |
| 1    | 120      | Plain end |
| ...  | ...      | ...      |
### Fittings
- Forged steel 6000 SW ASTM A-105, ANSI B16.11
- Carbon steel buttweld ASTM A-234 Grade WPB, Schedule to match pipe ANSI B16.9
- Coupling Forged steel 6000 SW
- Union Forged steel 6000 SW steel to steel seats
### Valves
- Gate valve FA120 (FA84) 1500 SW FA86-W BW 1500, etc.
- Globe valve FB120 1500 SW FB86-W BW 1500, etc.
- Triple Offset FH87 – CL 1500 buttweld
- Check valve FC122 (inclined piston check) 1500 SW, etc.
- Lift check valve FC120 - 1500 socketweld
- Silent check valve FC85 1500 wafer
### Notes
1. FC85 is to be used only in the boiler feedwater line at the inlet to the waste heat boiler as specified on the flow diagrams and at no other location.
2. When flanged gate valves are required for 2” and under use FA84.
3. All piping shall comply with ANSI B31.3 Basic Code (Fabrication & Erection, Section 327; Inspection & Examination, Section 340 & 341) unless otherwise specified in the shop fabrication specification.
4. Heat treatment of 10” thru 24” piping required per ANSI/ASME B31.3, Section 331.1.
5. Upon prolonged exposure to temperature above 775°F., the carbide phase of carbon steel may be converted to graphite (ASME B31.1, 1989, Table A-1, Note 2).
6. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in locations regardless of size change due to difficulty of realignment of piping after cutting.
7. All pipe & fittings to be internally cleaned per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
8. All pipe shall be installed “clean”. The minimum standard shall be free of mill scale, slag, dirt, construction debris and foreign objects.
9. At a minimum, all shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG – Heliarc) welding process.
