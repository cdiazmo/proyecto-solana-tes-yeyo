# 1950201-PIV-001

## Resumen

- The document is a Visual Examination Procedure for weld seams and base materials.
- It is owned by Abengoa and cannot be reproduced or distributed without permission.
- The procedure applies to surfaces specified in another document (1950201-PPI-001).
- Codes from ASME standards are referenced, including Section V, VIII Div1/Div2, and Section I.
- Qualified personnel must follow specific written practices for visual examinations.
- Visual examinations are mandatory before post-weld heat treatment if no specific customer requirements exist.
- Inspectors must ensure that weld surfaces are free from scale, paint, grease, or dirt.
- Remote visual examination can be used when direct access is not possible.
- Acceptance criteria include dimensions and surface conditions meeting welding procedure specifications.
- Unacceptable indications must be removed or reduced to acceptable sizes.
- El procedimiento de inspección visual ASME incluye requisitos generales, criterios de aceptación y zonas sujetas a inspección.
- Se requiere que el inspector verifique la documentación y las operaciones previas antes de realizar la inspección.

## 5.0 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

1. The Inspector performing visual weld examinations shall verify that the item correlates with the paperwork and that all required previous manufacturing operations and inspections have been successfully completed and signed off.
2. If any necessary stamping to identify material or individual performing welding processes is missing, the Inspector will observe such stamping after validation by company records prior to acceptance.
3. The Inspector will assure that the condition of the weld surface and at least 1" on either side of the weld to be examined are free of scale, paint, grease, or accumulation of dirt or excessive irregularities that would mask a deficiency.
4. Visual examination personnel shall be examined annually to assure natural or corrected near distance acuity in at least one eye, such that they are capable of reading Jaeger No. 1 letters at a distance of not less than 12". Personnel shall also demonstrate the capability of distinguishing and differentiating contrast between colors or shades of gray.
5. Visual examinations shall be performed to assure correct weld size, length, and location. Measurements may be achieved by using devices such as rulers, templates, gauges, micrometers, impressions, magnification such as boroscopes or magnifying glass or by comparison with standards such as workmanship samples or photographs, as necessary to evaluate specified physical attributes of welds and weldments prior to verifying the final inspection.
6. Direct visual examinations

Direct visual examinations shall be made when access is sufficient to place the eye within 600 mm of the surface to be examined and at an angle not less than 30 degrees to the surface to be examined. Mirrors may be used to improve the angle of vision and aids such as a magnifying lens may be used to assist examination.

Illumination (Natural or supplemental white light) for the specific part, component, vessel or section thereof being examined is required. The minimum light level shall be 1000 lu x. The light intensity shall be measured/verified with a meter prior to the examination.

Light meters shall be calibrated at least once a year or whenever the meter has been repaired. If meters have not been in use for one year or more, calibration shall be done before being used.

7. Remote Visual Examination

In cases where the welds are not accessible for direct visual examination, remote visual examination may be substituted for direct examination. Remote visual examination may use visual aids such as mirror, telescopes, boroscopes, fiber optics, cameras, or other suitable instruments. Such systems shall have a resolution capability at least equivalent to that obtainable by direct visual observation.

## Thickness Reduction
La reducción de espesor del material de las superficies adyacentes no será inferior al mínimo de espesor requerido en cualquier punto. La reducción de espesor durante el proceso de soldado es aceptable si la reducción no es mayor de 0,8mm o del 10% del espesor nominal del material base afectado.

## Welding Reinforcement
El refuerzo de las soldaduras debe tener una transición suave con el material base adyacente. El espesor del refuerzo no deberá exceder de los valores indicados en las tablas 2a y 2b (ASME VIII Div.1 & Div.2).

## Root Concavity Tolerances
Las imperfecciones de concavidad de raíz en soldaduras realizadas solo desde un lado serán aceptables siempre que se cumplan las dos condiciones siguientes:
- Que presenten una fusión completa con los bordes de soldadura adyacentes.
- Que el espesor nominal de la soldadura (incluyendo el refuerzo de la misma) no sea inferior al espesor nominal del elemento.
