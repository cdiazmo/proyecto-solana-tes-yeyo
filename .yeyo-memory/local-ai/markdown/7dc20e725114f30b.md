## Notes
1. Synthetic heat transfer fluid (HTF) Therminol VP-1 is eutentic mixture of 73.5% diphenyl oxide (DPO) and 26.5% Diphenyl (DP), at ambient temperature.
   - Therminol has very low vapor pressure, however at elevated temperature vapor pressure is much higher (Vapor pressure = 130 psig @ 550 deg. F).
   - Leaked HTF coming from pressurized system at elevated temperature will vaporize and ignite assuming the conditions are right with plentiful of oxygen.
   - In molten salt applications, salt tanks are under a constant nitrogen blanket protection (99% purity - concentration to be confirmed) which will prevent HTF from being ignited; however, leaked HTF at elevated temperature may create the following gases as a result of the thermal decompositon at low pressure environment:
     - 100 % HTF (wt. = weight) ≈ 41 % DPO (wt.) + 41 % Benzene (wt.) + 15 % DO (wt.) + 3 % Toluene (wt.)
2. VOC sensors are expected to be covered with a trace of molten salt over time, even sensors are physically away from salt liquid surface. Long sampling tubes (non in-line measurement) are not recommended due to trace of salt will solidify and completely block off the sample flow stream.
3. Due to similarities of decomposed gases (mentioned in Note 1 above) at the molecular level, measuring the total gas detected , or VOC, is a preferred method.
4. It's not practical to have the perfect match of HTF decomposed gases for calibration purposes, the method of calibrating VOC analyzer with 100 ppm test gas a reference, compare the sensitivities between sample gas and decomposed gases. Vendor to provide these conversions.
5. Vendor to consider using Isobutylene - 100 ppm, combine with overall correction factors (note 4) and provide instrument range in mg /m3 engineering unit
