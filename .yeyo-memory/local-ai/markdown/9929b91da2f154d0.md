## Project Summary
1.1 The Project is a concentrated solar power electric generating facility to be constructed on an approximately 1835-acre parcel of an existing 3000 acre tract in Maricopa County, Arizona, approximately 80 miles southwest of Phoenix, and 20 miles west of the town of Gila Bend near Interstate 8 and Painted Rock Dam Road.
The Project is a 280 MW (gross) parabolic trough solar power plant with a net nominal electrical output of 250 MW, and all necessary supporting infrastructure, which make a fully operational facility.
The facility design includes a thermal energy storage system will provide up to six hours of solar dispatchability. The solar field is oversized by about 70% relative to the actual calculated solar thermal requirements to ensure the plant will achieve summer on-peak capacity factors of approximately 100% from solar energy alone.
The Project design will incorporate parabolic trough technology to capture solar energy in a heat transfer fluid (HTF). Steam will be generated in heat exchangers, i.e., solar steam generators (SSGs). The steam produced in the SSGs will then be sent to two steam turbines which will produce electrical power which will be sold to Arizona Public Service (APS). When the solar field collects more energy than is needed to satisfy the requirements of the Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with the utility, the design of the HTF system shall incorporate the means for the excess solar energy collected by the HTF system to be directed to the Thermal Energy System (TES), where it will transfer its energy to molten nitrate salt during daytime hours with cloud cover or during night hours.

## Vendor Responsibilities
4.3.1 Shipping all fabricated spool pieces and loose pieces to the job site, taking precautions to avoid springing and misalignment of fabrications and damage to shop finish.
4.3.2 Identifying and locating Field Welds (FW) to accommodate spool shipping limitations and Field Fit Welds (FFW) that include extra pipe length to accommodate dimensional differences that typically occur in fabrication and actual equipment locations.
4.3.3 Providing properly beveled ends for all Field Weld (FW) joints.
4.3.4 Clearly marking or metal tagging each fabricated pipe spool or loose piece shipped to the job site with a unique piece number to facilitate the installation process. The project number, drawing number and line size shall be part of the piece number.
4.3.5 Providing the engineering office with defined number of fabricated pipe spool drawing prints. Field Weld (FW) and Field Fit Weld (FFW) locations are to be included on the spool drawings. The vendor shall mark all Field Weld locations and spool tag numbers on Abener Teyma isometrics and submit (1) copy to the Abener Teyma office in Chesterfield, MO, (1) copy to the Abener Teyma Office at the jobsite, and (1) copy to accompany each shipment/truck load to the jobsite.
4.3.6 Removing all loose scale, rust, grease and other foreign matter from piping material before welding. After welding, all weld splatters, icicles and other foreign matter shall be completely removed from the inside of the pipe.
4.3.7 Cleaning and painting all exposed CS piping or pipe supports in accordance with Abencs’ painting specification prior to shipping if required. Piping that is insulated does not require painting.
4.3.8 Protection of all flanged ends of pipe spools with a proper cover attached to bolt holes to insure the flange facings will not be damaged during shipment. The machined ends for field welds on pipe spools should be carefully capped with suitable caps securely anchored and taped in place.
