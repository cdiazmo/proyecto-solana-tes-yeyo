# 4521-PLN-AEE-75-52-0001-GAR Rev01 sealed

## Resumen

- El documento contiene detalles técnicos sobre el diseño y construcción de estructuras.
- Se especifican los requisitos para el anclaje de acero con epoxi.
- Hay diferentes tipos de desarrollos de acero según la fuerza del concreto y las condiciones sísmicas.
- Las normas de desarrollo de acero incluyen información sobre la longitud mínima de anclaje y los tipos de encajes permitidos.
- Bundled bars of more than two bars in the same plane are not allowed.
- Splice lengths vary based on bar diameter and concrete type.
- Seismic splices must be provided outside frame columns and shearwall boundary elements.
- Deformed reinforcing bars and threaded rods should follow manufacturer recommendations for installation.
- Drilled holes must be cleaned properly to avoid cutting existing reinforcement.
- Contract allows alternate billing cycles.
- Footings must not be undermined by pipe trenches.
- Reinforcement options include continuous, tooled joints, and plastic sheathing.

## Observaciones

- El texto contiene errores tipográficos y símbolos no reconocidos.
- La información sobre los tipos de desarrollos de acero es extensa y puede requerir una revisión adicional.
- Confidential and proprietary information.
- Backfilling and compaction must follow geotechnical recommendations.

**1. Bundled Bars of More Than 2 Bars (Including Splice Bar) in the Same Plane is Not Permitted. Acceptable Bundled Bar Layouts are:**
- Use bar diameter derived from equivalent total area to determine applicable splice type for bundled bars.
- See schedule below.

**2. Do Not Splice More Than One Bar in a Bundle. See Splice Diagram for Acceptable Bundle Bar Splice Diagram.**

**Additional Bundled Bar Notes:**
- CS: 1" Min.
- 3-BAR BUNDLE: Splice Bar, Non-bundled Bar
- SPLICE LENGTH: 1:6 Maximum
- SHOP OFFSET: Bend
- STAGGERED SPLICE: CS NO
- STAGGERED BAR SPACING: All splices shall be "CLASS B" unless noted otherwise.

**Bar Location:**
- TOP BARS - Horizontal bars with more than 12" of concrete cast below the bars.
- OTHER BARS - All vertical bars and horizontal bars with less than 12" of concrete cast below bars.

**Development Type:**
- TYPICAL: D1 - C ≥ 2db AND S ≥ 4db, D2 - ALL OTHER CONDITIONS, D3 - C < db OR S < 2db
- SEISMIC: Provide "SEISMIC" splice for all seismic splices located outside frame columns and shearwall boundary elements including drags, chords, diaphragm reinforcing and distributed shearwall reinforcing.

**Reinforcing Grade:**
- Reinforcing splice length is for grade and reinforcing. For grade 75 reinforcing multiply the splice lengths in schedule by 1.25.

**Concrete:**
- Reinforcing splice length schedule is for normal weight concrete. For lightweight concrete multiply lengths in schedule by 1.3.

**NOTES:**
- SPLICE LENGTH: 3-BAR BUNDLE, SPLICE LENGTH: 4-BAR BUNDLE, BUNDLE BAR SPLICE DIAGRAM: 2 = INDICATES "REINFORCING DEVELOPMENT NOTE" REFERENCE, CS = CLEAR SPACING, C = COVER, db = BAR DIAMETER
- ALL DEFORMED REINFORCING BARS AND THREADED ROD NOTED AS EPOXY SHALL BE INSTALLED PER MANUFACTURERS RECOMMENDATIONS. SEE SCHEDULE FOR MINIMUM EMBEDMENT LENGTHS.
- ONLY NON-REBAR-CUTTING DRILL BITS SHALL BE USED TO DRILL HOLES IN EXISTING CONCRETE. CARE IS TO BE TAKEN WHEN DRILLING HOLES SO AS NOT TO CUT ANY EXISTING REINFORCING.
- DRILLED HOLES SHALL BE CLEANED OF CONCRETE DUST AND DEBRIS USING EITHER ANY LON BRUSH AND A VACUUM, OR A NYLON BRUSH AND OIL-FREE COMPRESSED AIR. A BLOW-OUT BULB MAY BE USED IF A VACUUM OR COMPRESSED AIR IS NOT AVAILABLE.
