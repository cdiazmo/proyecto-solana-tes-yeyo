## Specification for Lube Oil and Control Oil Piping
### Application
For lube oil and control oil piping downstream of filters.

### Service Range
- **Temperature:** Ambient to 250°F
- **Pressure:** Not exceeding 150 PSIG

### Construction
- **Pipe & Fittings:** 304L SS, ASTM A-312 Grade TP304L, beveled end
- **Fittings:** Schedule 80 buttweld, Schedule 10S buttweld
- **Branch Conn. Use:** Fittings with slip-on flanges or lap joints
- **Flanges:** 304L SS, ASTM A-182, Grade F304L, Class 150 RF, slip-on or lap joint
- **Stub-ends:** Schedule 80 buttweld, Schedule 10S buttweld
- **Buttwelding:** Type 304L SS per ASTM A-403, Grade WP304L, MSS Type A
- **Bolting:** Alloy Stl. Stud bolts ASTM A-193 Grade B7 w/heavy hex nuts ASTM A -194 Grade 2H
- **Gasket:** 0.175” thick 304 SS & flexible graphite spiral wound gasket, Class 150, API 601 (compresses to 1/8 ” thick)

### Notes
1. Welding must be in accordance with ANSI B31.3.
2. Cold bends may be used as an alternative to buttweld fittings for sizes ¾” thru 4”.
3. Stub ends are required only for proper alignment at odd-angle piping configurations.
4. ½” pipe is permitted only between instruments and adjacent valves.
5. Shop and field welds must use inert gas shielded arc welding, no backup rings allowed.
6. All pipe & fittings to be internally cleaned per spec PS -010.
7. All pipe shall be clean with minimum standards of free from mill scale, slag, dirt, construction debris, and foreign objects.
8. Shop and field root pass welds must use Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG) welding process.
