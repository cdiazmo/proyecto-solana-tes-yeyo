## Materials
1.1 This specification sets forth acceptable standards of materials for aluminum jacketing.
2.1 Aluminum Jacketing:
- Conform to ASTM Specification B 209 and be Aluminum alloy Type 3003, 3004, 3105 or 5005 with H-14 Temper Range, or equivalent, with Stucco embossed surface.
- All jacketing, with the exception of corrugated jacketing, shall have a protective backing facing the insulation. This acid-resistant backing is made of kraft paper with 1 mil polyethylene film.
- Jacket thickness shall be as specified below.

### Vertical Cylindrical Vessels
(1) Vessel Side - Corrugated sheets of 0.024" minimum thickness. Corrugations shall be ¼” deep and 2 ½” (64 mm) across except for small vessels, blow down tanks for example, which shall have 1 ¼” (32 mm) corrugations.
(2) Vessel Top - Dished/Conical - Flat sheets of 0.032" (0.8 mm) minimum thickness.
(3) Vessel Top - Flat/Sloped - Flat sheets of 0.032” (0.8 mm) minimum thickness

### Horizontal Cylindrical Vessels
(1) Vessel Side - Flat sheets of 0.024" (0.6 mm) minimum thickness.
(2) Vessel Ends - Flat sheets of 0.032" (0.8 mm) minimum thickness.

### Economizers, Superheaters, and Heat Exchangers
Corrugated sheets of 0.024” (0.6 mm) minimum thickness. Corrugations shall be 1 ¼” (32 mm) across and ¼” (6 mm) deep.

## Ducting
- Flat sheets of 0.024" (0.5 mm) minimum thickness.

## Piping
- Factory fabricated smooth lengths with longitudinal lock joints and end butt straps or strapping bands:
  - Up to 24” (600 mm) diameter: 0.016” (0.4 mm) jacket thickness.
  - Over 24” (600 mm) diameter: 0.020” (0.5 mm) jacket thickness.
- Preformed covers, in noted thicknesses, for fittings wherever possible.

## Manhole Covers
- Flat sheets of 0.032" (0.8 mm) minimum thickness.

## Available Sizes
- Corrugated:
  - Length: Standard up to 12 feet (3.6 m), longer sheets with special order.
  - Width: 33”, 44” (825 mm, 1100 mm).
- Flat:
  - Length: 100 ft. (30.5 m) rolls.
  - Width: 36”, 48” (900 mm, 1200 mm).
