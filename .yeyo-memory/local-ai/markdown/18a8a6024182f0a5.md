# PERFORMANCE TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for hydraulic and mechanical performance test of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**: Tests will be carried out on the manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid. Pumps will be tested in accordance with specifications to ISO 9906. Any deviations from this procedure shall be agreed during signing of the contract between the purchaser and manufacturer and shall be fixed as a separate deviation list in written form.

**Test Procedure**: To begin a performance test, the pumps must be fully machined and assembled. The liquid containing parts shall be subject to hydrostatic test prior to assembling. The pump tester must identify the pump by model, size, contract and serial number or other means of identification. The pump will be coupled with the calibrated test motor or torque bar. Rated operating data fed into the test field programme. Starting the test field programme. Reading of measurements by the responsible tester. Measured parameters include capacity, suction head, discharge head, speed, power, and temperature.

**Records and Reports**: Handwritten or computer printed records shall be kept of all information relevant to a test. Such records shall be filed and kept available for a period of 10 years minimum. For performance test, a test certificate and a performance curve will be made. The measured points will be translated to the guaranteed speed and density following ISO 9906.

**Instrumentation**: All instruments are so selected that they can provide measurements with predicted accuracy. All equipment used are periodically calibrated in accordance with the RHEINHÜTTE Pumpen GmbH quality procedures.

**Test Tolerances**: The test speed must be in a range of 50-120% of the agreed speed. Values of tolerance factors and verifications of guarantees shall be in accordance with Table 1 and 2 depending on pump power input.

# BEARING TEMPERATURE TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the measurement of the anti-friction bearing temperature of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**:
- Tests on manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid.
- Pumps tested at rated point under stable conditions.
- Deviations agreed during contract signing, fixed in written form.
- Terms, definitions, calculations per DIN, HI-Standard, ISO.

**Test Procedure**:
1. Testing: Measurements by tester every 20 minutes (temperature - electric/infrared).
2. Records and Reports: Handwritten/computer printed records kept for 10 years; test certificate for ball bearing temperature measurement.
3. Instrumentation: Instruments calibrated per RHEINHÜTTE Pumpen GmbH quality procedures.
4. Test Tolerances: Max temperature of 82°C, stabilization after approx. 1 hour operation unless otherwise specified in ITP.

# RUNNING TEST PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: To define the internal procedure for the running test of centrifugal pumps and recording of the test results.

**Scope and Test Conditions**:
- Tests on manufacturers test stand using cold water as test liquid.
- Pumps tested at rated point under stable conditions.
- Deviations agreed during contract signing, fixed in written form.
- Terms, definitions, calculations per DIN, HI-Standard, ISO.

**Test Procedure**:
1. Testing: Measurements by tester every 20 minutes (capacity, suction head, discharge head, speed, power).
2. Records and Reports: Handwritten/computer printed records kept for 10 years; test certificate provided for running test.
3. Instrumentation: Instruments calibrated per RHEINHÜTTE Pumpen GmbH quality procedures.
4. Test Tolerances: Stable characteristics during running test regarding flow, head, power consumption, and vibration values.

# IMPELLER BALANCING PROCEDURE

**Purpose**: Balancing of impellers according to DIN ISO 1940 T1 on a rotational-balancing-machine.

**Balancing Machine**:
- Manufacturer: Company Schenk Ro Tec GmbH
- Type: ROTEC HM 30U
- Year of manufacture: 2001
- Calibration: Annually by manufacturer company Schenk

**Sequence**:
1. Finalized impeller brought to balancing machine.
2. Mounting auxiliary shaft in its seat for impeller mounting.
3. Adjusting and activating the balancing machine, obtaining imbalance points.
4. Eliminating imbalance through milling, grinding, drilling, or welding material at rotor.
5. Obtaining remaining imbalance and checking if within allowable limits (DIN ISO 1940, Grade 2.5).
6. Signing of "working papers" and release of impeller.

**Example Impellers**: 1-levels-balancing for D/B ≥ 6; 2-levels-balancing for D/B < 6.

## 8 Implementation
### 8.1 Surface preparation and pre-cleaning
The test parts must be completely cleaned from grease, oil or other contaminations with a solvent based cleaner.
All surfaces of the part must be dry when the penetrant is applied.
### 8.2 Applying of the penetrant
The penetrant is applied by spraying or brushing. During th dwell time the penetrant must be liquid on all areas. A drying oft he penetrant is not allowed.
### 8.3 Dwell time of penetrant
The dwell time ranges from 10 to 30 minutes, depending on the surface condition and the material. As required the parts must be turned, to avoid a pooling of the penetrant.
### 8.4 Surface temperature
The environmental and surface temperature range is between 10 and 50 °C.
### 8.5 Removing excess penetrant
After the dwell time of the penetrant has lapsed, the test piece will be cleaned with a jet of water (not sharp, with max. 3 bar,temperature between 10 and 38°C) with a distance of min. 30 cm. Do not wash off the work piece with water.
### 8.6 Drying
Drying of the workpiece must be performed immediately after cleaning the penetrant from the surface with a clean lint free cloth at room temperature.
### 8.7 Developing
The developer will be applied with spraying a thin and smooth film on the workpiece. The metallic bright on the surface must still be seen after spraying. The spray can has to shaken during spraying to mix the fluid in the Spray Can. Is the layer thickness to big, all the tested parts must be cleaned and all tests has to be redone.
### 8.8 Developer dwell time
The dwell time of the developer starts immediately after drying and varies from 10 min to 30 min. Standards or other specifications can force special dwell times (e.g. > 30 min).
## Inspection, Eye examination
With a color contrast penetrant, the developer forms a reasonably uniform thin white coating.
Surface discontinuities are indicated by bleed-out of the penetrant which is normally a deep red color that stains the developer. Indications with a light pink color may indicate excessive cleaning. Inadequate cleaning may leave an excessive background making cleaning and interpretation difficult. A minimum light intensity of 1000 lx (100 fc) is required on the surface to be examined to ensure adequate sensitivity during the examination and evaluation of indications, see ASME Sec. V, Art. 6, T-676.3 (ISO 3059, 5.3: 500 lx).
## 9 Evaluation of Indications
The evaluation of indications is performed during the developer dwell time (see Sec. 8.8) with the conditions of Section 8.3.
If an indication is unacceptable according the in Sec. 2 listed specifications or customer specifications, the level II examiner decides, if the part must be rejected or if the part can be repaired with machining or grinding. In any case a retest must be done.
## Documentation, Test Report
The test result must be documented on the papers of the part and/or with a separate test report acc. EN 10204-3.1, see Appendix 1.
The test report shall contain the following information (see ASME Sec. V, Art. 6, Table T-621, or customer request):
- Designation (name, material, dimensions, cast#)
- Surface preparation/condition
- Link to this written procedure (No.: 3.00.4642-0613 En, Rev. 3) and applied specifications
- Penetration System
- Description of Implementation (see Sec. 8)
- Minimum light intensity, test temperature
- Indication (type of discontinuities detected, size, location)
- Test location, date of testing
- Performance demonstration, see Sec. 5.4, if required
- Name and qualification of testing personnel
## Post-Cleaning
After penetration testing the workpiece will cleaned with a jet of water and with a clean, lint free and dry cloth.

## Dimension Check and Final Inspection Procedure
### Dimension checking:
After completion of the final assembly and the associated identity test, all pumps undergo a dimension inspection.
All main and connection dimensions are checked using calibrated hand measuring instruments, the latest general arrangement drawing (AZ) and the Technical Pump Data Sheet of the relevant order.
### Visual examination / Final inspection:
Prior to dispatch, the pumps are checked for visual appearance, cleanness, labelling (direction of rotation marking, type plate etc.), conformity of design according to the specifications, as well as for completeness (add-on parts, spare parts, accessories), using the Technical Pump Data Sheet and the picking list of the relevant order.
A further functional check (rotating the top shaft by hand) is performed, to eliminate any possible run-up of the impeller.
All the openings in the pressure chamber (flange) are fitted with weather-resistant protective caps, to protect against damage and fouling.
The pumps are packed in such a way that damage, fouling, and negative atmospheric influences are avoided.
### Certifications:
All tests are documented by the testing personnel. The documents are archived in accordance with ISO 9001:2008
Testing equipment subject to mandatory monitoring, such as the calipers used in the dimension inspection, are calibrated (frequency 1 year) and marked with test tags.
Monitoring performed in accordance with ISO 9001:2008
