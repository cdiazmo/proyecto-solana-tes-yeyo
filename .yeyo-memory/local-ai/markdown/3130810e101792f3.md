# 1950202 Convert EVEN to ODD train

## Resumen

- This document outlines the procedures for converting even-numbered train piping spools into odd-numbered systems, focusing on HTF pipes and molten salts pipes. It includes specific instructions for rotating spools, relocating drainage points, and addressing misplaced nozzles.

## Markdown estructurado

# 1950202 Convert EVEN to ODD train

**Manufactured piping spools for an EVEN TRAIN are valid to be located in an ODD TRAIN if minor works are performed on site.**

- **Molten salts pipes and accessories**: Located in the vertical plane, completely interchangeable despite the train configuration used.
- **HTF pipes and accessories**: Need on-site relocation to convert manufactured even train spools into an odd number system.

**In both cases, HEX-A is the cold unit while HEX-F is the hot unit.**

## Page 2

Manufactured HTF pipes (B-to-C and D-to-E) are formed using three spools:

- **SPOOL #1**: Can be rotated to be welded to HEX-C/E.
- **Drainage issue**: When rotating SPOOL #1, drainage will not be in the lower point. Two options to fix this:
  1. Plug existing nozzle and open a new drainage point in the lower point.
  2. Sub-divide (cutting) the spool into an elbow+pipe and place the pipe so that the drainage point is at the bottom. This requires an additional field-weld.

## Page 3

**SPOOL #3 follows the same concept as SPOOL #1.**
- **Misplaced nozzle**: The vent would be misplaced. Two options to point the vent nozzle upwards:
  1. Plug existing nozzle and open a new drainage point in the lower point.
  2. Sub-divide (cutting) the spool into an elbow+pipe and place the pipe so that the drainage point is at the bottom. This requires an additional field-weld.

**SPOOL #2 only needs rotation.**
- **PT nozzles**: Due to this movement, PT nozzles will be directed inwards.
