# 4521-PPI-LOI-039-062-0004-GAR Rev.00 INSPECTION POINT PROGRAM

## Resumen

- The document is an Inspection Point Program for a HEX replacement TES Solana project.
- It includes sections on documentation, material reception, and manufacturing processes.
- Inspection points cover various aspects such as welding, dimensional control, and non-destructive testing.
- The program is owned by Abengoa and requires approval from multiple parties before distribution.
- Volumetric inspection of process nozzles to head attachment.
- Non-pressure parts and removable welds dimensional checks.
- Welding parameters checking for various components.
- Surface inspections on pressure-boundary attachments, partition plates, and channels.
- Final assembly checks including bundle insertion and girth seam welding.
- Post-weld heat treatment with general inspection before PWHT.
- Inspections after PWHT for hardness survey and surface inspection of all welds.
- Hydrostatic tests on shell and tubes side.
