# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0006-GAR Rev.0 Magnetic Particle Examination Procedure

## Resumen

- The document is titled 'Magnetic Particle Examination Procedure' for Arizona Solar One's HEX replacement TES Solana project.
- It is owned by Abengoa and its reproduction or distribution without consent is forbidden.
- The procedure describes the detection of discontinuities in ferromagnetic materials using the Yoke magnetizing method.
- Equipment requirements include AC yokes, UV-A lamps, and calibrated light meters.
- Magnetic particle products can be either wet (fluorescent or visible) or dry (color contrast).
- The process involves checking the examination surface for discontinuities before applying magnetic particles.
- Magnetic particle examination requires checking field strength with a Berthold sample.
- At least two magnetic fields on the same area for 100% coverage.
- Indications must be greater than 1.5 mm to be considered relevant.
- Linear and rounded indications have specific criteria for evaluation.
- Acceptance criteria vary based on ASME standards I, VIII Div.1 & 2, and B31.1.

## 1.0 Object
This procedure describes the method for the detection of open and subsurface discontinuities of ferromagnetic materials such as steel welds and vessel parts using the Yoke magnetizing method.

# Magnetic Particle Examination Procedure

## General Requirements
- **Field Strength**: Checked with a Berthold sample. Two lower cross-section grooves must be clearly seen.
- **Magnetic Fields**: At least two magnetic fields on the same area following Table 1 for 100% surface coverage.
- **Overlap**: All examinations should have sufficient overlap to ensure 100% coverage.

## Evaluation
- **Indications**: Only those with major dimensions greater than 1.5 mm are considered relevant.
- **Linear Indications**: Length exceeding three times the width of the indication.
- **Rounded Indications**: Circular or elliptical, length less than three times the width.
- **Adjacent Indications**: Evaluated as a single continuous indication if separated by a lower distance than the bigger dimension of the smallest one.

## Accept/Reject Criteria
- **ASME I & VIII Div.1 & 2**: Cracks, relevant linear and rounded indications greater than 5 mm, four or more relevant rounded indications in a line.
- **ASME B31.1**: Any cracks or linear indications, rounded indications greater than 5 mm, four or more relevant rounded indications in a line separated by 2 mm or less, ten or more rounded indications in any 3870mm² of surface with the major dimension not exceeding 150mm.

## Final Report
- **Data**: Job number, drawings, piece description, procedure and revision, equipment used, magnetic particles type, NDE operator qualifications, map/record of relevant indications, material and thickness, lighting equipment, date of examination, evaluation, acceptance criteria.
