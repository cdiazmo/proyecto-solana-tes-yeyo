# TD-INS-SLN-012-01 Performance Test Procedure

## Resumen

- The document describes a performance test procedure for Oil to Salt heat exchangers.
- It outlines the aim, references, calculation method, agreements prior to the test, and testing responsibilities.
- Stakeholders must agree on various points before starting the test.
- The test aims to determine thermal balance, pressure drop, HAT/CAT values, OHTC, Duty, and Pressure loss.
- Correction factors are used to simulate real conditions during the test.
- Four out of six variables are measured for thermal calculations.
- Client and Lointek agree on reliable measurements.
- Steady state conditions required before measurements.
- Two measurements ensure repeatability.
- Fouling coefficient may be reviewed based on direct observation.
- Six heat exchangers in series per train.
- Instruments must have specific accuracy for flow, temperature, and pressure.

## 1. Aim
This document aims to describe the actions and measures to be considered during performance testing of Oil to Salt heat exchangers supplied by LOINTEK for HEX replacement TES Solana, placed in Arizona, USA. These tests shall determine:
- The thermal balance of both fluids, in steady state operation at reference conditions as indicated in Section 6 of the current procedure.
- Pressure drop on tube side and shell side at reference conditions.
- HAT (Hot Approach Temperature) and CAT (Cold Approach Temperature) values estimated at reference conditions.
- OHTC, Duty and Pressure loss calculation at reference conditions

## 3. Calculation method
The calculation method used is based on stated in ASME PTC 12.5 and predicts the performance values to be compared with the values measured during the test.
During the test, data of variables set for monitoring the performance test, shall be extracted from the centralized information on the DCS.
From these data, those which are considered representative of system operation shall be extracted.
In order to cross check the mentioned real measured values and the equipment production under the given input data, the “Correction Factors” are used. These factors (previously calculated with HTRI) simulate the sensitivity of a specific output of the unit (i.e. Tout_htf, Tout_ms, etc.) when the variables entered to the equipment differ from those in the Performance Case.
For this type of unit, the outputs to be cross checked are: Tout_htf, Tout_ms, Pout_htf and Pout_ms. The Correction Factors for each one of this values will be dependent of the following variables:
- Tout_htf = F (Qhtf, Qms, Tin_htf, Tin_ms)
- Tout_ms = F (Qhtf, Qms, Tin_htf, Tin_ms)
- Pout_htf = F (Qhtf)
- Pout_ms = F (Qms)
First of all, if the data match (within the limits set out in paragraph 7), LOINTEK will compare H&MB of both sides and will confirm that their similitude is acceptable in concordance to AME PTC 12.5. Then, once such acceptance is reach, LOINTEK would be allowed to figuring performance at reference conditions.
In case of non- coincidence, it is necessary to study the possible causes of the deviation (deficient instrument measures, fouling coefficient different from the estimate, other causes...).
The data will be sent to AEPC for approval.

## 4. Agreements prior to the Test
Stakeholders must agree on the following points before starting them:
- Adopt this procedure to perform and evaluate the results of the test.
- Define a testing responsible, which will have access to all necessary data and measurement operations with the team, while performing the tests. The client will be responsible for carrying out these tests; in these cases, LOINTEK will send qualified personnel to validate tests.
- Definition of reference conditions (four of the six variables of the system), see table of variables in section 6 , according to 4521-MEM-EPC-075-000-0001 MS -HTF HEX Warranty Annex COMMENTED.
- Establish the scope and criteria for inspections of equipment before the test.
- Establish criteria for steady state operation and design conditions.
- Establish agreements on fouling conditions to be considered in the calculations.
- The selection criteria of tools to be used in measurements of sy stem variables to be used in the performance calculation.
- Identification of known damage or equipment failures (eg plugged tubes, etc).
- Planning of inspections of equipments pre -test, calibration of measuring equipments to be used, preliminary performance test and performance test.
- The number, location and installation of temperature, flow and pressure measuring instruments, measurement units to be used during data analysis. In paragraph 9 is expanding on instruments used.
- Fix the frequency of the measures. In section 13, cadences of proposed measures are fixed.
- Acceptance test criteria for the Heat and Mass Balance (H&MB):When the different between the duty of both sides (Oil and molten salts ), as long as it is overlap of uncertainty bars as per ASME PTC 12.5-2000 Section 5.3.1.4, is equal or less than 1%, the test will be considered as valid.

## 5. Testing responsible
Client is responsible for carrying out this test, but Lointek is responsible for the analysis of results and issuance of the report.

## Measurement of System Variables
The Oil to Salt heat exchanger must operate at steady state conditions for at least 15 minutes before measurements are taken. Two measurements must be performed for repeatability.

## Fouling Conditions
The fouling coefficient used in design calculations is established as the initial criterion. If a direct observation of the exchange surfaces reveals an extraordinary element, a different coefficient may be used after agreement between stakeholders. The sizing of heat exchangers requires six (6) heat exchangers in series per train.

## Instrument Accuracy
Instruments must have an accuracy of ±5% for flow, ±3,69 ºF for temperature, and ±0,3 % for pressure. Uncertainty values are as follows: Transfer Rate ± 3 – 10 %, Heat Exchanged ± 3 – 10 %, Pressure Loss ± 3 – 12 %. Instruments used must be those for the control and safety of the power plant, communicated to the DCS.

# Performance Test Procedure

## 10. Known damages and deficiencies identification
In case of plugged tubes prior to performance test, a plane of the tubesheet of these equipment shall be included in Annex A, indicating clearly the total number of plugged tubes and their position.

As in the case of fouling resistance, if tubes are plugged to prevent leaking, total thermal duty and outlet temperatures of both fluids will be calculated with the HTRI calculation program, taking into account the new total effective number of tubes, that will be introduced in the program as input.

## 11. Inspections planning
Before doing the final performance test, a tracing of the general behavior of each equipment shall be carried out, in order to detect possible anomalies in the measurements of the parameters obtained.
It shall review, in particular:
- Overall dimensions of equipments, and correct placement and installation of lagging and its thickness.
- Define the number of plugged tubes (if applicable).
- Revision of the cleaning of inside surfaces that have been made after the hydrostatic test.
- Revision of the instruments to be used for the performance test, verifying its correct placement and operation.

## 12. Number, installation and location of measuring instruments
At least, the following measuring elements shall be installed on each equipment:
- Thermometers and / or temperature transmitters at inlet and outlet of the hot and cold fluids.
- Manometers and / or pressure transmitters at inlet and outlet of the hot and cold fluids.
- Mass flow meters in the lines of hot and cold fluids.

In section 7, instruments used for the realization of the performance test are detailed.

## 13. Frequency of measures
During performance testing, measures will be made, and they will be recorded in a table, with the following conditions:
- If the equipment could be operated under service conditions, these measures will be used to analyze the performance of the exchanger.
- The equipment must be operated at steady state conditions during at least 15 minutes. 15 measures shall be carried out during 30 minutes, with 2 minutes of separation between them.
- Whether any of the measures is inconsistent with data, it will be rejected and will not be used for the final analysis.
- When the operating conditions of the equipment change, the process shall be repeated as described above, so that, at every hour, different operating conditions can be analyzed.
