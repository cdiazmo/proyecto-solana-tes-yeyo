### Fittings and Valves
- Gate Valve: FA37 - CL 800 SW, FA52 - CL 300 RF flange
- Globe Valve: FB37 - CL 800 SW, FB52 - CL 300 RF flange
- Check Valve: FC36 (inclined piston check) CL 800 SW, FC51 (swing check) CL 300 RF flange
- Rotary Valve: FH55 – CL 300 RF flange
- Lift Check Valve: FC37 - CL 800 SW
- Silent Check Valve: FC55

### Notes
1. Lap joint flanges and stub ends are to be used only as required to assure proper bolt-hole alignment at odd-angle piping configurations.
2. When flanged gate valves 2” and smaller are required use FA54.
3. FC55 is to be used only in the boiler feedwater line at the inlet to the waste heat boiler as specified on the flow diagrams and at no other location.
4. All piping shall comply with ANSI B31.3 basic code (fabrication and erection, Section 327; inspection and examination, Section 340 and 341) unless otherwise specified in the shop fabrication specification.
5. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in locations regardless of size change due to difficulty of realignment of piping after cutting.
6. All pipe & fittings to be internally cleaned per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
7. At a minimum, all shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas (“TIG” – “Heli arc”) welding process.
8. All pipe shall be installed “clean”. The minimum standard shall be free of mill scale, slag, dirt, construction debris and foreign objects.
