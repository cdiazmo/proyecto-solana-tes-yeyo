# Butterfly Valves - Stainless Steel
## Specifications
- **Class 150 (insert)**
  - **Size**: 3” - 20”
  - **Material**: 310 SS insert wafer type butterfly valve
  - **Operator**: Weatherproof worm gear operator
  - **Flange Mating Face**: Serrated spiral flange mating face per MSS-SP-6
  - **Stem**: Lewmet 66 stem, Lewmet 15 disc
  - **Seals**: Glass filled TFE stem seals
- **Class 150 (insert)**
  - **Size**: 3” - 24”
  - **Material**: CD4M-CU insert wafer type butterfly valve
  - **Body and Disc**: CD4M-CU per ASTM A-351 and ASTM A-744
  - **Shaft**: Ferralium 255 shaft
  - **Pins**: 310 SS or ferralium 225 pins
  - **Seat**: CD4M-CU metal/metal seat with glass filled PTFE instr. Class VI shut-off at 450°F
