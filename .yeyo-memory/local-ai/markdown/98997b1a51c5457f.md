# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0008-GAR Rev00 - IV

## Resumen

- Visual Inspection Procedure for welding seams and base materials
- Applicable to surface condition, alignment, shape, and welding joints
- Codes include ASME Section V, VIII Div1/Div2, and SNT-TC-1A
- Responsibilities include Quality Manager and Qualified Personnel
- General requirements ensure proper inspection conditions and personnel qualifications
- Execution includes pre-weld heat treatment checks if no customer requirements
- Acceptance criteria based on Welding Procedure Specifications
- A reduction in thickness due to welding process is limited to 0.8mm or 10% of the nominal thickness.
- Weld reinforcement must have a smooth transition with adjacent base material and not exceed specified thicknesses.
- Concavity due to welding on root side is allowed if it meets certain thickness criteria.
- Alignment tolerance for butt welded sections requires no greater offset than listed in tables.
- Unacceptable indications should be removed or reduced, blended into surface, or rewelded by qualified personnel.

## Objectives and Scope
The procedure aims to establish conditions for visual examination of weld seams and base materials, applicable to surface condition, alignment, shape, and welding joints.

## Codes
- ASME Code, Section V – Non Destructive Testing (2015 Edition)
- ASME Code, Section VIII Div1 y Div2 – Pressure Vessels (2015 Edition)
- ASME Section I, Power Boilers (2015 Edition)
- WRITTEN PRACTICE ASME. QCM.PR05
- SNT-TC-1A

## Responsibilities and Authority
- **Quality Manager:** Determines items to check using this procedure.
- **Qualified Personnel:** Must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR 05, ISO 9712/2012 for EC Pressure Equipment Directive.

## General Requirements
- Inspector verifies item correlates with paperwork and previous manufacturing operations.
- Visual examinations require natural or corrected near distance acuity.
- Correct weld size, length, and location must be ensured.
- Direct visual exams require sufficient access and proper illumination.

## Execution of Examination
- Visual examinations before post-weld heat treatment if no customer requirements.
- Zones for examination include weld edges, surfaces, and base materials.

## Acceptance or Rejection Criteria
Welds must meet characteristics in Welding Procedure Specifications. Unacceptable indicators exceed Table 1 criteria.

## 7.3 Reduction in Thickness Due to Welding Process
A reduction in thickness due to the welding process is acceptable if it does not exceed 0.8mm or 10% of the nominal thickness of the adjoining surface, whichever is less.

## 7.5 Concavity Due to Welding Process
Concavity due to welding process on the root side of a single welded circumferential butt weld is permitted when the resulting thickness of the weld is at least equal to the thickness of the thinner member of the two sections being joined and the contour of the concavity is smooth.

## Unacceptable Indications
Unacceptable indications shall be removed or reduced to an indication of acceptable size. Whenever an indication is removed by chipping or grinding and subsequent repair by welding is not required, the area shall be blended into the surrounding surface so as to avoid notches, crevices, or corners. Where welding is required after removal of indications, the repair shall be rewelded by qualified welders using qualified welding procedures.
