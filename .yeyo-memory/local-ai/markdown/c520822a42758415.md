# 4521-CAL-AEE-58-11-0001-GAR Rev 02 HTF and Molten Salts Hydraulic Circuits Calculation Report

## Resumen

- Document evaluates implications of replacing existing TES System plate heat exchangers with new tube and shell heat exchangers.
- Simulations using Pipeflo® for both HTF and Molten Salts circuits under different operating cases (Summer, Winter Discharging, Max, 25%).
- Assumptions include pipes length, fittings details, operating pressure, temperatures, pump heights, and heat exchanger specifications.
- HTF circuit includes 10 main pumps and 6 HTF-to-Salt Heat Exchangers.
