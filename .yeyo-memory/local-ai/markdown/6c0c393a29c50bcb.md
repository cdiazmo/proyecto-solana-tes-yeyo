## Fill Materials
### Type A - Coarse aggregate road base
Defined in Specification 4251-ESP-ANC-00-50-2221, Aggregate Base Course.
### Type B – Sand
Natural river or bank sand; washed: free of silt, clay, loam, friable or soluble materials, or organic matter; graded in accordance with ANSI/ASTM C136, within the following limits:
- No. 4: 100%
- No. 40: 30 to 50%
### Type C – Excavated subsoils
Properly sampled, tested, and approved for reuse.
Material is to be homogeneous and substantially free of organic material and stone larger than 1 inch in diameter.
### Type D - Stabilized Soil
Chemically modified and stabilized excavated subsoil. Comply with Specification 4251-ESP-ANC-00-50-2342 for materials, quality control, and execution.
### Flowable Fill
Blend of ASTM C618 Class C Fly ash, cement, and water. Sand may be added as a filler. Flowable fill mixtures shall be proportioned to meet the following requirements:
1. Twenty-eight day compressive strength of the fill material shall be greater than 75 pounds per square inch, but less than 175 pounds per square inch, when cured and tested as standard concrete cylinders.
2. Workability shall be such that the mixture flows easily into all voids and crevices of an excavation with minimal mechanical agitation or dispersion. Flowability shall be such that the wet mixture is self-leveling. The mixture should have a slump of approximately 10 inches.
3. Shrinkage of the fill material shall be no more than 1/8 inch per foot of fill depth.
### Coarse Aggregate Fill
Washed stone; free of shale, clay friable material, and debris; graded in accordance with ASTM D2487 Group Symbol Coarse “GP”.
### Fly Ash
ASTM C618 Class C Fly Ash.
### Final fill course in unpaved or unsurfaced areas shall be topsoil capable of establishing vegetative ground cover.
### Concrete
Lean concrete conforming to Specification 4251-ESP-ANC-00-50-3300.
## Top Soil
Where shown on the drawings or otherwise required, provide topsoil consisting of friable, fertile soil, of loamy character, containing an amount of organic matter normal to the region, capable of sustaining healthy plant life, and reasonably free from subsoils, roots, heavy or stiff clay, stones larger than 1 inch in greatest dimension, noxious weeds, sticks, brush, litter, and other deleterious matter.

## Part 3 - Execution
### Examination
- Verify survey benchmark and intended elevation for the work as shown on the drawings.
- Verify fill materials to be reused are acceptable to Engineer, and test materials to establish compaction requirements.
### Preparation
- Identify required lines, levels, contours, and datum.
- Identify underground, above ground, and aerial utilities. Stake and flag locations.
- Protect above and below grade utilities that are to remain.
- Protect benchmarks, existing structures, fences, sidewalks, paving, and curbs from excavation equipment and vehicular traffic.
- Prepare subgrade according to Specification 4251-ES P- ANC -00-50-2230, Site Clearing and Rough Grading.
- Compact subsoil to 98% of its maximum dry density (Standard Proctor) within ± 3% of optimum moisture content determined in accordance with ANSI/ASTM D698.
### Excavation
- Take special precautions as required preserving condition and integrity of existing structures.
- Excavate subsoil required to accommodate building foundations, slabs-on grade, paving, and site structures.
- Scarify the exposed soil to an additional depth of 6 inches; adjust the soil moisture by adding water or dryer so the soil moisture content is within 3% of the optimum moisture content.
- Compact the subgrade soils to at least 98% Standard Proctor maximum dry density in accordance with ASTM D698.
- Grade top perimeter of excavation to prevent surface water from draining into excavation.
- Use precaution during final excavation to subgrade level to prevent disturbance and remolding of subgrade material. Hand trim excavation as required. Remove loose material.
- Remove lumped subsoil, boulders, and rock up to 1/3 cubic yard measured by volume.
- Notify the Engineer’s Field Representative of unexpected subsurface conditions or hazardous materials encountered. Discontinue affected work in area until notified to resume work.
- It is the Contractor’s responsibility to comply with applicable State and Federal regulations on excavation, shoring and trenching.
- Haul and store excess excavated material and excess imported material at site and as directed by Owner.

## Filling And Backfilling
### 3.5.1
Fill areas to contours and elevations with unfrozen materials. Do not backfill over porous, wet, frozen, or spongy subgrade surfaces.

### 3.5.2
Cut out soft areas of subgrade not capable of in situ compaction. Backfill with Type A fill or Type C fill, as appropriate, and compact to 98% Standard Proctor density.

### 3.5.3
Place fill materials in continuous layers not exceeding 8-inch loose depth and compact to 98% Standard Proctor density (ASTM D698).

### 3.5.4
Install and compact a 6-inch thickness of well-graded crushed stone with maximum 6% fines beneath building slabs.

### 3.5.5
Inaccessible areas should be compacted using hand equipment in 4-inch loose lifts.

### 3.5.6
Maintain optimum moisture content (± 3%) of all fill materials to attain required compaction density.

### 3.5.7
If subgrade material or previously placed subsoil fill has deteriorated due to weather exposure, scarify the top 2 inches of material to establish an acceptable interface prior to placing any additional fill.

### 3.5.8
Slope grades away from buildings a minimum of 2 inches in 10 feet, unless noted otherwise. Grade site to promote drainage for surfaces that are to remain exposed for an extended period of time to prevent water accumulation and subsequent softening.

### 3.5.9
Make grade changes gradual. Blend slope into level areas and match existing paving that will remain.

### 3.5.10
Remove surplus fill materials from site or dispose of in designated disposal areas.

## Placing Topsoil
### 3.6.1
Verify substrate base has been contoured and compacted.

### 3.6.2
Scarify subgrade to a depth of 4 inches where topsoil is scheduled. Scarify in areas where equipment is used for hauling and spreading topsoil and has compacted subsoil.

### 3.6.3
Fine grade topsoil, eliminating rough or low areas. Maintain profiles and contour of subgrade.

### 3.6.4
Remove roots, weeds, rocks, and foreign material while spreading.

### 3.6.5
Manually spread topsoil close to trees, plants, and buildings to prevent damage.

### 3.6.6
Lightly compact placed topsoil. Grade topsoil to eliminate rough, low or soft areas, and to ensure positive drainage away from structures.

### 3.6.7
Remove surplus subsoil and topsoil from site.

## Tolerances
### Top Surface of Backfilling Under Paved Areas: ± ½ inch from required elevations.
### Top Surface of General Backfilling: ± 0.1 foot from required elevations.

## Field Quality Control
### The final surface should be smooth and uniform and should conform to the required cross section and established grade.
### Tests and analysis of fill material will be performed in accordance with ANSI/ASTM D698.
### Compaction testing will be performed in accordance with ASTM D2922.
### Frequency of compaction testing:
1. One test per 100 feet of trench length.
2. Perform at least one test per 1,000 square feet per lift, unless otherwise noted by the Engineer. Minimum 2 tests per lift.
### If tests indicate work does not meet specified requirements, remove work, replace, and retest.
### Proofroll compacted fill surfaces under slabs-on-grade and site paving with smooth drum roller (5-ton minimum weight) to expose soft or weak areas of soil. Rework loose or soft areas to attain firm, uniform surface. Final density of reworked areas must equal or exceed density of adjacent material.

## Protection
### Protect excavations by methods required, preventing cave-in or loose soil from falling into excavation.
### Remove water which enters excavations.
### Protect soil adjacent to and beneath existing foundation from freezing.
