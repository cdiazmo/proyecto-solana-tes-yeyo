## Resumen

- Technical offer for 36 Oil to Salt Heat Exchangers for Solana Thermosolar Power Plant
- Includes design, material procurement, fabrication, inspection, testing at workshop, and transport to site
- Equipment designed for pressure loss as specified for SOLANA Power Plant
