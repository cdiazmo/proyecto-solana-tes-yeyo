## Specification Details
### Application
This specification is intended for fuel oil piping only.

### Service Range
- **Temperature:** Ambient to 325°F
- **Pressure:** 300 PSIG (Max.)

### Materials and Fittings
- **Construction:** Carbon steel pipe, SW, FS fittings, flanges, and valves
- **Fittings:** CL 3000 FS, SW, ASTM A-105 CS Schedule 40 buttweld, ASTM A-234 WPB
- **Coupling:** CL 3000 FS, SW, ASTM A-105
- **Union:** CL 3000 FS, SW, steel to steel seats
- **Branch Conn.:** Use fittings; for reduction use inserts; for full size through two sizes smaller use tee; for greater size change use sockolets or weldolets as required
- **Flanges:** CL 300 FS, SW, RF ASTM A-105, ANSI B16.5; CL 300 FS, slip-on, RF ASTM A-105, ANSI B16.5; use WN on BW fittings
- **Bolting:** Alloy steel stud bolts ASTM A-193 Grade B7 with heavy hex nuts, ASTM A-194, Class 2H
- **Gasket:** 1/8” thick Garlock Blue Guard No. 3000 ring type or approved equal

### Valves
- **Gate Valve:** FA37 - CL 800 socketweld, FA52 - CL 300 RF flanged
- **Globe Valve:** FB37 - CL 800 socketweld, FB52 - CL 300 RF flanged
- **Swing Check Valve (Vert. or Hor. Use):** FC37 - CL 800 socketweld, FC51 - CL 300 RF flanged

### Notes
1. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for tie-in locations.
2. All shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG) welding process.
