# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0009-GAR Rev.0 Visual Inspection Procedure

## Resumen

- The document is a visual inspection procedure for welding operations.
- It covers responsibilities, general requirements, and execution of examinations.
- Visual examinations ensure correct weld size, length, and location.
- Remote visual examination can be used for inaccessible welds.
- Acceptance criteria include dimensions and surface conditions.
- A reduction in thickness due to welding is acceptable if it does not exceed 0.8mm or 10% of the nominal thickness.
- Weld reinforcement must have a smooth transition with adjacent base material and cannot exceed specified thicknesses.
- Concavity due to welding on the root side of circumferential butt welds is permitted under certain conditions.
- Alignment tolerance for butt welded sections requires maximum offset not exceeding values in Table 3a & 3b.
- Unacceptable indications must be removed or reduced, and repairs should be made by qualified personnel using appropriate procedures.

## Responsibilities and Authority

### Quality Manager
- Has the authority to determine the items that must be checked using this procedure.

### Qualified Personnel
- Must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR 05.
- If tests meet EC Pressure Equipment Directive, personnel must be qualified as per ISO 9712/2012 (EN-473).
- Results must be interpreted by a Level II or III qualified and certified personnel.

## General Requirements

1. Inspector verifies item correlates with paperwork and previous manufacturing operations.
2. Missing stamps are observed after validation by company records.
3. Weld surface condition free of scale, paint, grease, dirt, or irregularities.
4. Personnel must have natural or corrected near distance acuity.
5. Visual examinations ensure correct weld size, length, and location.
6. Direct visual examinations require sufficient access and proper illumination.
7. Remote visual examination may be used for inaccessible welds.

## Execution of Examination

### Stage of Visual Examination
- Visual examinations before post-weld heat treatment if no customer requirement specified.

### Zones Subjected to Visual Examination
- Weld edges, surfaces, and weld seams.

### Acceptance or Rejection Criteria
- Dimensions and surface conditions must satisfy Welding Procedure Specifications.
- Welds with indicators exceeding Table 1 criteria are unacceptable.

## 7.3 Reduction in Thickness Due to Welding Process
A reduction in thickness due to the welding process is acceptable if it does not exceed 0.8mm or 10% of the nominal thickness of the adjoining surface, whichever is less.

## 7.5 Concavity Due to Welding Process
Concavity due to welding process on the root side of a single welded circumferential butt weld is permitted when the resulting thickness of the weld is at least equal to the thickness of the thinner member of the two sections being joined and the contour of the concavity is smooth.

## Unacceptable Indications
Unacceptable indications shall be removed or reduced to an indication of acceptable size. Whenever an indication is removed by chipping or grinding and subsequent repair by welding is not required, the area shall be blended into the surrounding surface so as to avoid notches, crevices, or corners. Where welding is required after removal of indications, the repair shall be rewelded by qualified welders using qualified welding procedures.

## 8.0 Examination Report

- Procedure and revision
- NDE operator qualifications
- Date of examination
