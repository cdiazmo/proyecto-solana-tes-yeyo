# 4521-HDD-AEE-39-70-0012-GAR Rev.01 Temperature transmitters datasheet

## Resumen

- Document title: Temperature Transmitters Datasheet
- Client: ABEINSA EPC USA, Project: Solana
- Prepared by Jose María Rodríguez León, reviewed by Manuel Muñoz Arranz
- Follow-up inspections include quality checks and manufacturing term tests
- Vendor's documentation includes calculation sheets, dimensional drawings, and certificates
- Final quality dossier requires 4 paper copies and 1 electronic copy
- Temperature transmitter datasheet for molten salt temperature monitoring.
- Three identical transmitters for different sections of TES HEX (C-D, D-E, E-F).
- Design parameters include temperature and pressure up to 750.2 ºF and 320 psig respectively.
- Instrument range from 32 to 800 ºF with accuracy of 0.1% of the adjustable range.
- Power supply is 24 VDC with output signal of 4 - 20 mA (HART compatible).
- Environmental protection class NEMA 4 and electrical classification Class 1, Division 2, Group D.
