## 1. SCOPE / ALCANCE
This procedure describes the operations to be followed in order to repair and isolate cracked, eroded, ruptured or otherwise leaking tubes from the tube bundle, by sealing both ends of the tube with plugs.

## 2. PLUGS / TAPONES
- **Plug material:** SA-105 or similar.
- **Tube material:** SA-210 A-1.
- **Filler metal:** The same as for the tube to tubesheet welding.

## 3. TUBE PLUGGING PROCEDURE / PROCEDIMIENTO DE TAPONADO

### 3.1. Remove moisture, grease and oil by using a brush.

### 3.2. Drive the clean plug, leaving the end 3 mm (0.118 in) out of the tube. See the sketch.

### 3.3. Tack weld and weld the plug to the tube.

### 3.4. Examine by dye penetrants.

### 3.5. Clean with stainless steel brush.
