# Thermal Energy Storage - Unit 6 Cold Salt Storage Tank
## Piping & Instrument Diagram
### Components
- **6-MT-251**: Cold Salt Electric Heater
- **6-MH-251COLD SALT ELECTRIC HEATER**: Located below pump suctions, close coupled manifold and insulated.
- **NITROGENDWG 4521-PLN-ANC-68-71-P207**: Springloaded valve.
- **MOLTEN SALT RECIRCULATION FROM HOT SALT TANKDWG 4521-PLN-AGE-68-71-P275**: Used for startup, temporary stack with birdscreen provided at exhaust.
- **6-MP-251**: Tes Main Heat Exchanger
- **6-MH-254NITROGEN HEATERS**: Emergency backup nitrogen tank connection.
- **6-MX-251**: Standby Hex
- **PCV**: Pressure Control Valve
- **SALT FILL LINE FROM HOT SALT TANK**: Salt fill line from hot salt tank, 36"MW capacity.
- **COLD SALT RECIRCULATION PUMP**: Cold salt pump, 6-MP-251 and 6-MT-251.
- **SALT FILL TO ATM**: Salt fill to atmosphere, 10% TE-251-31A1, 20% TE-251-31A2, etc.
- **VOC ANALYZER(OILDETECTION)**: For oil detection.
- **AIR BUBBLED**: Air bubbled in hot salt electric heater.
- **NITROGENTO STANDBY HEX 6-MX-252**: Standby hex for nitrogen supply.
