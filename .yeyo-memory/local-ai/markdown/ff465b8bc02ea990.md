# 5.10.1.2 CDO LSAT HII

## Resumen

- Certificación de José Enrique Myro Borrero como director técnico de obras e instalaciones.
- Proyecto de LSAT 220 kV S/C para evacuación de la planta solar termoeléctrica Helios II a la subestación colectora Helios I y Helios II en Arenas de San Juan.
- Ejecución bajo supervisión técnica del Colegio Oficial de Ingenieros Industriales de Andalucía Occidental.
- Cumplimiento de prescripciones técnicas de seguridad y pruebas previstas.
- Visado electrónico con número SE1103049.
- IEC 60287 standard for ampacity calculations applied to cables in flat formation.
- Ground temperature and resistivity values specified for thermal resistance calculations.
- Conductor, screen, and insulation properties detailed for cable specifications.
- Earthing system described as both ends single point cross-bonding.
- Multiple types of trench and crossing configurations mentioned.
- IEC 60949 standard for short circuit calculations.
- Conductor parameters include K, S, t, β, θf, and θi.

# IEC 60949 - short circuit calculations

## CONDUCTOR
- **K**: Constant depending on the material of the current carrying component (Cu=226, Al=148, Pb=41)
- **S**: Geometrical cross-sectional area of the current carrying component (300 mm²)
- **t**: Duration of short-circuit (1 sec)
- **β**: Reciprocal of temperature coefficient of resistance at 0°C (Cu=234.5, Al=228, Pb=230)
- **θf**: Final temperature (250 °C)
- **θi**: Initial temperature (90 °C)
- **IAD**: Short-circuit current calculated on an adiabatic basis

## METALLIC SCREEN
- **K**: Constant depending on the material of the current carrying component (Cu=226, Al=148, Pb=41)
- **S**: Geometrical cross-sectional area (50 mm²)
- **d**: Diameter of a single wire (1.04 mm)
- **n**: Number of wires in metallic screen (60)
- **Sw**: Geometrical cross-sectional area of a single strand (0.816 mm²)
- **θf**: Final temperature (350 °C)
- **θi**: Initial temperature (80 °C)
- **β**: Reciprocal of temperature coefficient of resistance at 0°C (Cu=234.5, Al=228, Pb=230)

## Induced screen voltage on open cable end calculations
- Using formula: E = a * d_s / ln(1 + a / d_s) * sqrt(I^2 * L)
- **a**: Axial distance between conductors (200 mm)
- **d_s**: Mean screen diameter (81 mm)
- For 1000m long cable with 300A current, induced voltage on open cable end is 31.3 V
