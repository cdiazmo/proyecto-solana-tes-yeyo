# TD-CAL-SLN-003-00 OPERATIONAL LIMITS

## Resumen

- This document outlines operational limits for the Oil to Salt Heat Exchanger at Solana Thermosolar Power Plant, including design conditions, thermal shock, operation area, and mass flow requirements.

## Markdown estructurado

# Operational Limits

## Aim
The purpose of this document is to define the Operational limits of the Oil to Salt Heat Exchanger of Solana Thermosolar Power Plant (Arizona, USA).

## Design condition
**Oil side (tube side):**
- Temperature: 399 ºC 750.2 ºF
- Pressure: 22 & FV barg 320 & FV psig

**Salt side (shell side):**
- Temperature: 399 ºC 750.2 ºF
- Pressure: 22 & FV barg 320 & FV psig

## Variation ratio in operation conditions
- Thermal shock +/- 126 ºF (70 ºC)
- Maximum heating/cooling ramp for incoming fluids must not be greater than 18 ºF/min (10 ºC/min).

## Operation area
**A. Temperatures:**
- Initially there is no flow of two fluids: |ΔT| < 126 ºF
- Initially there is flow of one fluid: |T1 – T4| < 216 ºF
- Both fluids in circulation: Operation temperature of the train has to be between 500 ºF (260 ºC) and 752 ºF ( 400 ºC ).

**B. Mass flow:**
- HTF flow: > 435.600 lb/hr (55 kg/s)
- SALT flow: > 674.600 lb/hr (85 kg/s)
- Maximum SALT mass flow allowed: 7.284.705 lb/hr (920 kg/s)
- Maximum HTF mass flow allowed: 4.516.173 lb/hr (570 kg/s)

## Others
- No special temperature limitation for the drain with N2, apart for the 18 ºF/min (10 ºC/min) temperature ramp.
