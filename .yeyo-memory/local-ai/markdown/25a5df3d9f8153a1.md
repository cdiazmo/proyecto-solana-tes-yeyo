# 4521-MOM-LOI-039-062-0001-GAR rev00 Performance Test Procedure

## Resumen

- The document outlines a performance test procedure for Oil to Salt heat exchangers.
- It aims to determine thermal balance, pressure drop, HAT/CAT values, and OHTC/Duty/Pressure loss.
- ASME PTC 12.5-2000 and MS-HTF HEX Warranty Annex COMMENTED are referenced.
- Correction factors are used to simulate real conditions during testing.
- Stakeholders must agree on various points before starting the test.
- Four variables are measured for thermal calculation according to ASME PTC 12.5.
- Cases include Maximum Charge, Discharging, Summer Charge, Winter Discharge, and 25% Load Charging.
- Heat exchangers must operate at steady state conditions before measurements.
- Fouling coefficient can be reviewed if direct observation indicates extraordinary elements.
- Instruments used for control and safety of the power plant are selected for measurements.
- Plugged tubes should be documented in Annex A.
- HTRI program calculates thermal duty and outlet temperatures for plugged tubes.

## 1. Aim
This document aims to describe the actions and measures to be considered during performance testing of Oil to Salt heat exchangers supplied by LOINTEK for HEX replacement TES Solana, placed in Arizona, USA. These tests shall determine:
- The thermal balance of both fluids, in steady state operation at reference conditions as indicated in Section 6 of the current procedure.
- Pressure drop on tube side and shell side at reference conditions.
- HAT (Hot Approach Temperature) and CAT (Cold Approach Temperature) values estimated at reference conditions.
- OHTC, Duty and Pressure loss calculation at reference conditions

## 2. References

## 3. Calculation method
The calculation method used is based on stated in ASME PTC 12.5 and predicts the performance values to be compared with the values measured during the test.
During the test, data of variables set for monitoring the performance test, shall be extracted from the centralized information on the DCS.
From these data, those which are considered representative of system operation shall be extracted.
In order to cross check the mentioned real measured values and the equipment production under the given input data, the “Correction Factors” are used. These factors (previously calculated with HTRI) simulate the sensitivity of a specific output of the unit (i.e. Tout_htf, Tout_ms, etc.) when the variables entered to the equipment differ from those in the Performance Case.
For this type of unit, the outputs to be cross checked are: Tout_htf, Tout_ms, Pout_htf and Pout_ms. The Correction Factors for each one of this values will be dependent of the following variables:
- Tout_htf = F (Qhtf, Qms, Tin_htf, Tin_ms)
- Tout_ms = F (Qhtf, Qms, Tin_htf, Tin_ms)
- Pout_htf = F (Qhtf)
- Pout_ms = F (Qms)

First of all, if the data match (within the limits set out in paragraph 7), LOINTEK will compare H&MB of both sides and will confirm that their similitude is acceptable in concordance to AME PTC 12.5. Then, once such acceptance is reach, LOINTEK would be allowed to figuring performance at reference conditions.
In case of non-coincidence, it is necessary to study the possible causes of the deviation (deficient instrument measures, fouling coefficient different from the estimate, other causes...).
The data will be sent to AEPC for approval.

## Measurement of system variables
The Oil to Salt heat exchanger must operate at steady state conditions before starting with taking measures for at least 15 minutes.

If in that period of time, flow stability is not achieved, the time of performing the test should be extended until this point is reached.

Two measurements must be performed to ensure repeatability of results.

Heat exchangers must operate at steady state conditions and as close as possible to the design mode. The limits, which are defined based on operating experience of this kind of power plants, are shown in the following table:

| Parameters | Deviation limit for test conditions of the desing mode | Steadystateconditionlimit |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Hot Fluid MassFlow | +- 10% | +- 3% |
| Hot Fluid Inlet Temperature | +- 36 ºF | +- 9 ºF |
| Hot Fluid InletPressure | +- 10% | +- 5% |
| Cold Fluid MassFlow | +- 10% | +- 3% |
| Cold Fluid Inlet Temperature | +- 36 ºF | +- 9 ºF |
| Cold Fluid InletPressure | +- 10% | +- 5% |

## Agreements on fouling conditions to be considered
The fouling coefficient used in design calculations is established as initial criterion.

Only in the case that a direct observation of the exchange surfaces is made and it is checked that some extraordinary element involves reasonably a review of this coefficient, a different coefficient to the design one shall be used. In any case, this new value will be agreed between stakeholders.

The sizing of the heat exchangers is based on the process conditions in the nominal design case (Case 1 of the Warranty Annex) and requires six (6) heat exchangers in series per train. Nevertheless, if the fouling resistance agreed by stakeholders is different from design one, total thermal duty and outlet temperatures of both fluids will be calculated with the HTRI calculation program, taking into account the new fouling factors agreed, that will be introduced in the program as input.

## Scope and criteria for selection of instruments used in the system variable measurements
The elements installed during assembly shall be used to calculate the performance of the exchanger.

His placement will be as close as possible to the exchanger.

Then, an indication of the accuracy that measures should have to the uncertainty of the results obtained is admissible.

Measurement Accuracy (over the measured value): Flow ± 5%, Temperature ± 3,69 ºF, Pressure ± 0,3 %

Result Uncertainty: Transfer Rate ± 3 – 10 %, Heat Exchanged ± 3 – 10 %, Pressure Loss ± 3 – 12 %

Note: The uncertainty is pending, must be justified through the delivery of supporting calculations.

## 11. Inspections planning
Before doing the final performance test, a tracing of the general behavior of each equipment shall be carried out, in order to detect possible anomalies in the measurements of the parameters obtained.
It shall review, in particular:
- Overall dimensions of equipments, and correct placement and installation of lagging and its thickness.
- Define the number of plugged tubes (if applicable).
- Revision of the cleaning of inside surfaces that have been made after the hydrostatic test.
- Revision of the instruments to be used for the performance test, verifying its correct placement and operation.

## 12. Number, installation and location of measuring instruments
At least, the following measuring elements shall be installed on each equipment:
- Thermometers and / or temperature transmitters at inlet and outlet of the hot and cold fluids.
- Manometers and / or pressure transmitters at inlet and outlet of the hot and cold fluids.
- Mass flow meters in the lines of hot and cold fluids.

In section 6, instruments used for the realization of the performance test are detailed.

## 13. Frequency of measures
During performance testing, measures will be made, and they will be recorded in a table, with the following conditions:
- If the equipment could be operated under service conditions, these measures will be used to analyze the performance of the exchanger.
- The equipment must be operated at steady state conditions during at least 15 minutes. 15 measures shall be carried out during 30 minutes, with 2 minutes of separation between them.
- Whether any of the measures is inconsistent with data, it will be rejected and will not be used for the final analysis.
- When the operating conditions of the equipment change, the process shall be repeated as described above, so that, at every hour, different operating conditions can be analyzed.
