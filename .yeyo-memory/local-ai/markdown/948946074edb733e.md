# Butterfly Control Valve Specification

**Tag:** Service
**P&ID Nº:** Line Nº Fluid Water
**Quantity:** 1 0A

## TV-1-320-10
**Design Conditions:**
- **Inlet Pressure:** 80,6 psia
- **Outlet Pressure:** NA
- **Temperature:** 250 ºF
- **Density:** 62,2 lb/ft³
- **Viscosity:** 0.309 Cp
- **Flow Coefficient (Cv):** 0,69

**Valve Specifications:**
- **Body Size:** Rating: 125 psig
- **Instrument Air Supply:** 60-80 psig
- **Communication Protocol:** HART
- **I/O Analog Signals:** 4-20mA DC
- **Actuator Orientation:** Increase to open
- **Action (Air Supply Failure):** Close
- **Action (Control Signal Failure):** Close
- **Body Material:** Bonnet Material: 316 SS (2"x4")
- **End Connection:** In/Out Flanged Flanged
- **Flange Face Finish:** RF
- **Travel:** 90% %
- **Valve Cv:** Note 2
- **Shutoff Class:** Zero leak
- **Disc Material:** ASTM A216 Gr. WCB
- **Seat Material:** Stellite
- **Ring Material:** Note 2
- **Stem Material:** (17-4 PH)
- **Filter:** Yes
- **Gauges:** Yes
- **Range:** 50-130 psig
- **Handwheel:** No
- **Hydro Pressure:** Yes
- **Material Certificates:** Yes
- **Leakage Test:** Yes

**Pending Information:**
1. Vendor to provide information.
2. Actuator sizing for 120% of the design upstream pressure - vendor to provide actuator sizing calculations.
3. Vendor to confirm: There is no technical issues with valve to be welded in the field.
4. Calibration Certificate and functional testing are required.
5. Valve body shall have an integral arrow indicating the direction of flow.

**Hazardous Area Classification:** Class1, Division 2
