# REPORT 068980-Corrosion study on A516Gr70 steel coupons (00000002)

## Resumen

- Corrosion study on A516Gr70 steel coupons under preheating conditions.
- Four parallelepiped coupons and one U-bend specimen analyzed.
- Similar corrosion morphology observed except for the U-bend coupon.
- General corrosion rate estimated using ASTM G1 standard formula.
- Metallographic study reveals uniform oxide layer without pitting.
- EDX analyses show significant amounts of magnesium (Mg) and oxygen (O) on the surface of A516Gr70 steel coupons.
- Magnesium oxide deposition occurs in the outermost part of the iron oxide layer.
- Iron oxide layer is identified as magnetite (Fe3O4).
- Deposits are mainly constituted of magnesium oxide (MgO).
- Cracking is not observed in the U-bend coupon.

## 1. INTRODUCTION AND OBJECTIVE OF THE STUDY
ABENGOA requests TECNALIA to carry out a corrosion study on several A516Gr70 carbon steel coupons tested under preheating conditions in a molten nitrate tank.

Four parallelepiped coupons and one U-bend stress corrosion cracking specimen are sent by ABENGOA for studies including examination, general corrosion rate estimation, metallographic study, chemical characterization of corrosion products, and cracking susceptibility analysis.

## 2. VISUAL EXAMINATION
Macrographs show similar corrosion morphology on A516 coupons except the U-bend coupon. Surface shows white to orange salt deposits with a grey-blackish corrosion layer. Pitting is not detected.

## 3. GENERAL CORROSION RATE ESTIMATION
Corrosion rate calculated using ASTM G1 standard formula. Values for coupons ref. 17, 18, and 19 are given in Table 1.

## 4. METALLOGRAPHIC STUDY
A cross-section of coupon ref. 20 is studied by optical and SEM microscopy, revealing a uniform oxide layer without pitting or localized corrosion.

## 6. U-BEND COUPON EXAMINATION
Corrosion products in the U-bend coupon are partially removed with a plastic brush. The surface is then examined by SEM. No cracking is observed at x 500 magnification.

## 7. CONCLUSIONS
- A516Gr70 carbon steel coupons tested under preheating conditions undergo generalized corrosion. Pitting or other kind of localized corrosion is not detected
- The average generalised corrosion rate measured for A516 coupons is about 1.66 ± 0.12 microns/year, for a testing period of 17860 hours. This corrosion rate value is the average of 3 descaled test coupons
- A516Gr70 coupons form protective iron oxide films in the molten salt media. The XRD analysis identifies iron oxide as magnetite (Fe3O4)
- Deposits on tested coupons are principally constituted of magnesium oxide (MgO)
- Cracking is not observed in the U-bend coupon
