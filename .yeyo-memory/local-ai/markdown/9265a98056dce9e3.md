# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0008-GAR Rev.0 Ultrasonic Examination Procedure

## Resumen

- Document title: Ultrasonic Examination Procedure
- Client: Arizona Solar One
- Project: HEX replacement TES Solana
- Prepared by Joanna Teixeira, reviewed and approved by Jon Baranda from LOINTEK
- Procedure for ultrasonic inspections of full penetration welds in materials thicker than 8mm
- Equipment requirements include pulse-echo ultrasonic equipment with specific frequency range and sensitivity control
- Calibration blocks are used for distance and sensitivity calibration, with specific material and heat treatment requirements
- Surface preparation involves removing weld spatter and foreign material from the base material
- Welds must be prepared if surface finishing does not allow correct examination.
- Temperature differential between calibration block and examination surfaces should be within 25ºF (14ºC).
- Ultrasonic equipment needs to be calibrated annually or before first use.
- Calibration involves checking screen height linearity and amplitude control linearity.

## 1.0 OBJECT
This procedure provides general requirements for ultrasonic inspections of full penetration welds of materials with thickness bigger than 8 mm.

## 2.0 SCOPE
As specified in the particular Inspection Program of the component.

## 4.0 RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY
### 4.1.0 QUALITY MANAGER
He has the authority to determine the items that must be checked using this procedure.
### 4.2.0 QUALIFIED PERSONNEL
The personnel who will perform the ultrasonic examinations must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR05. In those cases where the tests are required to meet the EC Pressure Equipment Directive, personnel must be qualified as per ISO 9712/2012 (EN-473) specifies.
The results must be interpreted by a Level II or III qualified and certified personnel.
A Change of a requirement in Table 1 (attached) identified as an essential variable shall require requalification of the written procedure by demonstration.
All changes off essential or non essential variables from those specified within this written procedure shall require revision of the written procedure.

## 5.0 EQUIPMENT
### 5.1.0 ULTRASONIC EXAMINATION EQUIPMENT
This examination shall be conducted with a pulse-echo ultrasonic examination equipment capable of operation at frequencies over the range of at least 1 MHz to 5 MHz. The equipment will have a control that allows to vary the sensitivity of the exam in steps of 1 or 2 dB.
### 5.2.0 SEARCH UNITS
The transducers will be straight beam transducers or angle beam transducers. The testing frequencies will be between 1 and 5 MHz. The angles of the transducers will be between 45º and 70º depending on the thickness.
### 5.3.0 CABLES
The cables will be coaxial type from 70 to 75 Ω of characteristic impedance.
### 5.4.0 COUPLANT
The couplant used must assure the quality of the examination and not be aggressive with the surface of the piece.
The same type of couplant will be used during calibration and examination.
### 5.5.0 CALIBRATION BLOCKS
#### 5.5.1 Calibration in distances
For the calibration in distances and to determine the actual exit point of the ultrasonic beam and actual angle of the transducer, it will be used the blocks V1 and/or 2 of the IIW.
#### 5.5.2 Calibration in sensitivity
##### 5.5.2.1 Similar and Dissimilar Metal Welds
For the calibration in sensitivity it will be used a block with holes and drills whose disposition and dimensions are the indicated in the Figure 1A (See to Appendix 1)
The material to build the block has to be produced by the same method according to the same specification or equivalent group of No. P that the material to test For this purpose the materials P-No. 1, 3, 4, 5A through 5C, and 15A through 15F are considered equivalent.
For calibration blocks of welds between dissimilar materials, the selection shall be based on the material side where the examination shall be performed. If the examination is performed from both sides, the calibration reflectors will be performed in both materials.
For welds between two or more different thicknesses, the thickness of calibration block shall be determined by the medium thickness of the weld.
The heat treatment of the calibration block shall be at least the minimum tempering requested by the material specification for the type and grade.
##### 5.5.2.2 Cladding
When the component to be examined is cladded, the material to fabricate the block shall be from one of the following:
- Nozzle dropout from component
- A component prolongation
- The same material specification, product form and heat treatment.
The block shall be cladded by the same method as used to clad the component to be examined. Where the machine method is impracticable, the deposition of clad may be by the manual method.
If the block contains welds others than cladding, and the component weld at the time of the examination has been heat treatment, shall receive the same heat treatment.
##### 5.5.2.3 General
The finishing of the calibration block surfaces shall be representative of the superficial finishing of the component to be examined.
Before the block manufacturing, the material shall be examined at 100% with straight beam transducer and the zones containing indications exceeding of the remaining reflection echo of the opposite surface will be excluded.
The configuration of the calibration block and dimensions will be the indicated in Figure 1A, with the following particularities:
For components whose diameter of the examination surface is higher than 500 mm, the calibration block may be plane or with curvature equal or approximate to the component curvature.
For components with diameter of 500 mm or less a curved block shall be used. In the way that the curvature shape of the component to be examined is inside the range from 0,9 to 1,5 times the diameter of the calibration block.

## 6.0 SURFACE PREPARATION
### 6.1.0 BASE MATERIAL
The base material on each side of the weld shall be free of weld spatter, surface irregularities or foreign material that might interfere with the examination.

## 6.2 WELD MATERIAL
Where the weld surface finishing does not allow a correct examination, the weld shall be prepared as needed to permit examination. It can be necessary the grinding of the weld overthickness.

## 6.3 TEMPERATURE
For contact examination, the temperature differential between the calibration block and the examination block and examination surfaces shall be within 25ºF (14ºC).

## 7 ULTRASONIC EQUIPMENT CALIBRATION
The ultrasonic equipment shall be calibrated at least once a year or prior to first use.

### 8.1 STRAIGHT BEAM CALIBRATION
#### Calibration in distance
The time base will be adjusted by the calibration block indicated in figure 1B (IIW: V1/V2).

#### Calibration in sensitivity (DAC)
For the calibration in sensitivity and to obtain the amplitude distance curve (DAC), the basic calibration block (Figure 1A) will be used.

### 8.2 ANGLE BEAM CALIBRATIONS
#### Calibration in distance
The time base will be adjusted by the calibration block indicated in figure 1B (IIW: V1/V2).

#### Calibration in sensitivity (DAC)
For the calibration in sensitivity and to obtain the amplitude distance curve (DAC), the basic calibration block (Figure 1A) will be used.

### 8.3 CALIBRATION FREQUENCY
Calibration in distances and sensitivities will be performed:
- At the beginning of the examination.
- When there is a change in the transducers, wire or equipment.
- When some of these variables change: type of material and/or thickness.
The calibration will be checked:
- At-the-end of each test or sequence of similar tests.
- Every four examination hours.
- When the operator changes.
- Always that it is suspected that it is not correctly calibrated.

This checking of the calibration shall be performed on, at least, one of the reflectors of the calibration block.

In the cases of some point of distance-amplitude curve (DAC) has diminished more than 20% or 2 dB, all the examination performed since the checking or previous calibration will be considered as null, they must be repeated with a new calibration.

If the point of the curve (DAC) has increased more than 20% or 2 dB the recorded indications since the checking or previous calibration will be cancelled, re-examined and recorded according to a new calibration.

If a point of the curve (DAC) has changed more than 10% in the base line of times, the calibration will be corrected. In case of noting some indication in the record sheets, these will be cancelled and the record will be repeated after the re-examination with the new calibration.

## 9 EXAMINATION
### 9.1 GENERAL
The moving speed of the transducer shall not be higher than 150 mm/sec.
Each pass of the transducer shall overlap a minimum of 10% of the dimension of the active element perpendicular to the direction of seam.
The evaluation and record shall be performed according to the primary reference level.
The exploration will be performed as a minimum of 6 dB higher than the reference level gain setting.
When reflector signal-to-noise ratio precludes effective indicator evaluation and characterization, a split DAC should not be used.

### 9.2 STRAIGHT BEAM EXAMINATION
Independently of the weld examination, it will be performed an exploration with straight beam of all the adjacent base material, in order to detect reflectors that could affect the interpretation of angle beam results. The location and areas of these reflectors will be registered, but not used as acceptance or rejection.
The welds in angle (Figure 3), whose surface S3 will be accessible and will be examined with normal transducer.

### 9.3 ANGLE BEAM EXAMINATION
The volume to examine will be the corresponding to the weld plus 12 mm to each side.
The examination will be performed in two directions that permit the reflectors detection with parallel and transverse direction to the weld, and two senses for each direction when it will be possible.

## 9.3.1 PARALLEL EXPLORATION TO THE WELD

For the reflectors detection with transverse direction to the weld. The angle beam shall be directed essentially parallel to the weld axis. The search unit shall be manipulated so that the angle beam passes through the required volumes of weld and adjacent base metal specified by the referencing Code Section. The scanning shall be performed at a gain setting at least two times the primary reference level. Evaluation shall be performed with respect to the primary reference level. The search unit shall be rotated 180 deg. and the examination repeated.

## 9.3.2 PERPENDICULAR EXPLORATION TO THE WELD

For the reflectors detection with parallel direction to the weld. The angle beam shall be directed at approximate right angles to the weld axis from two directions where possible. The search until shall be manipulated so that the ultrasonic energy passes through the required volumes of weld and adjacent base metal. The scanning shall be performed at a gain setting at least two times the primary reference level. Evaluation shall be performed with respect to the primary reference level.

## 10.0 ACCEPT / REJECT CRITERIA

All the discontinuities that produce a greater extent of the 20% of the reference level will be investigated by the operator with the purpose of determining the form, identity and location of such discontinuities to make the evaluation according to the established criteria.
They will be unacceptable the following discontinuities : - All those which will be interpreted as cracks, lack of fusion or incomplete breakthrough, independently of the length and extent. - All the other types of linear discontinuities whose extent exceed of the reference level and their length exceed of: • 6 mm for thickness (t) up to 19 mm. • 1/3t for 19 < t < 57 • 19mm for t > 57 mm.
In the case of different thickness welds the considered thickness will be the smaller of them.

## 11.0 FINAL REPORT

The result of the exam will be documented with the corresponding report that will include as minimum the following data:
- Job number
- Drawings
- Description of the piece or volume scanned
- Equipment and products
- Ultrasonic instrument identification (including manufacturer's serial number).
- Search unit identification (including manufacturer's serial number, frequency, and size)
- Beam angles used
- Couplant used, brand name or type
- Search unit cable used, type and length
- Special equipment when used
- Procedure and revision
- NDE operator qualifications
- Map or record of indications
- Material and thickness
- Calibration Block Identification
- Date of examination
- Evaluation
- Acceptance criteria
Beside this, all the reflectors that produce a response equal to or greater than 50% of the reference level must to be recorded. This record shall include: position, response level, size, depth and evaluation.
