# 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N180 Specification for Shop Applied Paint

## Resumen

- This document specifies the requirements for shop-applied paint on various steel fabrications for the Solana project.
- The contract allows alternate billing cycles upon request from the owner.
- All painting work must follow the manufacturer's instructions and Abencs paint specifications.
- Materials specified in standards or specificiations must be used without deviation, unless approved by the Purchaser.
- Surfaces to be painted include carbon steel, low alloy steel, and stainless steel fabrications with specific color requirements.
- Contract allows alternate billing cycles.
- Confidential information must not be shared without permission.
- Paint vendor's instructions govern shop painting procedures.
- Stainless steel surfaces require special cleaning methods.
- Fabricated items must be masked during shipment.
- Blasted surfaces should be painted immediately before rust bloom.
- Supporting painted surfaces properly is crucial for quality.

## Scope
This specification covers surface preparation, workmanship, and application of paint inside the vendor's shop to carbon steel, low alloy steel and stainless steel fabrications shown on drawings and schedules or specified in the purchase order.

## Application Conditions
1. Completion of Prior Work - Fabrication and required shop tests for tanks, vessels, equipment items, and piping sections or spools, including necessary repairs and re-testing when required, shall be completed before starting painting work.
2. Environmental Conditions - Surface preparation and painting of fabricated items shall be done in an area free of dust and contaminants from other shop work. Surface temperature of items to be painted will comply with paint vendor's instructions for minimum application temperature and relative humidity.

## Materials
All painting work shall follow the requirements of the paint manufacturer as described in their latest product bulletins. Conflicts, between those requirements and the requirements of Abencs paint specifications shall be brought to the attention of the Purchaser for resolution.

### Surface Preparation Materials
- **Carbon Steel:** 16 - 30 mesh sand, 40 - 50 mesh steel grit. Particulate sizes larger or smaller than the ranges designated may be used with the permission of the Purchaser provided they produce the required profile depth.
- **Stainless Steel:** Care must be taken to ensure mechanical cleaning methods do not contaminate stainless steel surfaces.

### Procedures and Workmanship
- The paint vendor’s instructions and the applicable sections of Steel Structures Painting Council Specification SSPC-PA-1-64 shall govern shop painting procedures and workmanship.
- Shop painting shall consist of the proper number of coats of paint material as noted in Paragraph 3. Method of application and dry film thickness (DFT) of each coat (in mils) shall be as shown in standards or specifications.
- The following surfaces shall not be painted: sight glasses and gauges, equipment identification nameplates, valve stems, plastic, rubber, copper, aluminum, galvanized steel, pre-finished metal furniture, glass and ceramic surfaces.

### Masking
- Machined surfaces of fabricated items shall be masked with durable material that will protect the surface against abrasive blast cleaning, paint, and contamination. Unless other shipping protection is specified, masking shall remain during shipment.
- Mask cleaned flange faces and cleaned pipe lengths, and structural members with a durable material, one inch from each end or edge. Masking shall remain during shipment.

### Surface Preparation
- **Fabricated Items:** Surfaces of all uncoated fabricated items shall be prepared for painting as specified on paint system standards or specifications (refer to Par. 3.1 for standards to be used). Blasted surfaces shall be painted on the same day before rust bloom occurs.
- **Placement of Work:** Fabricated items to be painted shall be supported to minimize contact with the painted surfaces. Freshly blast-cleaned steel shall be supported on clean supports and when painted shall be high enough to eliminate the possibility of abrasive being blown into the paint. Dirty bucks shall be covered with polyethylene or other clean, suitable material. Vessels may be supported on unpainted leg or lug surfaces.
- **Application of Paint:** Follow the paint vendor's instructions for mixing, thinning, pot-life, application, curing, and recoating. Remove all residues from cleaned surfaces with a clean brush or with dry, oil free compressed air.

### 4.6 Quality Control
- Unless otherwise agreed, the Purchaser, or the inspector as his representative, will observe work in progress and will inspect completed work for compliance with specified end conditions.
- The end condition of blast cleaning described in Par. 4.5 will be judged by visual comparison with profile panels such as Clemtex, Ltd. surface profile gauges and cleanliness standards such as NACE Standard TM 01-70.

### 4.7 Painted Surfaces
- Measurement of paint dry film thickness will be made with a calibrated Mikrotest gauge. Failure to meet specified thickness requirements is cause for rejection of the work.
- Work may be rejected if there is evidence of inadequate drying or curing, or of poor workmanship. Acceptable finished work must be free of detrimental dust and dirt inclusions, abrasions, over-spray, mud cracks, pinholes, runs, and sags, and must be uniform in color and even and continuous in appearance.

### 4.8 Correction of Defects
- The Supplier will be advised as quickly as possible of any defective or unacceptable work so that corrections can be made with a minimum of delay.
- Inadequate surface preparation and rejected portions of the paint shall be corrected as agreed upon by the Purchaser and the Supplier.

### 4.9 Handling of Painted Items
- Painted steel shall not be handled or moved until paint is sufficiently dry and cured to minimize abrasion and scarring.
- Steel cables or chains are not to be used for lifting or loading. All handling, lifting and loading shall be done with belt slings.
- Painted steel loaded for shipment shall be protected from direct steel-to-steel contact with wood or other suitable spacers.
- Cable, strap, or chain used to tie-down and secure steel loaded for shipment is separated from painted steel surfaces suitable wood or rubber spacers or mats.

### 4.10 Inspection
Purchaser has the authority and privilege of inspection of all work to assure conformance with specifications. The supplier responsible for surface preparation and painting shall notify Purchaser one week prior to the date the material will be ready for painting.
