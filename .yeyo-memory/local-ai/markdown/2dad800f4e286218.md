2. All final drawings shall be sent in ".pdf" and ".dwg" formats for the project. Final drawings not sent in CAD format will result in payment delays.
3. Each drawing shall have the correct CAD "paper size" noted on the document.
4. Vendor shall notify Abener Teyma if any changes are made to the final drawings in writing no more than 5 days after any design change. This letter should explain the change with pictures & drawings and explain why it was necessary. Abener Teyma reserves the right to accept or make modification/corrections to this change. Vendor shall be responsible for updating all final manufacturing drawings and incorporating these changes into the as-built drawings.
5. Bill of material shall show all OEM names, equipment part numbers, and equipment ratings.
