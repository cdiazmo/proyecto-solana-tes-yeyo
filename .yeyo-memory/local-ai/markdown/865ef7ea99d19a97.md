# Overall Site Plan

## Description
This document describes the site plan for the Solana Power Generation Plant. The plant covers portions of Sections 33 & 34, Township 5 South, Range 7 West and Sections 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 15 & 16, Township 6 South, Range 7 West in Maricopa County, Arizona.

## Key Features
- **Retention Basin**: Located at the site to manage stormwater runoff.
- **Hullage System Improvements**: Enhancements to the existing system for better water management.
- **Bearing Basis**: Benchmark set at S 89°52'26" E along the south line of Township 5 South, Range 7 West.

## Dimensions and Coordinates
- **ACCESSWAY**: Located at coordinates 10+00 to 11+00 feet.
- **Curve**: 723.92 Curve at various points.
- **Phase**: The site is divided into phases including Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 4, and GD-20 through GD-14.
