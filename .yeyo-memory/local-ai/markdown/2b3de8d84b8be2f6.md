# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0010-GAR REV 0 Coating and Painting Procedure

## Resumen

- The procedure is for painting equipment at the SOLANA SOLAR POWER PLANT project.
- It involves surface preparation and painting to increase corrosion resistance.
- Applicable standards include UNE-EN ISO 8503-1, UNE-EN ISO 8504, etc.
- Painting system uses inorganic zinc silicate for heat-insulated surfaces.
- Surface preparation includes shot blasting with grade Sa 2 ½ and grit-medium of 38.1 to 63.5 µm.
- Painting procedure involves checking surface status, cleaning residues, and applying paint immediately.
- Working conditions include temperature, humidity, product temperatures, and illumination requirements.
- Minimum inspection equipment includes thermometers, hygrometers, and gauges for film thickness and adherence.

### SURFACE PREPARATION / PREPARACIÓN DE SUPERFICIES
4.1. Before proceeding with shot blasting steel to remove rust, the steel shall be free of grease and other pollutants. Chemical waste will be removed by scrubbing with tepid water or pressure blast.
4.2. Rust will be removed from the entire carbon steel surface by abrasive shot blasting grade Sa 2 ½ as per ISO-8501-1.
4.3. The Grit-medium will be 38.1 to 63.5 µm (1.5 t o 2 mil). The control will be made with M10-123 of ELCOMETER.

# PAINTING PROCEDURE
PROCEDIMIENTO DE PINTURA

**6. PAINTING / PINTADO**
- Check surface status for the first coat application.
- Clean residues if necessary using aspiration or blow.
- Apply paint immediately after cleaning to prevent rusting.
- Use paint in perfect conservation conditions, stored as recommended by the manufacturer.
- Follow painting manufacturer's application specifications.
- Apply paint uniformly, air-less (free of water-oil), without drips or sagging.

**7. WORKING CONDITIONS FOR PAINTING / CONDICIONES DE TRABAJO**
- Do not apply abrasive blast-cleaning and paints if surfaces are wet or dirty.
- Surface temperature must be between 4-43ºC (39.2-109.4ºF) or above the dew point by 2ºC (35.6ºF).
- Relative humidity should not exceed 90%.
- Product temperatures must be between 4-45ºC (39.2-113ºF).
- Avoid applying when product mix has surpassed its Pot-life.
- Poor illumination is also a contraindication.

**8. MINIMUM EQUIPMENT OF INSPECTION / INSTRUMENTACIÓN MINIMA**
**8.1 Environmental conditions / Condiciones medioambientales**
- Ambient thermometer
- Contact thermometer
- Hygrometer

**8.2 Preparation of surface / Preparación de la superficie**
- Wrinkled meter
- Rust measures for steel surfaces and preparation measures for anti-rust painting
- Adhesive band for dust presence test

**8.3 Application / Aplicación**
- Wet-film thickness gauge
- Dry-film thickness gauge
- Adherence test equipment
