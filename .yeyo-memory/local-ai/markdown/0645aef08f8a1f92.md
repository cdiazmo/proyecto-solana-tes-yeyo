## Project Summary
The Project is a concentrated solar power electric generating facility to be constructed on an approximately 1835-acre parcel of an existing 3000 acre tract in Maricopa County, Arizona. The Project is a 280 MW (gross) parabolic trough solar power plant with a net nominal electrical output of 250 MW.
## Scope of Work
This contract includes all pipe fabrication for the HTF piping and supporting systems. Vendor shall fabricate all pipe spools as shown on the piping isometric drawings as part of this contract and deliver to project jobsite. All isometrics in HTF area are to be fabricated only once.
## Items Furnished by Abener Teyma
All items listed on the drawing Bill of Material that are not indicated as shop furnished such as bolts, gaskets, and valves. All specialty items which include, but are not limited to strainers, flow meters, manual valves, relief valves and control valves. All pipe supports, except those supports that are welded to fabricated pipe spools and clearly noted on drawings. All pipe & fittings for this project are pre-purchased by Abener Teyma.
## Vendor Responsibilities
Vendor Shall Comply with the Following:
- All welding shall be performed in accordance with ASME B 31.3 "Process Piping".
- Fluid Service Type ASME B 31.3 Section Reference

- **4.2 Cleaning**
  - All pipe spools shall be supplied clean without any excessive debris or rust.
  - Carbon steel pipe and fittings for specific systems will be internally cleaned per spec 4521-EPS-ANC-00-61-PS010.
  - Root pass welds must use Tungsten Inert Gas (“TIG” – “Heliarc”) welding process.
  - Pipe spools must adhere to spec 4521-EPS-ANC-00-61-PS010 prior to shipment.
- **4.3 Vendor Shall be Responsible for**
  - Shipping all fabricated spool pieces and loose pieces to the job site, taking precautions to avoid springing and misalignment of fabrications and damage to shop finish.
  - Identifying and locating Field Welds (FW) and Field Fit Welds (FFW).
  - Providing properly beveled ends for all Field Weld joints.
  - Clearly marking or metal tagging each fabricated pipe spool or loose piece with a unique piece number.
  - Providing the engineering office with defined number of fabricated pipe spool drawing prints.
  - Removing all loose scale, rust, grease and other foreign matter from piping material before welding.
  - Cleaning and painting all exposed CS piping or pipe supports in accordance with Abencs’ painting specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N180 prior to shipping if required.
