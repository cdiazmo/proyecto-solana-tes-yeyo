## General
1.1 This specification describes the scope of work for supply of all instruments as listed in this request for quote needed for the project.
1.2 Definitions
1.2.1 "Supplier" shall mean the instrument supply company chosen for the project.
1.2.2 "Customer" shall mean Abener Teyma.
1.2.3 "Fabricator" or "fabricators" shall mean any instrument assembly shop subcontracted by Abener Teyma (if applicable).
1.2.4 "Owner" shall mean Abengoa Solar Inc.
1.3 The attachments identified in this document are considered part of this specification and Supplier shall be bound to adhere to these specifications. Any specifications attached shall be met and followed by Supplier in full. All drawings and documents included in this package are considered "Contract Documents".
1.4 The imperative language of this specification is directed to the Supplier, unless specifically noted otherwise. Supplier shall perform all work described unless other parties are specifically identified as performing the work. All directives such as "provide", "install", "perform", "test", etc., are addressed solely to the Supplier, whether or not the word "Supplier" is specifically mentioned. Work included for others will be specifically identified.
1.5 English/ U.S. units shall be used on all drawings, calculations and any other documentation submitted by Supplier.

## Scope
2.1 Scope of this contract is supply of instruments as outlined in the following engineering specifications required for the project including all cables, gasket, manifolds … etc as identified in the main specification as options or accessories. Customer plans to establish unit pricing for material to be used throughout the duration of the project. Quantity of instruments needed for the contract should be identified on the main lead engineering specification. Shipping schedule to be confirm with purchasing during the final commercial agreement
2.2 Packaging - To be defined later.
2.3 Labeling, tagging: Specific labeling /tagging requirements to be defined later.
2.4 Supplier shall be responsible for delivery of all material to Abener Teyma selected Fabricator(s) and/or project jobsite. Fabricator(s) will notify Supplier of material required on an as needed basis. Shipments to Fabricator or jobsite should be scheduled to ensure a full load will be delivered in order to reduce transportation costs.
