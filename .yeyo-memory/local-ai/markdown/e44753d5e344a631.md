# Engineering Standards and Practices
## Material and Installation Standards
### Class 300 Carbon Steel – Piping System
- **Project**: Solana
- **HTF Return Ref.**: PS-CS77
- **Issue Date**: 6 Nov 11

#### Service Range
- **Maximum Pressure**: 350 PSIG @ 750°F

#### Construction Materials
- **Pipe**: Carbon steel, Schedule 80, seamless, PE, ASTM A-53 or ASTM A-106 Grade “B”
- **Fittings**: Socketweld FS fittings, flanges and valves
- **Wall Thickness**: .625" to .688"

#### Notes
1. Piping must comply with ANSI B31.3 unless otherwise specified.
2. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for tie-in locations.
3. Flanges should be used only where required to mate with equipment nozzles.
4. All pipe & fittings must be internally cleaned per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
5. Shop and field root pass welds must use TIG welding process.
6. Shop welds require 5% X-ray, field welds require 100% X-ray.
7. All pipe must be clean with no mill scale, slag, dirt, debris, or foreign objects.
