## Introduction
Flowserve has earned the reputation as a world leader in the supply of highly engineered pumps. For example, Flowserve is recognized throughout the process industry as a leader in the supply of ASME (ANSI) and ISO alloy and nonmetallic pumps for corrosive, toxic and other severe chemical duties.

Flowserve’s history of manufacturing molten salt pumps dates back to the 1940s. Existing designs cover a range of temperatures from 290ºC (550ºF) to 790ºC (1450ºF). Very few companies have the expertise to design and build pumps for this application. Flowserve offers the world’s most complete line of vertical pumps with the widest range of hydraulic coverage, pressure and temperature capabilities.
