# WALL BOUNDARY REINFORCING SPLICE NOTES

**1. Bundled Bars:**
- More than 2 bars (including splice bar) in the same plane is not permitted.
- Acceptable bundled bar layouts are detailed in Schedule below.
- Do not splice more than one bar in a bundle; see splice diagram for acceptable configurations.

**Additional Bundled Bar Notes:*
- Minimum CS: 1".
- 3-bar bundle with splice bar.
- Non-bundled bar 4-bar bundled splice.
- Maximum shop offset: 1:6.
- Bend CS staggered.
- Staggered bar spacing: 1.25 times the number of bars in a bundle.

**Splice Lengths:**
- All splices shall be "CLASS B" unless noted otherwise.
- For CLASS A splice length, see schedule for typical straight reinforcing development lengths.
- Bar location: top bars (horizontal with more than 12" concrete below), other bars (vertical and horizontal with less than 12" concrete below).

**Development Type:**
- Typical: D1 - C ≥ 2db and S ≥ 4db; D2 - all other conditions; D3 - C < db or S < 2db.
- Seismic: provide "SEISMIC" splice for all seismic splices outside frame columns and shearwall boundary elements.

**Reinforcing Grade:**
- Reinforcing splice lengths are for grade and reinforcing. For grade 75, multiply splice lengths in schedule by 1.25.

**Concrete:**
- Reinforcing splice length schedule is for normal weight concrete. For lightweight concrete, multiply lengths in schedule by 1.3.

**Notes:**
- Deformed reinforcing bars and threaded rod noted as EPOXY must be installed per manufacturer's recommendations; see schedule for minimum embedment lengths.
- Only non-rebar-cutting drill bits should be used to drill holes in existing concrete; care must be taken not to cut existing reinforcing.
- Drilled holes shall be cleaned of concrete dust and debris using a long brush and vacuum, or a nylon brush and oil-free compressed air. A blow-out bulb may be used if a vacuum or compressed air is not available.
- Special inspection required for installation of all adhesive anchors; inspector to verify and document embedment length, hole preparation, and cleanliness; inspect correct implementation of manufacturer's instructions for installation.
- Drill & epoxy to be used only where indicated on drawings.

**Seismic Bar:**
- At a joint: legends include crosstie, seismic stirrup, single-piece hoopseismic stirrup, seismic hook.
- 6≥ 3in
