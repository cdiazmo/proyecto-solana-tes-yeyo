## Gate Valves: Specification

### Class 125 FF flanged
- **Size:** 2” - 24”
- **Body:** IBBM gate, iron body, bolted bonnet, OS & Y
- **Disc:** Bronze solid wedge disc
- **Seats:** Renewable seats
- **Packing and Gasket:** Asbestos free packing and gasket
- **Rating:** per MSS SP 70

### Class 600/800 threaded
- **Size:** ¼” - 2”
- **Body:** Forged steel gate, bolted bonnet, OS & Y

- **Seats:** Stellited seats
- **Packing and Gasket:** Flexible graphite core packing, spiral wound bonnet gasket

### Class 600/800 socketweld
- **Size:** ½” - 2”
- **Body:** Forged steel gate, bolted bonnet, OS & Y
- **Disc:** Rising stem 13 CR flexible or solid wedge disc
- **Seats:** Stellited seats
- **Packing and Gasket:** Flexible graphite core packing, spiral wound bonnet gasket

### Class 800 socketweld
- **Size:** ½” - 2”
- **Body:** Forged steel gate, bolted bonnet, OS & Y
- **Disc:** Monel stem, flexible or solid wedge disc and seats
- **Packing and Gasket:** Teflon packing, spiral wound Monel/teflon bonnet gasket

### Class 600/800 sockweld
