## Machine Setup for Tube Bending
There are two different tube bending machines in the Lointek workshop:

- **Automatic machine for small radius bends:** Equipped with fixed radius tools and controllable bend angle.
- **Manual machine for larger radius bends:** With adjustable radius and controllable curve development.

The second machine can be used when the bending radius is greater than 15 times the external diameter of the tube.

## Bending Procedure
For the first machine, set the bend angle slightly lower than required (180°), then increase it slightly to recover from elastic deformation and keep the angle close to 180°. For the second machine, manually adjust until the curve development is slightly lower than required, then increase it slightly for similar reasons.

## Acceptance Criteria
Tubes must fit in carved holes of a wooden sample to ensure all measures are within tolerance. The wall thickness of the U-bent section should not be less than calculated using the equation: Tf = T * 4*R / (4*R + D).

## Stress Relieving Heat Treatment
The treatment should be done by induction method, with temperature and holding time specified in the applicable U-Tubes Purchase Specification.

## Hydrostatic Test
All tubes must be hydrostatically tested after bending and stress relieving according to ASME VIII Div.2 and SA-556 codes, using parameters from SA-210 code as per the applicable specification.

## Additional Requirements
- 10% of tubes should be inspected by liquid penetrant on the bent area.
- Clean all tubes of lubricants after bending.
- Maximum out-of-roundness at bends: no more than 10% deviation from nominal diameter.
