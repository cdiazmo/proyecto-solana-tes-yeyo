# 4521-ESP-ANC-58-61-PS001

## Resumen

- The project is a concentrated solar power facility.
- Vendor responsibilities include pipe fabrication and welding according to ASME standards.
- All pipe supports are pre-furnished by Abener Teyma.
- Specialty items like strainers and control valves are included in the contract.
- Socketweld joints cannot be radiographed, all socket weld connections shall be visually inspected.
- Vendor must ensure material delivered meets piping specifications and report discrepancies immediately.
- All pipe spools for specified systems must be internally cleaned per specific standards before shipment.
- Fabricator is responsible for maintaining cleanliness of piping during fabrication process.
- Field Welds (FW) and Field Fit Welds (FFW) must be properly marked and located on spool drawings.
- Vendor must remove all foreign matter from piping material before welding and clean/paint as required.
- Contract allows alternate billing cycles other than thirty days.
- Confidential information must be handled according to contract terms.

## Project Summary
The project is a concentrated solar power electric generating facility to be constructed on an approximately 1835-acre parcel of an existing 3000 acre tract in Maricopa County, Arizona. The project includes all necessary supporting infrastructure for a fully operational facility with a net nominal electrical output of 250 MW.
## Scope of Work
This contract includes all pipe fabrication for the HTF piping and supporting systems. Vendor shall fabricate all pipe spools as shown on the piping isometric drawings and deliver to project jobsite. All isometrics in HTF area are to be fabricated only once.
## Items Furnished by Abener Teyma
All items listed on the drawing Bill of Material that are not indicated as shop furnished such as bolts, gaskets, and valves. All specialty items which include strainers, flow meters, manual valves, relief valves and control valves. All pipe supports, except those supports that are welded to fabricated pipe spools and clearly noted on drawings. All pipe & fittings for this project are pre-purchased by Abener Teyma.
## Vendor Responsibilities
Vendor Shall Comply with the Following:
- All welding shall be performed in accordance with ASME B 31.3 "Process Piping".
- Fluid Service Type ASME B 31.3 Section Reference

- **4.1.3 Piping material specifications**

- **4.1.5 Vendor’s responsibility to ensure that any material delivered by Abener Teyma supplier meets the piping specifications. Any discrepancies shall be brought to the attention of Abener Teyma and the Supplier immediately.**
- **4.2 Cleaning**
  - In general, all pipe spools shall be supplied clean without any excessive debris or rust.
  - All carbon steel pipe and fittings for the following systems will be supplied to the Fabricator internally cleaned per spec 4521-EPS-ANC-00-61-PS010:
    - Molten Salt (MS)
    - Heat Transfer Fluid (HTF)
    - Steam (S, SV, SHS)
    - Condensate (C, CV)
    - Closed Cooling Water (CCWS, CCWR)
    - Boiler Feed Water (BFW & BFS)
    - Lube Oil (LO)
  - It is the Fabricator’s responsibility to ensure that cleanliness of piping is maintained while the material is in the Fabricator’s custody as well as throughout the fabrication process.
  - All root pass welds for the above listed services shall, at a minimum, be done using Tungsten Inert Gas (“TIG” – “Heliarc”) welding process.
  - **4.2.3 All pipe spools for services listed in paragraph 4.2.2 must adhere to spec 4521-EPS-ANC-00-61-PS010 prior to shipment to the jobsite.**
- **4.3 Vendor Shall be Responsible for:**
  - Shipping of all the fabricated spool pieces and loose pieces to the job site, taking precautions to avoid springing and misalignment of fabrications and damage to shop finish.
  - Identifying and locating Field Welds (FW) to accommodate spool shipping limitations and Field Fit Welds (FFW) that include extra pipe length to accommodate dimensional differences that typically occur in fabrication and actual equipment locations.
  - Providing properly beveled ends for all Field Weld (FW) joints.
  - Clearly marking or metal tagging each fabricated pipe spool or loose piece shipped to the job site with a unique piece number to facilitate the installation process. The project number, drawing number and line size shall be part of the piece number.
  - Providing the engineering office with defined number of fabricated pipe spool drawing prints. Field Weld (FW) and Field Fit Weld (FFW) locations are to be included on the spool drawings. The vendor shall mark all Field Weld locations and spool tag numbers on Abener Teyma isometrics and submit (1) copy to the Abener Teyma office in Chesterfield, MO, (1) copy to the Abener Teyma Office at the jobsite, and (1) copy to accompany each shipment/truck load to the jobsite.
  - Removing all loose scale, rust, grease and other foreign matter from piping material before welding. After welding, all weld splatters, icicles and other foreign matter shall be completely removed from the inside of the pipe.
  - Cleaning and painting all exposed CS piping or pipe supports in accordance with Abencs’ painting specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N180 prior to shipping if required. Piping that is insulated does not require painting.

##### Protection of Flanged Ends
All flanged ends of pipe spools must have a proper cover attached to bolt holes to protect the flange facings during shipment. Machined ends for field welds should be carefully capped with suitable caps securely anchored and taped in place.
##### Special Considerations
###### Carbon Steel Piping Systems
Victaulic couplings are required, and the Vendor shall provide necessary couplings and gaskets and groove pipe ends as detailed where grooved couplings are specified.
###### Stainless Steel Piping Systems
The Vendor shall furnish and install all carbon steel flanges at stub-end connections where applicable per piping specification or drawing.
###### Fabrication Tolerances
###### Scope
This specification shows tolerances for end preparation, dimensioning, and component alignment in the fabrication of metallic piping.
###### Reference Standards
The latest approved editions of the following standards apply, except as modified herein: Factory-Made Wrought Steel Building Fittings - ASME B16.9; Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings - ASME B16.5; ASME B16.47 Series A, only. Fabricating Tolerances - PFI ES-3.
###### Tolerances
(1) End Preparation: Pipe, fitting, and valve ends shall be prepared for welding as specified in the welding procedure specification. Preferred practice is shown in Figure 1.
(2) Linear Dimensions: Tolerances shown in Figure 2 apply to face-to-face, face-to-end, and end-to-end measurements of fabricated piping spools and to center-to-center, center-to-face, and center-to-end measurements of piping offsets, bands, nozzles, and support attachments.
(3) Angular Tolerances: The included angle of a pipe bend shall not deviate more than +0.5 degree from that specified.
(4) Exceptions: Closer tolerances shown on drawings supersede this specification.
