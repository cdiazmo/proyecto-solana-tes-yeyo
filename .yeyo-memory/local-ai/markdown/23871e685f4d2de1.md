## Material and Installation Standards

### Class 600 Carbon Steel – Piping System
- **Pressure/Temperature Chart (Figure No. 1) on Page 2 of 2**

### Construction
- **Carbon steel pipe, socketweld FS fittings, flanges and valves**
- **Carbon steel pipe, CS buttweld fittings, lap joint and WN flanges and flanged valves**

### Nominal Pipe Size and Material Specifications
| Nominal Pipe Size | Wall Thk. | Material |
|-------------------|----------|----------|
| 3/4               | 1.00”    | Carbon steel, ASTM A-53 or API 5L Grade “B”, beveled ends |
| 11                | 1.125”   | Carbon steel buttweld ASTM A-234 Grade WPB, ANSI B16.9 |

### Fittings and Valves
- **Forged steel CL 3000 SW fittings**
- **Gate Valve FA40 - CL 600/800 SW, FB40 - CL 600/800 SW**
- **Globe Valve FB66 - CL 600 buttweld, FC66 - (swing check) CL 600 buttweld**

### Notes
1. All piping shall comply with ANSI B31.3 basic code.
2. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in locations.
3. Flanges shall be used only where required to mate with equipment nozzles.
4. All pipe & fittings to be internally cleaned per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
5. Shop and field root pass welds shall be done using TIG welding process.
6. Shop welds to receive 5% X-ray, field welds to receive 100% X-ray.
7. All pipe shall be installed “clean” with no mill scale, slag, dirt, construction debris, or foreign objects.
