## Project Description
The overall Solana Generating Station project consists of a "greenfield" plant site near Gila Bend, Arizona. A new solar power generating plant will be built at the site. Vendor is to determine the optimum equipment required to meet the process requirements set forth in this specification. Vendor is to provide a quotation for equipment based on the worst case conditions.

## General
1. **Goal**: Define the scope of work and general conditions for the design, fabrication, and shipping of the nitrogen heater for the cold pump purge.
2. **Function**: The nitrogen electric heaters are installed to provide heat to the nitrogen stream. Equipment must provide the necessary nitrogen heating for salt pump seal purge.
3. **Operation**: Continuous.
4. **Location**: The equipment will be installed outdoors on an elevated platform, with no weather protection. See attached specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001, Project Design Criteria, for site specific information.
5. **Equipment Numbers**: Equipment Tag Number Required Service
   - MH 254 6 Cold Salt Pump Purge

## Performance
1. **Performance requirements are defined under the column marked “Abener Requirement” on the Vendor Data Sheets**
2. **Vendor is solely responsible for meeting these requirements. Vendor shall provide all equipment, controls, valves, appurtenances, and accessories required in order to meet this performance**
3. **Comments and exceptions to the specification shall be listed in a separate part of the proposal titled “Exceptions to the Specification”. All exceptions shall be listed in this section and shall reference the appropriate specification section. No other exceptions will be allowed**

## Codes And Standards
At a minimum, all applicable portions of the codes and standards listed below and in the attached specification, 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001 shall be followed. Vendor shall also meet all federal, state, and local codes for design, fabrication, and testing.

#### Scope of the supply
- Six (6) nitrogen electric heaters are required, each operating independently.
- Vendor shall furnish design, submittal data, fabrication, assembly, surface preparation and painting, inspection, testing, packing and shipping of nitrogen heaters as specified in this document.
- Vendor shall design nitrogen heaters in accordance with codes and standards identified in this specification and meet operating conditions and performance requirements set forth in this specification.
- This specification defines minimum requirements for the Vendor's design and fabrication of nitrogen heaters and applicable accessories.
- Vendor is responsible for all requirements delineated in this specification, except agreed upon exceptions.
- Vendor shall determine the optimum nitrogen heater required to meet process requirements.
- Vendor’s quotation includes line item pricing for startup and commissioning spare parts with quantities of materials provided.
- Vendor’s quotation includes line item pricing for recommended spare parts for one year of operation. Purchase is at Owner/Operator's discretion.
#### Design
- Nitrogen heaters handle nitrogen stream, maintain discharge temperature.
- Heater skids include heater, piping, control/power panel, power and control conduit and wiring.
- Heater design accommodates thermal forces over the design range.
- Vendor sizes nitrogen heater based on performance conditions, accounting for heat losses and system inefficiencies.
- Nitrogen heaters are thoroughly cleaned inside and outside, free from foreign matter.
- Maximum allowable pressure loss indicated on Data Sheets; vendor optimizes pressure loss.
- Materials of construction specified on Data Sheets.
- Each heater skid has a minimum of four lifting lugs.
