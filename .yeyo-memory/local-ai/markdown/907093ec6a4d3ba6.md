### Gate Valves: Specification
- **Class 150 RF flanged**: 14” - 24”
  - Cast alloy 20 (CN-7M) gate; bolted bonnet; OS & Y; solid wedge disc, integral seats; TFE packing and bonnet gasket, serrated spiral flange face per MSS-SP-6
- **Class 150 RF flanged**: ½” - 12”
  - Cast alloy 20 (CN-7M) gate; bolted bonnet; OS & Y; flexible or double disc, integral seats; TFE packing and bonnet gasket, serrated spiral flange face per MSS-SP-6

### Globe Valves:
- **Class 150 RF flanged**: ½” - 6”
  - Cast alloy 20 (CN-7M) globe; bolted bonnet; OS & Y; V-port plug disc; integral seat; A-20 stem & disc, TFE packing & bonnet gasket
- **Class 150 RF flanged**: ½” - 12”
  - Cast alloy 20 (CN-7M) globe; bolted bonnet; OS & Y; modified plug disc, integral seats; TFE packing and bonnet gasket, serrated spiral flange face per MSS-SP-6

### Ball Valves:
- **Class 150 RF flanged**: 1” - 8”
  - Cast alloy 20 (CN-7M) ball; bolted bonnet; single entry; A-20 ball & stem; TFE gasket & seats

### Needle Valves:
- **Class 3000 threaded**: ½”
  - Alloy-20 (CN-7M) needle globe valve; inside screw; screwed bonnet rising stem; needle point disc integral with stem; integral seat teflon packing

### Sampling Valves:
- **Class 300 threaded male inlet & plan end outlet**: ¼” - ¾”
  - Alloy-20 (CN-7M) angle needle sampling valve; outside stem thread; union bonnet; rising stem; needle point disc integral with stem; renewable teflon seat, TFE packing

### Check Valves: Specification
- **Class 150 RF flanged**: ½” - 12”
  - Cast alloy-20 (CN-7M) swing check; bolted cap; alloy 20 swing disc; integral seat; TFE gasket
