## 1. Purpose
This document presents the H&M Balances of KaXu Solar One power plant. These balances correspond to the most important operation points of the plant according to the Performance Model.

Every H&MB is named according to the net power simulated. The title of the H&MB indicates also whether the TES is charging or discharging. The last two balances refer to a scenario in which the TES is charging at a reduced load (there is no thermal energy available to charge the TES at normal rate).

## 2. Site Conditions
The thermal balances presented have been calculated considering the following site conditions:
- Ambient temperature: 25 ºC
- Ambient relative humidity: 30 %
- Ambient wet bulb temperature: 13.95 ºC
- Ambient pressure: 0.9 bar
