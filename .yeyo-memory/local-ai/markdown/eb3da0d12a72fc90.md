# Specification Form - Solana (Rev. 3/2011)

## Check Valves

### TFC43
- **Class**: 150 Flanged
- **Size**: ½” - 6”
- **Type**: PFA lined ball check, horizontal installation
- **Body**: Split ductile iron bolted body
- **Ball**: Solid PTFE ball, full port
- **Wetted Surfaces**: PTFE/PFA

### TFC44
- **Class**: 150 Flanged
- **Size**: 1” - 4”
- **Type**: PFA lined poppet type check, vertical up-flow
- **Body**: Split ductile iron bolted body
- **Plug and Guide**: PTFE encapsulated lift plug and guide
- **Wetted Surfaces**: PFA lined

## Ball Valves

### TFB43
- **Class**: 150 Flanged
- **Size**: 1” - 10”
- **Type**: PFA lined ball valve, split ductile iron bolted body
- **Ball**: PFA encapsulated ductile iron ball, M.I. hand lever
- **Dimensions**: F/F dimensions per ANSI B16.10 (same as Plug valve)
- **Wetted Surfaces**: PFA

### TFB44
- **Class**: 150 Flanged
- **Size**: ½” - 2”
- **Type**: PFA lined ball valve, split ductile iron bolted body
- **Ball**: PFA encapsulated ductile iron ball, M.I. hand lever, full port
- **Wetted Surfaces**: PFA

## Notes
- Valves to be furnished with provision for position locking.
