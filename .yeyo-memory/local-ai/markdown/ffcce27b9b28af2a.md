## Description
This drawing covers the design and construction details of a Hot Salt Tank for Kaxu Solar One. The tank is designed to hold up to 600 Tn of salt water with specific insulation, coating, and pressure requirements.

## Materials
- **Shell:** A516 Gr70
- **Heads:** A516 Gr70
- **Gaskets:** Graphite PSM-ASA
- **Internal:** API 650
- **Flanges:** SA 335 P11
- **Bolts/Nuts:** SA 182 F11 - 150#
- **Tube:** A516 Gr70

## Connections and Details
- **Nozzles:** Located at various angles for different functions such as salt inlet, level (radar), pressure, temperature, etc.
- **Drain Valves:** Gate valve with a 300LBS WCB/TRIM bonnet.
- **Insulation Support Rings:** Added for better insulation support.
- **Cable Supports & Skin Points:** Ensured for cable routing and structural integrity.
