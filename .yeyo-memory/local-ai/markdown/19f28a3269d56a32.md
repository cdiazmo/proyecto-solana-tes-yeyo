# 4521-ESP-ANC-68-61-CS32

## Resumen

- This document outlines the material and installation standards for a Class 600 Carbon Steel piping system, including specifications for pipe materials, fittings, valves, and quality control measures. It also includes pressure/temperature limits for components.

# Engineering Standards and Practices
## Material and Installation Standards
### Class 600 Carbon Steel – Piping System
- **Project**: Solana
- **HTF Supply Ref.**: PS-CS77
- **Issue**: 0
- **Date**: 14 Nov 11

#### Construction
- **Pipe**: Carbon steel pipe, socketweld FS fittings, flanges and valves
- **Buttweld Fittings**: CS buttweld fittings, lap joint and WN flanges and flanged valves
- **Nominal Pipe Size**: Various sizes from 3/4 to 42 inches
- **Pipe Material**: Schedule 80, seamless, PE, ASTM A-53 or ASTM A-106 Grade “B”, standard weight, seamless, XS Sch 40, .875” wall thk.
- **Buttweld Fittings**: Carbon steel buttweld ASTM A-234 Grade WPB, ANSI B16.9
- **Coupling and Union**: Forged steel CL 3000 SW, steel to steel seats
- **Branch Connections**: Use fittings for reduction, tee for full size through two sizes smaller, sockolet or weldolet as required
- **Flanges**: FS CL 600, SW, RF, ASTM A -105, ANSI B16.5, MSS -SP44
- **Bolting**: Alloy steel stud bolts ASTM A -193 Grade B7 with heavy hex nuts, Class 2H
- **Gasket**: 0.175” thick 304 SS and flexible graphite, spiral wound gasket, CL 6 00 API 601 (compresses to 1/8” thickness), MSS -SP44

#### Valves
- **Gate Valve**: FA40 - CL 600/800 SW, FA 66 - CL 600 buttweld
- **Globe Valve**: FB40 - CL 600/800 SW, FB6 6 - CL 600 buttweld
- **Triple Offset Valve**: FH66 – CL 600 buttweld
- **Check Valve**: FC39 - (inclined piston check) CL 600/800 SW, FC66 - (swing check) CL 6 00 buttweld

#### Notes
1. All piping shall comply with ANSI B31.3 basic code unless otherwise specified.
2. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in locations.
3. Flanges shall be used only where required to mate with equipment nozzles. All other joints shall be buttweld connections.
4. All pipe & fittings to be internally cleaned per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
5. At a minimum, all shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas (“TIG” – “Heliarc”) welding process.
6. All shop welds to receive 5% X-ray. All field welds to receive 100% X-ray.
7. All pipe shall be installed “clean”. The minimum standard shall be free of mill scale, slag, dirt, construction debris and foreign objects.

## Pressure/Temperature Chart
- **Pressure & Temperature Limits**: Various values from 926 PSIG at 100F to 602 PSIG at 750F
