# 4521-LIS-AEE-00-70-0001-GAR Rev.01 I&C Signal List

## Resumen

- Document is for the I&C Signal list of the Solana TES HEX project.
- Prepared by Manuel Muñoz Arranz and reviewed/approved by Jose María Rodríguez León.
- Changes in signals are indicated with red, green, or yellow colors.
- Signal codification follows the project's established system.
- Maximum character limits for signal tags, equipment descriptions, and signal descriptions are 18, 22, and 22 characters respectively.
- The document contains a list of equipment signals with their corresponding connections, redundancy cards, and base terminals.
- Equipment types include Pressure Indicators (PIT), Temperature Indicators (TIT), and HEX (Heating/Exhaust Exchanger) units.
- All equipment is connected to the TES (Thermal Energy Storage) system.
- The signals are transmitted using AI (4-20 mA) with a pressure range of 185,3 psia and temperature range of 800°F.
- There are multiple redundant cards for each piece of equipment.
- Multiple pressure and temperature sensors for molten salt and HTF systems.
- Valves and switches for controlling the flow of molten salt and HTF.

- Algunos valores numéricos tienen signos confusos (+1, -1) que pueden ser errores de OCR.
