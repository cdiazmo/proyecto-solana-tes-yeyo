## Specification
### Application
This specification is for above ground cooling water and other services with chlorides >1000MG/L.

### Service Range
225 PSIG at 210°F max.

### Construction
- Fiberglass reinforced epoxy pipe and fittings
- Nominal Pipe Size: 1, 1 1/2, 2, 3 thru 24, 36, 42, 48, -60
- Fiber Glass Systems: Red Thread II or approved equal; F-Chem or approved equal
- Pipe Qualification: ASTM D2992, D1599, D2996, D2105
- Fittings Qualification: ASTM D5685 and compatible with piping system
- Branch Conn. Use fittings (Note 1)
- Flanges: Qualified to ASTM D4024, ANSI 150# drilling pattern and pressure rating
- Bolting: Carbon steel A307 Grade “B” bolts with heavy hex nuts and washers; mandatory under bolt heads and nuts
- Gaskets: 1/8” Garlock Gylon 3540 / 3545 or Gortex Grade or butyl rubber full face gasket; 5 0-70 Shore A durometer hardness

### Notes
1. Pipe saddles with bonded type outlet may be used for outlet sizes up to and including one-half the nominal header size.
2. Pipe spool may be required to prevent interferences between disc and other specialty items upon operation of valve.
3. For pipe sizes 2” and smaller, see pipe spec 4521-ESP-ANC-68-61-CS95.
