# W103387-0-60070 Painting Procedure en-00

## Resumen

- Painting method uses HEAT-FLEX HI-TEMP 1200 coating by Sherwin Williams.
- Surface preparation involves sand blasting SSPC-SP10.
- Layer thickness requirements are >125 µm for each coat and >375 µm total dry film.
- Temperature range for application is ambient to 260 °C, with operation at >500 °C.
- Coating provides protection against corrosion under insulation and high heat applications.
- Recommended uses include power plants, refineries, and chemical facilities.
- HEAT-FLEX® HI-TEMP 1200 coating is used under insulation for protective and marine applications.
- Surface preparation is crucial; it must be clean, dry, and free of foreign materials.
- Minimum recommended surface preparation varies by material type (iron/steel, stainless steel).
- Application conditions include temperature and humidity ranges.
- Packaging options are available in 1 gallon or 5-gallon containers.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects with limited liability for defective products.

# Paint Coat Application and Rust Prevention Procedure

**Product Data:**
- **Painting Method:** Single-component inert multipolymeric matrix coating
- **Manufacturer:** Sherwin Williams
- **Color:** B59A225 Grey

**Surface Preparation:** Sand Blasting SSPC-SP10. The surfaces are dried and clean.

**Layer Thickness:**
- 1st layer coat: > 125 µm
- 2nd layer coat: > 125 µm
- Top layer coat: > 125 µm
- Dry coat thickness total: > 375 µm

**Temperature Range:**
- Operation: > 500 °C
- Application: ambient to 260 °C

**HEAT-FLEX® HI-TEMP 1200 Coating Characteristics:**
- Finish: Low Sheen
- Color: Gray and Dark Gray
- Volume Solids: 57% ± 2%
- Weight Solids: 81% ± 2%
- VOC (EP A Method 24): <375 g/L; 3.2 lb/gal
- Recommended Spreading Rate per coat: Minimum 8.0 (200), Maximum 10.0 (250) Wet mils (microns)
- Coverage sq ft/gal (m2/L): 152 (3.7) - 182 (4.5)

- @ 50°F/10°C: To touch 30 minutes, To handle 90 minutes, To recoat 3 hours, To ship 24 hours*
- Full cure at 350°F/177°C is 30 minutes

**Shelf Life:** 12 months, unopened at 77°F (25°C)

**Storage:** Indoors at 40°F (4.5°C) to 100°F (31°C)

**Flash Point:** 87°F (31°C) SETA

**Recommended Uses:**
- Direct to steel or stainless steel

- **Stainless Steel:**
  - SSPC-SP1, Do not use chlorinated solvents for cleaning

# HEAT-FLEX® HI-TEMP 1200 COATING UNDER INSULATION

**Protective & Marine Coatings**

**Application Bulletin**

Surface preparation must be completed as indicated.

**Mixing Instructions:** Mix paint thoroughly with low speed power agitation before use. Obtain a uniform consistency. Additional mixing during application may be necessary due to heavy consistency. Do not incorporate air.

**Recommended Spreading Rate per Coat: Minimum Maximum Wet mils (microns) 8.0 (200) 10.0 (250) Dry mils (microns) 5.0 (125) 6.0 (150) Coverage sq ft/gal (m2/L) 152 (3.7) 182 (4.5)

**Drying Schedule @ 8.0 mils wet (200 microns):**
- **@ 50°F/10°C:** To touch: 30 minutes, To handle: 90 minutes, To recoat: 3 hours, To ship: 24 hours*
- **@ 77 °F/25°C:** To touch: 20 minutes, To handle: 60 minutes, To recoat: 2 hours, To ship: 24 hours
- **@ 120 °F/49°C:** To touch: 10 minutes, To handle: 30 minutes, To recoat: 1 hour, To ship: 24 hours

**Full Cure at 350°F/177°C is 30 minutes.**

**Application of coating above maximum or below minimum recommended spreading rate may adversely affect coating performance.

**Clean-up Instructions:** Clean spills and spatters immediately with Xylene, R2K4. Clean tools immediately after use with mineral spirits. Follow manufacturer's safety recommendations when using any solvent.

**Performance Tips:** When using spray application, use a 50% overlap with each pass of the gun to avoid holidays, bare areas, and pinholes. If necessary, cross spray at a right angle. Spreading rates are calculated on volume solids and do not include an application loss factor due to surface profile, roughness or porosity of the surface, skill and technique of the applicator, method of application, various surface irregularities, material lost during mixing, spillage, overthinning, climatic conditions, and excessive film build. No reduction of material is recommended as it can affect film build, appearance, and adhesion. *If reduction is required for application to hot steel, use MAK, R6K30 up to a maximum of 5% by volume.* During application to hot steel, apply coating in several thin passes to allow solvent to escape and to prevent blistering. Allow at least 15-20 minutes between each coat. If blistering does occur, brush out immediately with a china bristle brush. Always test adhesion by applying a test patch of 2-3 square feet. Allow one week to dry before checking adhesion.

**In order to avoid blockage of spray equipment, clean equipment before use or before periods of extended downtime with xylene. Minor color change may be exhibited in exposed service, but will not affect performance.

**Topcoating:** If applying a topcoat, apply a mist coat of the topcoat. Allow 10 minutes flash off and follow with a full coat.
