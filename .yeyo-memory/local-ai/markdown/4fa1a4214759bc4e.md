Information and know-how hereon are confidential and proprietary property of Abener and may not be used, reproduced, or revealed to others except in accordance with the terms of a contract with or other written permission of Abener.

## General Description
This specification sets forth acceptable standards of materials for removable and reusable blanket, pad and jacket type insulation. This type of insulation shall be used for covering such items as vessel and duct manway/access way covers, duct expansion joints and duct blank-offs, gates, valves, sight glasses, pumps, etc.
