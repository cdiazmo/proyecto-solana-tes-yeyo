## Specification
### Application
This specification is for misc. water (not potable) and chemical feed.

### Service Range
- **Temperature:** 40°F to 120°F
- **Pressure:** 40 PSIG Maximum

### Materials
- **Pipe:** Unplasticized, polyvinyl chloride (PVC) Type 1, Grade 1, Schedule 40 plain ends
- **Fittings:** Injection molded PVC Type 1, Grade 1, Schedule 40, socket ends
- **Valves:** Ball Valve, Globe Valve, Butterfly Valve, Ball Check Valve
- **Bolts:** Carbon steel ASTM A-307 Grade B bolts with heavy hex nuts and washers
- **Gaskets:** 1/8” Butyl rubber full face gaskets

### Notes
1. Threaded connections may be used at equipment or specialty items as required.
2. All threaded connections to be made up with TFE thread seal tape.
3. Cabot Type 80P PVC solvent cement for socket joints.
4. PVC lines can be electric traced, but not steam traced.
5. Pipe spool may be required for valve operation.
6. Washers mandatory under bolt heads and nuts.
7. Above-ground PVC piping exposed to direct sunlight must be painted per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N101 as UV protection.
