## 1. INTRODUCTION
This specification, together with the related specifications, codes and standards constitutes a complete “User’s Design Specification” and shall contain sufficient detail to provide a complete basis for design, construction, examination and test in accordance with the requirements of the ASME Code, Section VIII, Div 2, Ed. 2017, Class 2.

## 7 DESIGN FATIGUE LIFE
A fatigue analysis is required for this vessel as the intended vessel operation does not satisfy the Requirement of Clause 5.5.2. of Part-5 of ASME code section VIII Div. 2:
1. This pressure vessel is not subjected to fatigue due to pressure (constant pressure in both sides).
2. The total number of full range pressure cycles and operating pressure cycles (including startup and shutdown), plus those in which the range of pressure variation exceeds 20% of the design pressure, is below 1000 cycles.
3. The only source of fatigue cycles is metal temperature variations (thermal cycles).
