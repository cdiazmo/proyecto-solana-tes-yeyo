# TD-INS-SLN-012-00 Performance Test Procedure

## Resumen

- Document describes performance testing procedure for Oil to Salt heat exchangers.
- ASME PTC 12.5-2000 and 4521-MEM-EPC-075-000-0001 MS-HTF HEX Warranty Annex COMMENTED are referenced.
- Calculation method based on ASME PTC 12.5 predicts performance values to be compared with measured data.
- Stakeholders must agree on various points before starting the test, including testing responsible and reference conditions.
- Client is responsible for carrying out tests, while Lointek analyzes results and issues reports.
- Four variables are measured for thermal calculation according to ASME PTC 12.5.
- Five cases (Maximum Charge, Discharging, Summer Charge, Winter Discharge, and 25% Load Charging) define reference conditions.
- Heat exchanger must operate at steady state for at least 15 minutes before measurements.
- Fouling coefficient may be reviewed if extraordinary elements are found during inspection.
- Instruments used for control and safety of the power plant will measure system variables.
- Plugged tubes should be documented in Annex A.
- HTRI program calculates thermal duty and outlet temperatures for plugged tubes.

## 1. Aim
This document aims to describe the actions and measures to be considered during performance testing of Oil to Salt heat exchangers supplied by LOINTEK for HEX replacement TES Solana, placed in Arizona, USA.

## 2. References
- ASME PTC 12.5-2000 Single Phase Heat Exchangers: Performance Test Codes
- 4521-MEM-EPC-075-000-0001 MS-HTF HEX Warranty Annex COMMENTED

## 3. Calculation method
The calculation method used is based on ASME PTC 12.5 and predicts the performance values to be compared with the values measured during the test.

## 4. Agreements prior to the Test
Stakeholders must agree on the following points before starting them:
- Adopt this procedure to perform and evaluate the results of the test.
- Define a testing responsible, which will have access to all necessary data and measurement operations with the team, while performing the tests.
- Definition of reference conditions (four of the six variables of the system).
- Establish the scope and criteria for inspections of equipment before the test.
- Establish criteria for steady state operation and design conditions.
- Establish agreements on fouling conditions to be considered in the calculations.
- The selection criteria of tools to be used in measurements of system variables to be used in the performance calculation.
- Identification of known damage or equipment failures (eg plugged tubes, etc).
- Planning of inspections of equipments pre-test, calibration of measuring equipment to be used, preliminary performance test and performance test.
- The number, location and installation of temperature, flow and pressure measuring instruments, measurement units to be used during data analysis.
- Fix the frequency of the measures.
- Acceptance test criteria for the Heat and Mass Balance (H&MB).

## 5. Testing responsible
Client is responsible for carrying out this test, but Lointek is responsible for the analysis of results and issuance of the report.

## Measurement of system variables
The Oil to Salt heat exchanger must operate at steady state conditions for at least 15 minutes before measurements are taken.

## Agreements on fouling conditions to be considered
The fouling coefficient used in design calculations is established as the initial criterion. If a direct observation of the exchange surfaces reveals an extraordinary element, a different coefficient may be used after agreement between stakeholders.

## Scope and criteria for selection of instruments used in the system variable measurements
Instruments used for control and safety of the power plant will be used, communicating with the DCS to record necessary measures. The uncertainty must be justified through supporting calculations.

# Performance Test Procedure

## 10. Known damages and deficiencies identification
In case of plugged tubes prior to performance test, a plane of the tubesheet of these equipment shall be included in Annex A, indicating clearly the total number of plugged tubes and their position.

As in the case of fouling resistance, if tubes are plugged to prevent leaking, total thermal duty and outlet temperatures of both fluids will be calculated with the HTRI calculation program, taking into account the new total effective number of tubes, that will be introduced in the program as input.

## 11. Inspections planning
Before doing the final performance test, a tracing of the general behavior of each equipment shall be carried out, in order to detect possible anomalies in the measurements of the parameters obtained.
It shall review, in particular:
- Overall dimensions of equipments, and correct placement and installation of lagging and its thickness.
- Define the number of plugged tubes (if applicable).
- Revision of the cleaning of inside surfaces that have been made after the hydrostatic test.
- Revision of the instruments to be used for the performance test, verifying its correct placement and operation.

## 12. Number, installation and location of measuring instruments
At least, the following measuring elements shall be installed on each equipment:
- Thermometers and / or temperature transmitters at inlet and outlet of the hot and cold fluids.
- Manometers and / or pressure transmitters at inlet and outlet of the hot and cold fluids.
- Mass flow meters in the lines of hot and cold fluids.

In section 7, instruments used for the realization of the performance test are detailed.

## 13. Frequency of measures
During performance testing, measures will be made, and they will be recorded in a table, with the following conditions:
- If the equipment could be operated under service conditions, these measures will be used to analyze the performance of the exchanger.
- The equipment must be operated at steady state conditions during at least 15 minutes. 15 measures shall be carried out during 30 minutes, with 2 minutes of separation between them.
- Whether any of the measures is inconsistent with data, it will be rejected and will not be used for the final analysis.
- When the operating conditions of the equipment change, the process shall be repeated as described above, so that, at every hour, different operating conditions can be analyzed.
