# 4521-MEM-EPC-075-000-0001 MS-HTF HEX Warranty Annex COMMENTED

## Resumen

- Document title: MS-HTF Shell & Tube Heat Exchangers
- Client: Arizona Solar One LLC, Project: Solana Generation Station
- Prepared by Ricardo R. Delgado A., Reviewed and Approved by Andrés Barros Borrero and Jaime Rojas Blasco respectively
- Purpose: Include guaranteed figures for Supplier to pass Practical Completion Test
- Acceptance Test includes Heat and Mass balances with specific pressure drop and temperature requirements
- Noise warranty requires sound pressure level not to exceed 85 dB(A)
- The performance test procedure must be delivered 6 months before the Acceptance Test and approved by Teyma USA & Abener Engineering and Construction Services General Partnership.
- The availability warranty must be fulfilled after receiving the heat exchanger, with a minimum of 99.9% availability guaranteed for 24 months starting from Practical Completion.
- Availability is measured in 12-month periods using a specific formula that considers operation hours, capacity factors, stand-by time, scheduled and non-scheduled maintenance.
- Non-scheduled maintenance does not include delays due to causes not attributable to the Supplier.

## 1. Purpose
The main object of this document is to include all the guaranteed figures that must be reached by the Supplier to obtain the Practical Completion Test of the plant.
## 2. Acceptance Test
To pass the Acceptance Test, the equipment must fulfill the requirements shown on the following points:
### 2.1. Heat and Mass balances
The Supplier must guarantee the approach temperatures and pressure drop for each stream considered in the cases presented below.
**Guaranteed Case 1:**
- **Psia:** 137.7, **ºF:** 554.40, **Lb/h:** 6,622,459, **BTU/Lb:** 183.16
- **Outlet:** 39.35, **730.80, 6,622,459, 246.42**
- **Heat Exchanged [MM BTU/h]:** 419.8
- **Cold Approach Temperature [ºF]:** 9.6
- **Hot Approach Temperature [ºF]:** 6.8
- **Cold Fluid pressure drop [psi]:** 73.97
- **Hot Fluid pressure drop [psi]:** 50.77
**Evaluation factor:** 20%
*Estimated values for RQF process. Once having a Vendor awarded, these values will be reviewed and agreed for the final contract issue.*
## 3. Noise warranty
The Supplier guarantees that the sound pressure level will not be higher than 85 dB(A) according to below conditions.
The A-weighted surface sound pressure level is averaged over the measurement surface according to ISO3746(2011).

# 2.3. Acceptance Test Procedure
The performance test procedure will be delivered by the Contractor 6 months before the Acceptance Test. Such procedure must be in compliance with a recognized standard and it must include the correction factors to be applied during the performance test, in case the four cases to be tested don't meet the exact conditions above referred (e.g. slight variations in cold and/or hot fluid mass flows with respects to the guaranteed cases). This procedure needs to be approved by Teyma USA & Abener Engineering and Construction Services General Partnership.

# 3. Availability warranty

The availability must be 99.9% and has to be guaranteed during 24 months, It will start after Practical Completion of the plant. The availability will be measured in periods of 12 months.
