It specifies pipe sizes, materials, fittings, flanges, bolting, gaskets, and valves with special considerations for cut groove piping and installation techniques.

## Material and Installation Standards
- **Service Range:** Welded Pipe - 150 PSIG @ 150°F, Cut Groove System - 125 PSIG @ 250°F
- **Construction:** CS pipe, screwed MI fittings, threaded flanges and valves
- **Nominal Pipe Size:** 1/2, 3/4, 1, 1 1/2, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48
- **Pipe CS ERW Schedule:** ASTM A-53 Grade “A” or “B”, Standard wt. CS A-53 Grade B, cut groove ends
- **Fittings:** MI CL 150, thread ASTM A-197, CS buttweld ASTM A-234 Grade WPB, standard weight couplings w/ bolts and nuts, Union MI CL 300, thread (all iron seat), Branch Conn. use fittings, Stub-in all branches 3” and larger within service range
- **Flanges:** CS CL 150 RF, threaded flanges ANSI B16.5, ASTM A-105, MSS-SP44 Class CL 150 RF slip-on, ASTM A-105
- **Bolting:** CS ASTM A-307 Grade “B” hex head bolts with heavy hex nuts
- **Gasket:** 1/8” thick ring type - Garlock Blue Gard Style 3000 or approved equal (EPDM as manufactured)
- **Valves:** Gate Valve FA7, Globe Valve FB7, Butterfly Valve DH45, DH46, Swing Check Valve FC6, Dble. Door Check CC42

## Special Considerations
1. Cut groove piping for sizes 3” thru 24” only, per Victualic Co., ITT-Grinnel Corp., Aeroquip Corporations’ methods.
2. All screwed connections with TFE thread seal tape by field.
3. Threadolet or stub-in preferred at tie-in locations.
4. Pipe spool may be required for valve operation.
