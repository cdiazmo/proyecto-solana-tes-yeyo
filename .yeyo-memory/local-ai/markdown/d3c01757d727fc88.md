## Project Summary
The Project is a concentrated solar power electric generating facility to be constructed on an approximately 1835-acre parcel of an existing 3000 acre tract in Maricopa County, Arizona. The Project is a 280 MW (gross) parabolic trough solar power plant with a net nominal electrical output of 250 MW.
## Scope of Work
This contract includes all pipe fabrication for the HTF piping and supporting systems. Vendor shall fabricate all pipe spools as shown on the piping isometric drawings as part of this contract and deliver to project jobsite.
## Items Furnished by Abener Teyma
All items listed on the drawing Bill of Material that are not indicated as shop furnished such as bolts, gaskets, and valves. All specialty items which include, but are not limited to strainers, flow meters, manual valves, relief valves and control valves. All pipe supports, except those supports that are welded to fabricated pipe spools and clearly noted on drawings.
## Vendor Responsibilities
Vendor Shall Comply with the Following:
- All welding shall be performed in accordance with ASME B 31.3 "Process Piping".
- Fluid Service Type ASME B 31.3 Section Reference

- **Socket Weld Joints**: Cannot be radiographed; all socket weld connections shall be examined by visual in-process examination.
- **Piping Material Specifications**: Vendor's responsibility to ensure material delivered meets piping specifications; any discrepancies must be reported immediately.
- **Cleaning**:
  - All pipe spools shall be supplied clean without excessive debris or rust.
  - Carbon steel pipe and fittings for specific systems (Molten Salt, Heat Transfer Fluid, Steam, Condensate, Closed Cooling Water, Boiler Feed Water, Lube Oil) must be internally cleaned per spec 4521-EPS-ANC-00-61-PS010.
  - Root pass welds for listed services must use Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG/Heliarc) welding process.
  - All pipe spools for listed services must adhere to spec 4521-EPS-ANC-00-61-PS010 prior to shipment.
- **Vendor Responsibilities**:
  - Shipping all fabricated spool pieces and loose pieces to the job site, taking precautions to avoid springing and misalignment of fabrications and damage to shop finish.
  - Identifying and locating Field Welds (FW) and Field Fit Welds (FFW).
  - Providing properly beveled ends for all FW joints.
  - Clearly marking or metal tagging each fabricated pipe spool or loose piece with a unique piece number.
  - Providing the engineering office with defined number of fabricated pipe spool drawing prints, including FW and FFW locations.
  - Removing all loose scale, rust, grease, and other foreign matter from piping material before welding; removing weld splatters, icicles, and other foreign matter after welding.
  - Cleaning and painting all exposed CS piping or pipe supports in accordance with Abencs' painting specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N180 prior to shipping if required. Piping that is insulated does not require painting.

##### Protection of Flanged Ends
All flanged ends of pipe spools must have a proper cover attached to bolt holes to protect the flange facings during shipment. Machined ends for field welds should be carefully capped with suitable caps securely anchored and taped in place.
##### Special Considerations
###### Carbon Steel Piping Systems
Victaulic couplings are required, and the Vendor shall provide necessary couplings and gaskets and groove pipe ends as detailed where grooved couplings are specified.
###### Stainless Steel Piping Systems
The Vendor shall furnish and install all carbon steel flanges at stub-end connections where applicable per piping specification or drawing.
###### Fabrication Tolerances
###### Scope
This specification shows tolerances for end preparation, dimensioning, and component alignment in the fabrication of metallic piping.
###### Reference Standards
The latest approved editions of the following standards apply, except as modified herein: Factory-Made Wrought Steel Building Fittings - ASME B16.9; Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings - ASME B16.5; ASME B16.47 Series A, only. Fabricating Tolerances - PFI ES-3.
###### Tolerances
(1) End Preparation: Pipe, fitting, and valve ends shall be prepared for welding as specified in the welding procedure specification. Preferred practice is shown in Figure 1.
(2) Linear Dimensions: Tolerances shown in Figure 2 apply to face-to-face, face-to-end, and end-to-end measurements of fabricated piping spools and to center-to-center, center-to-face, and center-to-end measurements of piping offsets, bands, nozzles, and support attachments.
(3) Angular Tolerances: The included angle of a pipe bend shall not deviate more than +0.5 degree from that specified.
(4) Exceptions: Closer tolerances shown on drawings supersede this specification.
