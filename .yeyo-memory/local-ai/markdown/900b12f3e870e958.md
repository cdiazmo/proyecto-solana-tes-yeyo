### Reinforcing Development Notes
1. Straight bars terminated at a joint shall pass through the confined core of a seismic column or boundary element.
2. Bar location: TOP BARS - HORIZONTAL BARS WITH MORE THAN 12" OF CONCRETE CAST BELOW THE BARS; OTHER BARS - ALL VERTICAL BARS AND HORIZONTAL BARS WITH LESS THAN 12" OF CONCRETE CAST BELOW BARS.
3. Development type: TYPICAL: D1- C ≥ 2db AND S ≥ 4db; D2 - ALL OTHER CONDITIONS; D3 - C < db OR S < 2db.
4. Seismic: Straight bars anchored in seismic frame column or shear wall boundary element.
5. Reinforcing grade: Reinforcing development length is for GRADE60 REINFORCING. For GRADE 75 REINFORCING, multiply the splice lengths in schedule by 1.25.
6. Concrete: Reinforcing development length schedule is for NORMAL WEIGHT CONCRETE. For LIGHT WEIGHT CONCRETE, multiply lengths in schedule by 1.3.
7. See "TYPICAL REINFORCING SPLICE SCHEDULE" for bar spacing information.

9. Lap splices shall be used only when it is not practical to use straight bars.
10. Exposed reinforcing dowels, left for future expansion, shall be protected for corrosion when future exceeds 60 days.
