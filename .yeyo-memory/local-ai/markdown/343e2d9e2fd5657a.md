## Resumen

- The document is for a Magnetic Particle Examination Procedure.
- It covers the method for detecting discontinuities in welds and vessel parts using the Yoke magnetizing method.
- References include ASME codes and standards.
- Responsibilities are assigned to Quality Manager and Qualified Personnel.
- Equipment requirements include AC yokes, UV-A lamps, and light meters.
- Wet particles should be applied before magnetizing current.
- Visible magnetic particle inspection requires a minimum light intensity of 1000 lux.
- Fluorescent particle inspections must be performed in a darkened area with specific UV-A lamp requirements.
- Field strength must be checked using a Berthol sample to ensure appropriateness for the exam.
- At least two magnetic fields should be applied on the same area following Table 1.
- Indications greater than 1.5 mm are considered relevant, except for specific types of cracks and rounded indications.
- Final reports must include job number, drawings, operator qualifications, and more.

## 1.0 Object
This procedure describes the method for detecting open and subsurface discontinuities in welds and vessel parts using the Yoke magnetizing method.

## 2.0 Scope
As specified in the I.P.P. or in the particular Inspection Program of the component.

## 3.0 References
- ASME Code, Section V – Non Destructive Examination (2015 Edition)
- ASME Code, Section VIII Div 1 y 2 – Pressure Vessels (2015 Edition)
- ASME Section I, Power Boilers (2015 Edition)
- ASME B 31.1 Power Piping (2014 Edition)
- WRITTEN PRACTICE ASME. QCM.PR05
- SNT-TC-1A (2006 Edition)

## 4.0 Responsibilities and Authority
### 4.1 Quality Manager
He has the authority to determine the items that must be checked using this procedure.
### 4.2 Qualified Personnel
The personnel who will perform the liquid penetrant examinations must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR05. In those cases where the tests are required to meet the EC Pressure Equipment Directive, personnel must be qualified as per ISO 9712/2012 (EN-473) specifies.

## 5.0 Equipment
- AC yoke that produce longitudinal magnetisation with a pull force of 4,5 kg at the maximum pole spacing used.
- UV-A lamp with a wavelength of 320-380 nm and intensity not lower than 1000 μW/cm² for fluorescent particles.
- Light meters calibrated at least once a year or whenever the meter has been repaired.

## 6.0 Magnetic Particle Products
### 6.1 Wet Particles
These particles shall be fluorescent to be examined with black light or non-fluorescent to be examined with white light, immediately before applying particles, the suspension shall be agitated to guarantee an uniform fluid dispersion.
- Ardrox 800/3. Black Particle.
- Ardrox 8901W: Lacquer for Contrast Enhancement when it is necessary.

### 6.2 Dry Particles
The operator must be sure that the powder used is adequate for the temperature of the piece and that it is dry enough.
- Ferromor ND-8 Yellow. Surface temperature limit: 200ºC.
- Ferromor ND-5 Grey. Surface temperature limit: 300ºC.

## 7.0 Description of the Process
### 7.1.1 Superficial Condition
Before the magnetic particle examination, a check of the examination surface shall be conducted to locate any discontinuity surface opening which may not attract and hold magnetic particles because of their width.

## WET PARTICLES
Wet particles shall be applied before magnetizing current is applied. If the wet particles are applied from aerosol spray cans, the magnetizing current may be applied before and/or after the particles have been applied.

**Visible Magnetic Particles**
- The minimum light intensity shall be 1000 lux on the surface to be examined.
- The light intensity shall be measured with a light meter prior to the evaluation of indications.
- If the contrast of the surface is not appropriate for the inspection, a Lacquer for Contrast Enhancement will be applied.
- Nonmagnetic surface contrast may be applied by the examiner to uncoated surfaces, only in amounts sufficient to enhance particle contrast. When nonmagnetic surface contrast enhancement is used, it shall be demonstrated that indications can be detected through the enhancement.

**Fluorescent Particles**
- It shall be performed in a darkened area (the visible light will be less than 20 lux).
- Operators shall be in a darkened area for at least 5 min prior to performing the examination to enable their eyes to adapt to dark viewing.
- UV-A lamp must be used with a wavelength of 320-380 nm and an intensity not lower than 1000 μW/cm². Reflectors and filters shall be checked and, if necessary, cleaned prior to use. Cracked or broken filters shall be replaced immediately.

## GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
Field strength shall be checked with a Berthol sample, having two lower cross section grooves. If two grooves are clearly seen, the magnetizing field shall be considered appropriate to perform the exam.
The exam will be made applying at least two magnetic fields on the same area following Table 1 to guarantee the 100% exam of the surface.

## EVALUATION
An indication is the evidence of a mechanical imperfection. Only indications with major dimensions greater than 1.5 mm shall be considered relevant.
- Linear indications are those having a length exceeding three times the width of the indication.
- Rounded indications are those which are circular or elliptical and have a length of less than three times the width of the indication.
- Any doubtful or questionable indication will be checked again to verify if it is relevant.
When two adjacent indications are separated by a lower distance than the bigger dimension of the smallest one, they should be evaluated as a single continuous indication.

## ACCEPT / REJECT CRITERIA
All surfaces to be examined shall be free of:
- Cracks like indications detected irrespective of surface conditions (ASME VIII Div 2 only)
- Relevant linear indications.
- Relevant rounded indications greater than 5 mm.
- Four or more relevant rounded indications in a line separated by 1,5 mm. or less (edge to edge).

According with ASME B31.1:
- Any cracks or linear indications
- Rounded indications greater than 5 mm
- Four or more relevant rounded indications in a line separated by 2 mm. or less (edge to edge)
- Ten or more rounded indications in any 3870mm² of surface with the major dimension of this area not to exceed 150mm with the area taken in the most unfavorable location relative to the indications being evaluated
Only indications with major dimensions greater than 1,5 mm shall be considered relevant.
