# 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001 Rev.5 Project Design Criteria

## Resumen

- The contract allows for alternate billing cycles.
- Site information includes location, elevation, and climate data.
- Structural design categories include wind and seismic factors.
- Wind load calculations are based on ASCE 7-05 standards.
- Earthquake loads are calculated using ASCE 7-05 guidelines.
- Project Design Criteria document number: 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001
- Site Class D with specific coefficients and spectral response acceleration coefficients
- Noise level requirement for individual equipment is 85 dBA SPL @ 3 ft.
- Cooling tower water temperatures are 82°F supply and 102.9°F return
- Mechanical and electrical equipment temperature ratings vary based on outdoor or indoor conditions
- Fire water available pressure: 125 psig
- Materials must be vendor standard, suitable for industrial environments, no asbestos allowed

## Structural Design Basis
### Occupancy Category for Wind and Seismic Importance Factors (ASCE 7-05 Table 1-1)
- Structures within the solar collector field: Category II
- Structures and buildings within the central plant power block: Category III
- Firewater storage tank and firewater pump building: Category IV

### Wind Load
- Basic Wind Speed: 90 MPH (Figure 6-1)
- Wind Exposure Category: C
- Wind Importance Factor:
  - IW = 1.00 for Category II (solar field)
  - IW = 1.15 for Category III (central plant)
  - IW = 1.15 for Category IV (firewater)
- Wind Directionality Factor:
  - KD = 0.85 for buildings and signs
  - KD = 0.95 for round chimneys and tanks
- Topographic Factor: KZT = 1.0
- Gust Effect Factor: G = 0.85 (for rigid structures)

### Earthquake Loads
- Seismic Importance Factor:
  - IE = 1.0 for Category II (solar field)
  - IE = 1.25 for Category III (central plant)
