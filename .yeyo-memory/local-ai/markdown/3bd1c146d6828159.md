## Scope
This plan provides general requirements for the organization of LOINTEK, S.L. and defines the responsibility and authority with organizational assignments to assure the scope of supply of ENGINEERING / OIL TO SALT HEAT EXCHANGERS.

## Customer Requirements
Technical and quality requirements are included in the following contractual documents:
- Order: 4500887352
- Shell & Tube HEX Technical Specification: 4521-SPE-SGT-039-0062-0001 Rev.1
- HTF-MS Heat Exchanger data Sheet: 4521-HDD-AEPC-075-062-0001 Rev 0
- Project Design Criteria: 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001
- Shell & Tube HEX Maximum Allowable Nozzle Loads: 4521-ESP-ANC-039-0062-0004

## Applicable Standards
Design and fabrication will be carried out according to the following Codes and Standards:
- Shell & Tube HEX Technical Specification: 4521-SPE-SGT-039-0062-0001 Rev.1
- ASME Code, Section VIII Div. 2 - Pressure Vessels
- ASME Code, Section II Material Specifications and Properties
- ASME Code, Section V Non-Destructive Testing
- ASME Code, Section IX Welding and Brazing Qualifications

## Organization
LOINTEK, S.L. planned the following organization for carrying out this order:
See Document: MC-SECC 04 Organization Chart (attached)

Each Manager may delegate the performance of any of his duties to personnel immediately under his authority. It is emphasized however, that the responsibility of these duties cannot be delegated and remains with the Manager himself.

A Manager may perform the duties of personnel under his responsibility if he is qualified for the duties.

The following clauses explain the duties of the people involved with the Quality Management System:
- General Manager: Overall responsibility for managing all activities at LOINTEK, S.L.
- Commercial Manager: Preparing quotations, contacting customers, marketing activities, receiving orders.
- Purchasing Manager: Marketing research, preparing Purchase Orders, distributing Purchase Orders.
- Industrial Manager: Managing manufacturing activities including supervision of departments.
- Engineering Manager: Development of drawings and calculations, approval of calculations, issuing drawings, purchasing materials.
- Production Manager: Scheduling, coordinating shop works, storage of materials, shipment of completed units or parts.
- Welding Engineer: Preparing WPS, conducting qualification tests, managing performance qualifications of welders.
- Project Manager: Coordinating departments, planning actualization, communicating with customers.
- Workshop Manager: Instructing and assigning qualified welders.
- Quality Manager: Preparation, approval, distribution, implementation, revision, control of the Quality Manual, implementing specifications, approving non-conformities, calibrating equipment, reviewing Material Test Reports.
