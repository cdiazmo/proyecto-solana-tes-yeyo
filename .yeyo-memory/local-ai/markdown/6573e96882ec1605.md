# 1950201-WPS-001 rev.0

## Resumen

- El documento es una especificación de proceso de soldadura (WPS) para el proyecto HEX replacement TES Solana.
- Se detallan los procedimientos de soldadura, incluyendo tipos de soldadura, temperaturas y tiempos de aplicación.
- Los materiales de aportación son especificados para cada proceso de soldadura.
- El documento está firmado por Marta Guerreiro y aprobado por TD-INS-SLN-010.
- Postweld heat treatment at 610ºC ± 10ºC for positions 2G (1st & 2nd GMAW) and 1G (SAW).
- Preheat temperature minimum of 100ºC with shielding gas SG-AC-18(GMAW) 18%CO2+82%Ar.
- Electrical characteristics include current, polarity, tungsten electrode size, and metal transfer mode for GMAW.
- Technique includes string or weave bead, contact tube to work distance, orifice or gas cup size, multiple passes per side, initial and interpass cleaning, single electrodes, method of back gouging, travel speed, oscillation, and peening.
- Weld layers include classes 1 and 2 with filler metals BOHLER NiMo 1-IG and OE-SD3 1Ni ½ Mo.
- Welding procedure specification includes GTAW + SAW processes for shell-tubesheet circumferential welds, tubesheet-channel circumferential welds, and channel-nozzle M joints.
- WPS document for L-1950201-02, revision 0, dated 07/01/2020.
- Post-weld heat treatment at 610ºC ± 10ºC for all positions (GTAW).
