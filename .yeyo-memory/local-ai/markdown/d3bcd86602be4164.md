## Project Description
1.1 The overall Solana Generating Station project consists of “greenfield” plant site near Gila Bend, Arizona. A new solar power generating plant will be built at the site. Vendor shall determine the optimum equipment required to meet the process requirements set forth in this specification. Vendor is to provide a quotation for equipment based on a 30 year life.

## Function
2.1 To maintain equipment integrity by providing sufficient fluid relief at the designated pressure levels.

## Operation
3.1 Safety Valves shall be designed to meet all design and climatic conditions in this Specification.
3.2 Vendor shall ensure that Set Pressure of safety valves does not vary with varying back pressure.

## Schedule
4.1 Short schedule duration has been projected for this project. Vendor shall propose the best available delivery dates.

## General
5.1 Valves shall be tested and pressure set by Manufacturer in accordance with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1, and shall be National Board certified.
5.2 Materials of construction shall be suitable for the specified service in the Vendor Data Sheets. No copper or copper-bearing alloys are permitted for items exposed to outdoor atmosphere, handling atmospheric air, or in contact with process streams (Heat Transfer Fluid (Therminol VP-1), steam, Cooling Water, Nitrogen). Vendor must identify all items made of copper and copper-bearing alloys within the proposal. The use of items and materials containing asbestos is not permitted.
5.3 See data sheet for relief valve requirements.

## Additional Requirements
6.1 Vendor shall complete the column titled “Vendor Supplied” of the attached Vendor Data Sheets and return with the proposal.
6.2 Comments and exceptions to this specification shall be listed in a separate part of the proposal titled “Exceptions to the Specification”. All exceptions shall be listed in this section. No other exceptions will be allowed.
6.3 Vendor shall provide a stainless steel tag with each valve as indicated in the Vendor Data Sheets.
