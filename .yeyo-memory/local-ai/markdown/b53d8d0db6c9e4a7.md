## Specifications
### Application
This specification is for demineralized water with less than 20 PPM chloride level.

### Service Range
150 PSIG @ 300°F (Max.)

### Construction
- **Pipe:** 304L SS, Schedule 80 per ASTM A-312, Grade TP304; Schedule 10S per ASTM A-312, Grade TP304L
- **Fittings:** CL 3000 SW, Type 304L SS per ASTM A-182, Grade F304L
- **Welds:** Buttwelding, Sch. 10S, Type 304L SS per ASTM A-403, Grade WP304L
- **Coupling:** CL 3000 SW, Type 304L SS per ASTM A-182, Grade F304L
- **Union:** CL 3000 SW, Type 304L SS with 304L SS nuts
- **Branch Conn.:** Use fittings; for reduction use inserts, tee, or weldolet/sockolet/stub-in with reinforcement pad as required per ANSI B31.3
- **Flanges:** CL 150 RF, SW, 304L SS, ASTM A-182, Grade F304L; CL 150 lap joint, CS, ASTM A-105, ANSI B16.5 (for Type A stub end)
- **Stub-ends:** Buttwelding, Schedule 10S, Type 304L SS per ASTM A-403, Grade WP304L, MSS Type A
- **Bolting:** Carbon steel machine bolts with hex heads and heavy hex nuts per ASTM A-307 Grade B
- **Gasket:** 1/8” thick Garlock Blue Gard No. 3000, ring type or approved equal

### Notes
1. All screwed connections at specialty items to be made up with TFE thread seal tape (by field).
2. TA1 (CL 150 screwed gate valve) may be used with Schedule 80 nipples for vents, PI connections, drains, and orifice taps (field fab.).
3. Use blind gasket at all blind flange locations.
4. Sockolets, weldolets, or stub-ins with reinforcement pad as required per ANSI B31.3 are preferred at tie-in locations regardless of size change due to difficulty of realignment of piping after cutting.
5. Pipe spool may be required to prevent interferences between disc and other specialty items upon operation of valve.
6. At a minimum, all shop and field root pass welds shall be done using Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG – Heliarc) welding process.
