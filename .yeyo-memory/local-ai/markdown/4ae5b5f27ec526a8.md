## 1. Document Purpose
This document is a technical note to estimate the corrosion loss of Lointek's salt to HTF heat exchangers' U-tubes due to contact with molten nitrate salts in Solana powerplant.

## 2. U-Tubes Design Conditions
- **Material**: SA210 A1 Seamless (general carbon steel)
- **Wall Thickness**: 1.651 mm / 0.065 in
- **Design Temperature**: 400 ºC / 750.2 ºF
- **Fluid**: Molten nitrate (60% NaNO3 – 40% KNO3)

## 3. Corrosion Data
Corrosion of carbon steel in molten nitrate salt is driven by chloride ions and temperature. A study by Sandia laboratory found that corrosion rate could increase by a factor of approximately x3.8 with a 50ºC increase of salt temperature.

## 4. Corrosion Loss Calculation
- **Assumptions**: Total lifetime = 25 years, design temperature correction factor = 1.52
- **Corrosion Rate**: 1.66±0.12 µm/year at 380ºC
- **Total Expected Corrosion Loss**: 63.08 µm (3.8% of initial thickness)

## 5. Conclusion
The total expected corrosion loss on Lointek's heat exchangers' U-tubes due to operation in Solana's molten nitrate salt is 63.08 µm.

5 Conclusion
The thickness loss by corrosion of the U-tubes due to molten salt mixture, expected after the whole lifetime of Lointek’s heat exchanger in Solana power plant have been estimated based on the most representative available literature. This thickness loss is 0.06308mm. This corrosion loss can be considered as low regarding the initial tube thickness (3.8%), which indicates that the design may be safe. Nevertheless, an additional structural calculation considering this corrosion loss should be performed by Lointek’s to guarantee the structural integrity of the tube after 25 years of operation.
