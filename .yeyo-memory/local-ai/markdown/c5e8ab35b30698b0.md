# 1950201-PPC-001 Rev00

## Resumen

- The document is a Quality Plan for the HEX replacement TES Solana project by LOINTEK S.L.
- It includes sections on scope, customer requirements, applicable standards, organization, communications, procedures, measuring equipment, documents, subvendors/suppliers, and non-conformities.
- The plan is prepared, reviewed, and approved by Paula Perez, Pedro Alvarez, and Pedro Alvarez respectively.
- Customer requirements are detailed in contractual documents such as the Order and Technical Specifications.
- Quality plan for LOINTEK's manufacturing process.
- Involves specifications, non-conformity resolution, equipment calibration, and document management.
- Communications are to be documented and confirmed by the project manager.
- General procedures outlined in Lointek S.L. Quality Management Manual.
- Specific procedures for fabrication and inspection detailed in attached documents.
- Measuring equipment calibrated according to LOINTEK's quality manual.
- Documents submitted using official templates and formats.
- Supplier control procedure described in the quality management manual.

## 1. - SCOPE
This plan provides general requirements for the organization of LOINTEK, S.L. and defines the responsibility and authority with the organizational assignments in order to assure the scope of supply of ENGINEERING / OIL TO SALT HEAT EXCHANGERS.

## 2. - CUSTOMER REQUIREMENTS
The technical and quality requirements are included in the following contractual documents:
- Order: 4500900424
- Shell & Tube HEX Technical Specification: 4521-SPE-SGT-039-0062-0001 Rev.1.
- HTF-MS Heat Exchanger data Sheet: 4521-HDD-AEPC-075-062-0001 Rev 0.
- Project Design Criteria: 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001.

- ASME Code, Section II Material Specifications and Properties.
- ASME Code, Section V Non-Destructive Testing.
- ASME Code, Section IX Welding and Brazing Qualifications.

## Objectives
- Implementation of specifications and procedures used to assure conformance of the product to the Code requirements.
- Concurrence and approval of the resolution of the non-conformities.
- Calibration of measuring and test equipment.
- Review and approval of Material Test Reports.
- Preparing, collecting and storing of Quality Control documents.
- Preparation and approval of the Inspection Points Program and its administration in the shop.
- Incoming material control and source control (when required).
- Performance of the required inspections, examinations and tests.
- Preparing “Non Conformance Reports”.
- Non Destructive Examinations and NDE personnel administration.
- Lointek’s NDE Personnel Certification.
