## 8 Implementation
### 8.1 Surface preparation and pre-cleaning
- Test parts must be cleaned from grease, oil or other contaminations with a solvent-based cleaner.
- All surfaces of the part must be dry when the penetrant is applied.

### 8.2 Applying of the penetrant
- The penetrant is applied by spraying or brushing.
- During dwell time, the penetrant must be liquid on all areas.
- A drying of the penetrant is not allowed.

### 8.3 Dwell time of penetrant
- Ranges from 10 to 30 minutes, depending on surface condition and material.
- Parts must be turned to avoid pooling of the penetrant.

### 8.4 Surface temperature
- Environmental and surface temperature range is between 10 and 50 °C.

### 8.5 Removing excess penetrant
- Clean with a jet of water (not sharp, max. 3 bar, temperature between 10 and 38°C) at a distance of min. 30 cm.
- Do not wash off the work piece with water.

### 8.6 Drying
- Perform immediately after cleaning the penetrant from the surface with a clean lint-free cloth at room temperature.

### 8.7 Developing
- Apply developer by spraying a thin and smooth film on the workpiece.
- Metallic bright on the surface must still be seen after spraying.
- Spray can must be shaken during spraying to mix the fluid in the Spray Can.
- If layer thickness is too big, all tested parts must be cleaned and tests redone.

### 8.8 Developer dwell time
- Starts immediately after drying and varies from 10 min to 30 min.
- Standards or other specifications can force special dwell times (e.g. > 30 min).

## 9 Inspection, Eye examination
- With a color contrast penetrant, the developer forms a reasonably uniform thin white coating.
- Surface discontinuities are indicated by bleed-out of the penetrant which is normally a deep red color that stains the developer.
- Indications with a light pink color may indicate excessive cleaning.
- Inadequate cleaning may leave an excessive background making cleaning and interpretation difficult.
- Minimum light intensity of 1000 lx (100 fc) required on the surface to be examined.

## 10 Documentation, Test Report
- Test result must be documented on the papers of the part and/or with a separate test report acc. EN 10204-3.1.
- Test report shall contain:
  - Designation (name, material, dimensions, cast#)
  - Surface preparation/condition
  - Link to this written procedure (No.: 3.00.4642-0613 En, Rev. 3) and applied specifications
  - Penetration System
  - Description of Implementation (see Sec. 8)
  - Minimum light intensity, test temperature
  - Indication (type of discontinuities detected, size, location)
  - Test location, date of testing
  - Performance demonstration, see Sec. 5.4, if required
  - Name and qualification of testing personnel

## 11 Post-Cleaning
- After penetration testing the workpiece will be cleaned with a jet of water and with a clean, lint-free and dry cloth.
