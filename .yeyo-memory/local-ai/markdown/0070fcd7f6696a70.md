## Specifications
### Application
This specification is intended for potable water, cooling water, hot water, process water, miscellaneous waters, chemical feed piping (feed water heater), non-acid drains from cold heat exchangers, blowers, etc., above-ground sanitary sewer.

### Cut Groove Piping
Cut groove piping (3” through 24” only) is intended to be used only in pipe racks or on other long straight runs where fittings and changes of direction are minimized. Cut groove piping will be used only as detailed on piping drawings (See Note 1).

### Service Range
- **Welded Piping:** 150 PSIG at 150 °F. Max., 125 PSIG at 325 °F. Max.
- **Cut Groove Sys.:** 125 PSIG at 250 °F. Max.

### Materials and Fittings
- **Pipe:** Galvanized Schedule 80 carbon steel pipe, ASTM A-53 Grade “A” or “B”, standard weight ERW carbon steel ASTM A-53 or API-5L Grade “A” or “B”, beveled ends; cut groove ends 3” through 24” only.
- **Fittings:** Galvanized MI screwed fittings, ASTM A-197, ASA B16.3 (See Note 3).
- **Coupling:** Same as fitting above, Cut groove standard coupling with bolts and nuts.
- **Union:** CL 300 galvanized MI brass ground seat.
- **Branch Conn.:** Use fittings. For full size through two sizes smaller use tee. For greater size change, use threadolet or weldolet as required.
- **Flanges:** Galvanized CL 150 RF screwed ANSI B16.5 forged carbon steel ASTM A-105; Carbon steel, CL 150 RF slip-on, ASTM A-105, ANSI B16.5 (use weld neck adjacent to buttweld fittings).
- **Bolting:** Carbon steel machine bolts with hex heads and heavy hex nuts per ASTM A-307 Grade B.
- **Gasket:** 1/8” thick Garlock Blue Gard No. 3000, ring type or approved equal (EPDM as mfg’d. For cut groove pipe couplings, 3” through 24”).

### Valves
- **Gate Valve:** FA7 - CL 800 screwed, EA41 - CL 125 FF flanged.
- **Ball Valve:** DF1 - CL 300 screwed, DF41 - CL 150 RF flange.
- **Globe Valve:** FB7 - CL 800 screwed, EB41 - CL 125 FF flanged.
- **Butterfly Valve:** DH45 - CL 150 flanged (insert type), DH46 - CL 150 FF (insert type).
- **Check Valve:** FC6 (inclined piston check) CL 800 screwed, EC41 (swing check) CL 125 FF flanged.
- **Dble. Door Check Valve:** CC43W - CL 125 wafer (See Note 2), CC42 – CL 150 wafer.

## Notes
1. See piping drawings for details on cut groove pipe usage.
2. Refer to specific valve types and their applications in the document.
3. Specifications may vary based on project requirements and site conditions.
