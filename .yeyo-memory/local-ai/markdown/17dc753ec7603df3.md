# Trunnion Receptacle Installation

## Notes:
1. All plate material shall be 1/2" THK ASTM A516-70.
2. All welding shall be FULL FILLET SEAL WELDS unless otherwise shown.
3. Trunnion receptacle bottom plate, side walls, and inside & outside walls to be SHOP FABRICATED IN TWO PIECE SECTIONS.
4. Trunnion receptacle to be provided by SALT TANK FABRICATOR.
5. Expanded clay material shall be supplied and installed by the FOUNDATION CONTRACTOR per SPECIFICATION 4521 ESP ANC 00 50 2060.

## Construction Sequence Notes:
A. Field weld the two halves of the trunnion receptacle together (BY SALT TANK FABRICATOR).
B. Place on level bed of expanded clay that is 8' —0 1/2" below bottom of tank floor (BY SALT TANK FABRICATOR).
C. Fill the voids between the inside and outside walls with expanded clay to the top (flush with the top of the side walls) (BY FOUNDATION CONTRACTOR).
D. Fill the void in the center with expanded clay up to the top (BY FOUNDATION CONTRACTOR). The trunnion will be pushed down into the clay (BY SALT TANK FABRICATOR).
E. Field weld all eight (8) "PIE" sections of the top ring plate onto the inside, outside, and side walls according to the details on this sheet (BY SALT TANK FABRICATOR).
F. Back fill around the trunnion receptacle with the rest of the subgrade as shown on detail 1/sheet 0008 (BY FOUNDATION CONTRACTOR).
