# 4521-ESP-AGE-39-70-0010 R2

## Resumen

- This document is an instrument specification for the Solana project.
- It defines roles such as Supplier, Customer, Fabricator, and Owner.
- The scope includes supply of instruments and related materials.
- Shipping schedule needs to be confirmed with purchasing during final commercial agreement.
- Confidential information must not be disclosed without permission.
- Alternate billing cycles are allowed by the contract.
- Vortex Flowmeter specifications for the Solana power plant.
- Min. Design Temperature: ANSI - 150 Schedule 80.
- Fluid: Nitrogen, Density: ASTM A-53.
- Electrical Connection: ½" NPT, Output Signal: 4...20 mA + HART.
- Power Supply: 24 Vdc, Hazardous Area Classification: NA.
- Calibration Certificate Required, Warranty for materials to be at least 1 year from plant operation start.
