## Description
This drawing covers the design and construction details of a hot salt tank for the Kaxu Solar One project. The tank is designed to hold up to 600 Tn of water with a capacity of 22,000 Tn when filled with operation fluid.

## Materials
- **Shell**: A516 Gr70
- **Heads**: A516 Gr70
- **Gaskets**: Graphite PSM-ASA
- **Internal**: A105A 106 Gr B
- **Flanges**: SA 335 P11
- **Bolts/Nuts**: SA 182 F11 - 150#
- **Tube**: API 650

## Connections and Details
- The tank includes various connections for salt inlet, level (radar), vent nitrogen, pressure, temperatures, and vent salt pumps.
- Reinforcing plates are used to support the roof and shell of the tank.
- Insulation is applied to the tank to maintain temperature stability.
