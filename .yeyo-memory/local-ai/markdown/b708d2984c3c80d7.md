# 4521-ESP-AGE-58-70-0001 R3

## Resumen

- This document is an engineering specification for the supply of instruments for the Solana project.
- The specification includes details on confidentiality, alternate billing cycles, and the scope of work.
- Supplier responsibilities include delivering materials to designated locations and providing a contact person for tracking.
- Exclusions from this subcontract are listed, including certain items not covered by this agreement.
- Engineering Specification for Solana power plant.
- Material must meet NEC, IEEE, UL standards.
- Vendor data required for project progress.
- Equipment to be delivered at purchaser's site.
- Strict delivery schedule by March 2012.
- Spare parts list with prices and delivery times needed.
- Inspection by Abener Teyma personnel before shipment.
- Alternate quotations invited for improvements.
