## Project Description
The overall Solana Generating Station project consists of “greenfield” plant site near Gila Bend, Arizona. A new solar power generating plant will be built at the site. Vendor is to determine the optimum equipment required to meet the process requirements set forth in this specification. Vendor is to provide a quotation for equipment based on the worst case conditions.

## General
1. **Goal**: The present specification proposal is to define the scope of work and general conditions for the design, fabrication and shipping of the nitrogen heater for the cold pump purge.
2. **Function**: The nitrogen electric heaters are installed to provide heat to nitrogen stream. Equipment must provide the necessary nitrogen heating for salt pump seal purge.
3. **Operation**: Continuous.
4. **Location**: The equipment will be installed outdoors on an elevated platform, with no weather protection. See attached specification 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001, Project Design Criteria, for site specific information.
5. **Equipment Numbers**: Equipment Tag Number Required Service
   - MH 255 6 Hot Salt Pump Purge

## Scope of the supply:
- Six (6) nitrogen electric heaters are required.
- Each electric nitrogen heater will operate independently.
- The Vendor shall furnish design, submittal data, fabrication, assembly, surface preparation and painting, inspection, testing, packing and shipping of the nitrogen heaters as specified in this document.
- The Vendor shall design the nitrogen heaters in accordance with the codes and standards identified in this specification and meet the operating conditions and performance requirements set forth in this specification.
- This specification, together with Data Sheets and other referenced attachments define the minimum requirements to be satisfied by the Vendor in the design and fabrication of the Nitrogen heaters and applicable accessories. The attachments identified in this document are considered part of this specification.
- The Vendor shall be responsible for all requirements delineated in this specification. All exceptions shall be noted in the Vendor’s technical proposal or quotation.
- After the purchase order is awarded, the Vendor shall be responsible to satisfy all requirements of this specification except for the agreed upon exceptions. Any requirements that are not included with the Vendor’s equipment and are in the equipment specification shall be furnished and installed at the Vendor’s expense.
- The Vendor shall determine the optimum nitrogen heater required to meet the process requirements set forth in this specification.
- The Vendor’s quotation shall include line item pricing for startup and commissioning spare parts with quantities of materials provided.
- The Vendor’s quotation shall include line item pricing for recommended spare parts for one (1) year of operation. Purchase of these spares is at the discretion of Owner / Operator of the Solar Power Plant.
## Design:
- The nitrogen heaters shall be designed to handle the nitrogen stream and to maintain the nitrogen heater discharge temperature.
- Nitrogen inlet and outlet temperatures are indicated on the Data Sheets.
- The nitrogen heater skids shall be assembled complete with, as a minimum, heater, piping, control / power panel, power and control conduit and wiring and interconnecting piping.
- The heater and the heater support structure design shall accommodate the thermal forces produced over the design range of the heater (ambient, heating).
- Vendor to size nitrogen heater based upon performance conditions specified in the data sheets accounting for all heat losses and system inefficiencies.
- The nitrogen heaters shall be thoroughly cleaned inside and outside and shall be free from grease, weld spatter, scale, slag, rust and any other foreign matter.
- Nitrogen heater maximum allowable pressure loss is indicated on the Data Sheets. Vendor shall optimize heater pressure loss.
- Nitrogen heater materials of construction are specified on the Data Sheets.
- Each heater skid shall have a minimum of four (4) lifting lugs.
