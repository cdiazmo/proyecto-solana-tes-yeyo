# Engineering Standards and Practices

## Material and Installation Standards
- **Document No:** 4521-ESP-ANC-68-64-V380
- **Job No:** 120006
- **Project:** Solana
- **Block Valves – Misc. for Solar Plants**

### Gate Valves
- **FA5, FA40, FA6, TA5:** Class 300 Buttweld, Std. Wt. Bore: 3" - 18", X-Stg. Bore: 20” – 36”, Steel gate, bolted or pressure seal bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, flexible graphite packing, spiral wound 304 SS/flexible graphite gasket.
- **FA6:** Class 600 Buttweld, Std. Wt. Bore: 12” – 18”, X-Stg. Bore: 20"-24", Sch 40 Bore, Wall Thickness Bore: .875” & 1.00”, Steel gate, bolted or pressure seal bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, flexible graphite packing, spiral wound 304 SS/flexible graphite gasket.

### Globe Valves
- **FB5, FB40:** Class 300 Buttweld, Std. Wt. Bore: 3" - 18", X-Stg. Bore: 20” – 36”, Steel globe, bolted or pressure seal bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, plug disc stellited seat and disc, flexible graphite packing.
- **FB6:** Class 600 Buttweld, Std. Wt. Bore: 12" – 18", X-Stg. Bore: 20"-24", Sch 40 Bore, Wall Thickness Bore: .875” & 1.00”, Steel globe, bolted or pressure seal bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, plug disc stellited seat and disc, flexible graphite packing.

### Check Valves
- **FC56:** Class 300 Buttweld, Std. Wt. Bore: 3" - 18", X-Stg. Bore: 20” – 36”, Steel swing check, bolted cap, Swing disc, stellited seat and disc.
- **FC39:** Class 600/800 Socketweld, Std. Wt. Bore: ½" – 2 ½", Steel piston check, inclined body, bolted bonnet or welded cover, guided disc, stellited seats (horizontal or vertical use).
- **FC66:** Class 600 Buttweld, Std. Wt. Bore: 3" - 18", X-Stg. Bore: 20” – 36”, Steel swing check, bolted cap, Swing disc, stellited seat and disc.

### Valves for MS (Nitrate Salt) Service
- **FF:** Class 300 Buttweld, Sch 80 Bore: 2", Std. Wt. Bore: 3" – 18", X-Stg. Bore: 20” – 30”, Steel gate, bolted and extended bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, flexible graphite packing, stellited gate and seats, primary stem seal: bellows seal (10,000 cycles), secondary stem seal: Flexita llic brand “Thermiculite” vermiculite mineral.
