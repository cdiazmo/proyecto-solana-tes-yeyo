# 1950201-PPS-001.00 Painting procedure

## Resumen

- The document is for the painting procedure of equipment at the SOLANA SOLAR POWER PLANT project.
- It includes surface preparation, painting system, and working conditions.
- The painting system uses inorganic zinc silicate with a total thickness of 75 µm.
- Surface preparation involves shot blasting to remove rust and cleaning.
- Working conditions include avoiding application under wet or dirty surfaces.
- Check surface cleanliness before painting.
- Clean residues using aspiration or blow if necessary.
- Apply paint immediately after cleaning to prevent rusting.
- Use paint in perfect conservation conditions and store appropriately.
- Follow manufacturer's application specifications.
- Paint should be applied uniformly, air-less, without sags.
- Do not apply abrasive blast-cleaning or paints under certain conditions.

## Surface Preparation / PREPARACIÓN DE SUPERFICIES
4.1. Before proceeding with shot blasting steel to remove rust, the steel shall be free of grease and other pollutants. Chemical waste will be removed by scrubbing with tepid water or pressure blast.
4.2. Rust will be removed from the entire carbon steel surface by abrasive shot blasting grade Sa 2 ½ as per ISO-8501-1.
4.3. The Grit-medium will be 38.1 to 63.5 µm (1.5 to 2 mil). The control will be made with M10-123 of ELCOMETER.
Antes de proceder a granallar el acero para eliminar el óxido, el acero debe está libre de grasa y otros contaminantes. También tienen que re tirarse los residuos químicos mediante el chorreado de agua tibia o bajo presión. El óxido será eliminado mediante granallado de grado Sa 2 ½ según la norma ISO-8501-1. El acabado medio será de entre 38.1 y 63.5 µm (de 1.5 a 2 mil). El control se realizará mediante el micrómetro M10-123 de ELCOMETER.
## Working Conditions for Surface Preparation / CONDICIONES DE TRABAJO PARA LA PREPARACIÓN DE SUPERFICIE
As a general rule, abrasive blast-cleaning and paints must not be applied if the following are true:
- Surfaces wet or dirty
- Imminent condensation
- Relative humidity > 85%
- The abrasive is wet or dirty
- When substrate temperature is <= 3ºC (37.4ºF) above the air's dew point
- Poor illumination
Como regla general, ni el granallado ni la pintura se pueden aplicar si se cumple lo siguiente:
- Superficies mojadas o sucias
- Condensación inminente
- Humedad relativa > 85%
- El abrasivo está mojado o sucio
- Cuando la temperatura del sustrato es <= 3ºC (37.4ºF) por encima del punto de rocío
- Poca iluminación

# PAINTING PROCEDURE
PROCEDIMIENTO DE PINTURA

**6. PAINTING / PINTADO**
- Check surface status before applying the first coat.
- Clean residues if necessary using aspiration or blow.
- Apply paint immediately after cleaning to prevent rusting.
- Use paint in perfect conservation conditions and store in recommended places.
- Follow manufacturer's application specifications.
- Apply paint uniformly, air-less (free of water-oil), without sags.

**7. WORKING CONDITIONS FOR PAINTING / CONDICIONES DE TRABAJO**
- Do not apply abrasive blast-cleaning or paints if surfaces are wet or dirty.
- Temperature range for painting: 4-43°C (39.2-109.4°F).
- Relative humidity should not exceed 90%.
- Product temperatures must be within 4-45°C (39.2-113°F).
- Avoid applying when product mix has surpassed its Pot-life.
- Poor illumination is also a contraindication.

**8. MINIMUM EQUIPMENT OF INSPECTION / INSTRUMENTACIÓN MINIMA**
- Ambient thermometer, contact thermometer, hygrometer.
- Wrinkled meter, rust measures for steel surfaces, adhesive band for dust presence test.
