## Resumen

- Company: RHEINHÜTTE Pumpen GmbH
- Customer: Rheinhuette Pumps, LLC - Arizona Solar One LLC
- Item No.: 6-MP-255 - Molten Salt Pump
- Pump Type: GVSO 80/250 A W103282 / 1803.1.409
- Operating temperature up to 399.0 °C
- Flow rate Qrated m³/h: 43.2
- Max power Pmax. (at nmax): 77.0 kW
- Lubrication interval: 3000 hours
- Pump type: A80/250, W103282, GVSO

- Motor from Siemens with IP 55 protection class
- Frequency converter and line filter included

## Observaciones

- Inexact, missing or wrong data can affect pump design and operation.
- Starting frequencies exceeding manufacturer's guidelines require consultation.
- Ensure the pump is suspended during the process and does not contact the floor.
- For immersion in hot molten salt, insert or draw out the pump slowly (max. speed: 0.05 m/min) for temperatures > 200°C.
- OCR errors in some text
- The mixability of different greases must be checked!

## General
### Instructions for using the Operating Instructions for pumps
#### Introduction
Rheinhütte pumps meet high demands in various applications. Proper use and handling are required. Personnel must read and understand the operating instructions, especially the "Safety" section.

Warranty is provided if used as intended. Incorrect operation or unauthorized repairs void warranty claims.

For technical advice, contact Customer Service or Spares Department.

#### Area of application and purpose of use
Pumps must be operated in specified areas. Compliance with limitations is mandatory. Warranty becomes invalid for non-compliance.

Operating instructions mainly refer to the pump itself; documentation on accessories and components should also be consulted.

Pictures are symbolic drawings.

### Copyright
Copyright remains with Rheinhütte Pumpen GmbH. Instructions and drawings must not be duplicated or published without permission.

## Safety
### General
These Operating Instructions contain fundamental safety instructions for installation, operation, and maintenance. Always read before assembly and commissioning by fitters and specialists/operators.

Safety instructions must be observed, including special symbols and warnings.

Instructions on the machine must be obeyed and maintained in readable condition.

### Qualification and Training of Personnel
Operational personnel must have correct qualifications. User should ensure full understanding of Operating Instructions.

Requirements for authorized personnel are regulated by TRBS 1203, among others.

### Dangers from non-compliance with the safety instructions
Non-compliance can endanger personnel, environment, and machine. It can lead to loss of claims for damages.

Potential risks include failure of important functions, incorrect maintenance methods, electrical, mechanical, chemical risks, and environmental hazards due to hazardous material escape.

### Safety-conscious working
Adhere to safety instructions, national regulations, and user's internal working, operating, and safety regulations.

# Safety Instructions for the User/Operator
- Hot or cold parts must be protected against being touched.
- Anti-contact guards must not be removed during operation.
- Hazardous media leaks must be removed safely.
- Electricity risks must be prevented.
# Safety Instructions for Maintenance, Inspection and Fitting Operations
- All work must be done by authorized personnel.
- Machinery should only be worked on when stopped.
- Pumps handling hazardous media must be decontaminated.
- Safety equipment must be replaced after work.
# Unauthorised Modification and Manufacture of Spares
- Modifications require discussion with the manufacturer.
- Use genuine spare parts for safety.
# Prohibited Methods of Operation
- Machine operation must follow specifications.
- Data sheet limits must not be exceeded.
# Transport and Storage
- General safety rules apply during transport and storage.
- Care must be taken when handling pumps.
- Packaging should remain intact during storage.
- Pumps should be set down gently on level ground.

# Transporting the Pump/Assembly
If the pump is removed from its transport protection, it should be transported in accordance with the following illustrations. In the case of pumps with long immersion depths (from approx. 6m onwards), a special drawing may be made for the erecting, tilting, assembly and dismantling, which is appended after the dimensional arrangement drawing of the documentation.

All the warning instructions given so far are to be complied with! Before lifting the pump or assembly, the centre of gravity must always be determined as closely as possible!
During the entire procedure of transporting the pump / the aggregate (incl. setting up and lying down) care must be taken, that any damages on pump / aggregate, caused by rope or chain, are avoided.
Personnel must be kept out of the danger area!
The user must take all necessary measures to protect people, the environment and the product!

## Column A - Correct choice of transport;
- Suspend the pump from the hoist using eye-bolts and chain (depending on the pump size 2 or 4 eye-bolts or holes are provided)!
- Raising the complete aggregate „pump / motor“ into a vertical position and following transport is possible as well, if using a proper crane-traverse, which should be designed to avoid any damages on pump and motor too.

## Storage
Please note that all the instructions on storage refer only to the pump! The instructions regarding the storage of other purchased parts (e.g. motor, mechanical seals, thermo-siphon equipment, etc.) must also be complied with!
### Requirements for the storage area:
- The pumps must be stored in a frost free place.
- The storage area must be roofed over, dry and safe against flooding.
- With closed rooms care should be taken to ensure that they are well ventilated.
- „Pump / aggregate“ must be protected against impact and shock loads, e.g.caused by objects, tumbling down.
### Storage and Maintenance Instructions:
- The storage-position is optimally, if corresponding to installation for operation; that means vertically and adequately supported at the sole plate of the delivery elbow. If vertically storage is impossible, the pumps must be stored in the horizontal position (lying down) and adequately supported. This means that pumps with a long submersion depth must be supported every 2m to prevent them from sagging.
- The pumps must also be adequately fixed in their location
- All openings on the pump must be covered so that neither dust, water nor dirt can enter.
- On metal pumps or plastic pumps having metal components in the hydraulic section of the pump (e.g. impeller, volute casing, suction cover etc.) the following additional maintenance instructions must also be complied with.
  - Remove the covers from the suction and discharge nozzles every 4 - 8 weeks and check the pump visually for corrosion damage.
  - Smear the sealing faces of the suction and discharge nozzles with a suitable preservative.

#### General:
The RHEINHÜTTE vertical centrifugal pump type GVSO generally consists of a submerged tank pump (dry installation is possible to a limited extent. Please consult RHEINHÜTTE). The pump casing with double volute is normally (for wet installation) submerged in the liquid to be handled and is connected to the mounting flange by the suspension pipe. The pump is driven by a vertical flanged motor mounted on top of the motor stool.
Depending on the pump size, the shaft is supported by one or more intermediate bearings (depending on the depth of submersion) for submersion depths greater than approx. 2200 mm. The column and suspension pipes consist of the discharge pipe (for the medium) and the shaft column. This means that the rotating shaft is not exposed to the flow.
There are overflow holes in the upper part of the delivery suspension pipe which prevent the medium rising through the sleeve bearing gap from reaching the shaft entry.
The overflow holes in the delivery suspension pipe should be above the level of the liquid in the tank at all times and be kept open!
The shaft entry does not come into contact with the pumped medium.
The support bearings in the volute and stage casing (for multi-stage pumps) and the intermediate bearings are lubricated by the pumped medium, provided this is free from solid particles.
In this version (“Molten Salt” - version), the machine is not approved for use in gas-explosive atmospheres or dust-explosive atmospheres!

Each pump unit features a rating plate on the bearing bracket. The customer is advised to note the sales order number (R..) and serial number (P..) and to quote these in relation to all enquiries and when ordering spare parts from Rheinhütte.
These numbers must be quoted in all correspondence and all orders for spare parts.
#### Design features:
The design depicted is explained in the Technical Data Sheet describing the pump in detail.
##### Shaft seal options:
In the case of molten salts, depending on the requirement, the following shaft sealing models come into the reckoning:
- **Model A with stuffing box**: This type of sealing is used in pumps that are built in tanks, in the case of which there is a small differential pressure to the atmosphere, as well as for deployment against gasifying and fuming media. Between the packing rings, there is a locking ring, which can be charged, depending on the requirement, with compressed air or nitrogen (20 – 50 mbarg). The consumption is 60 – 80 Nl (standard litres) / min
- **Design Y**: The shaft opening is sealed by one or several lip rings or by a pressurized carbon floating ring seal. This design can only be used if the shaft guide pipe is not subject to pressure. With a carbon floating ring seal, container pressures up to max. 4 bar can be sealed by air or nitrogen
##### Direction of rotation:
The direction of rotation of the GVSO is anti-clockwise (to the left) when viewed from the drive end shaft stub. Exceptions are the pump sizes 200/400, 250/400 300/400, 400/500 und 450/500 which rotate in a clockwise direction (to the right)

Depending on the load (i.e. depending on the size and speed), the shaft is supported by one or two radial bearings in the thrust bearing lantern in the case of GVSO pumps.

Pumps with intermediate bearings have a split shaft. The joint of the shaft units and the transmission of the torque are achieved by means of a sleeve coupling which also acts as a shaft sleeve in the intermediate bearings.

The flange size indicated on the dimensional drawings (installation drawing) corresponds to the inside diameter.
Corrosion resistant pumps usually have a plated mounting flange.
##### Foot bearing, intermediate bearing:
In case of molten salts, the lubrication of the sleeve bearing is done by the transported material. Lubricant is applied via holes from the pressure pipe and the suspension tube filled with conveyed medium.
##### Design for installation in tanks:
In this design the suction flange on the pump is not drilled. In the suspension pipe there are overflow holes through which the rising liquid drains back into the tank.

A bolt is correctly tightened when it has been strained below the yield point, causing pre-tension force that presses the bolted components together. # Tightening Torques for Stretch Bolts

## Introduction
Normal bolts subjected to alternating loads will break due to fatigue, while stretch bolts are better suited for such conditions. Their shank diameter is about 90% of the thread root diameter, allowing them to withstand higher loads.

## Correct Tightening
Bolts should be tightened evenly within the elastic limit using a torque wrench until they reach the specified torque loading. This ensures that the bolt has been strained below its yield point, maintaining proper pre-tension force.

## Pre-Tension Force and Torque Loading
The table provides values for different bolt classes (8.8, 10.9, 12.9) and thread cross-sections, specifying maximum pre-tension in KN and maximum torque loading in Nm. These values are calculated to ensure that the tensile stress in the cross-sectional area of the stretch shank and torsional stress during tightening reach 90% of the 0.2% proof stress.

1. Block off all supply lines and measurement devices.

3. Remove motor sun shade if present.

5. Insert pre-assembled thrust bearing ring into bearing housing (J4).
6. Insert key into shaft and insert bearing housing over shaft into thrust bearing lantern (K).
7. Block shaft against rotation, screw on and tighten shaft nut, and secure it.

# Metastream™ TSKS Kupplung

## Einbau-
Nach dem Entfernen der Verpackung ist die Kupplung sorgfältig auf Beschädigungen zu überprüfen. Achten Sie hierbei besonders auf die Nabenbohrungen und Anschlußzentrierungen, die weder Grat noch Beschädigungen aufweisen dürfen.

## Nabenmontage
Arbeiten an der Kupplung dürfen nur im Stillstand ausgeführt werden. Das Antriebsaggregat muss gegen unbeabsichtigtes Einschalten gesichert werden. Die Naben sind während der Montage ausreichend gegen Herabfallen zu sichern.

Stellen Sie sicher, daß die Wellenenden und Bohrungen der Kupplungsnaben sauber sind.

### Zylindrische Bohrung mit Paßfeder
1. Überprüfen Sie Wellendurchmesser und Nabenbohrung auf die korrekte Passung.
2. Montieren Sie die Paßfeder(n) in die entsprechende Wellennut und ziehen, bei Vorliegen einer Spielpassung, die Kupplungsnabe auf die leicht geschmierte Welle auf. Die Paßfeder sollte hierbei fest in der Wellennut sitzen und ausreichend Rückenspiel aufweisen. Richten Sie die Innenseite der Kupplungsnabe bündig mit dem Wellenende aus. Die axiale Sicherung der Kupplungsnabe erfolgt in der Regel durch einen Gewindestift auf der Paßfeder.
3. Liegt eine Übergangs- oder Presspassung vor, kann ein Erwärmen der Kupplungsnabe notwendig sein. Ein warmes Ölbad ist hierbei normalerweise ausreichend. Vermeiden Sie eine punktuelle Erwärmung bzw. Temperaturen größer 175° C, da es hierdurch zu Verformungen kommen kann!
Richten Sie die Nabe wie zuvor beschrieben aus.

### Kegelbohrung mit Paßfeder ABB. 2
1. Die Kupplungsnabe ohne Paßfeder auf die Welle schieben und mit geeignetem Werkzeug auf den Wellenkegel pressen.
2. Mit einem Tiefenmikrometer den Abstand vom Wellenende bis zur Nabeninnenseite messen und mit dem entsprechenden Sollmaß in der Ausführungszeichnung abgleichen.
3. Wenn nötig, die Nabe entfernen und die Paßfeder(n) einsetzen. Die Paßfeder sollte hierbei fest in der Wellennut sitzen und ausreichend Rückenspiel aufweisen.
4. Nach dem Einsetzen der Paßfeder(n), die Nabe wieder soweit auf die Welle schieben bis der zuvor gemessene Abstand zwischen Wellenende und Nabeninnenseite erreicht ist. Siehe Abb. 2. Beachten Sie bitte, daß bei kegeligen Bohrungen Nabeninnenseite und Wellenende nicht bündig sind.

### Naben mit Ölpressverband
1. Stellen Sie sicher, daß Nuten und Radien der Paßflächen, die Ölverteilung und Ablaufrinnen gut abgerundet sind und keinen Grat aufweisen.
2. Alle Kontaktflächen sind zuvor gründlich zu säubern und die kegligen Flächen mit Öl zu schmieren.
3. Schieben Sie die Nabe auf die Welle.
4. Montieren Sie die Ölpumpe und das entsprechende Montagezubehör gemäß Ausführungszeichnung. Beachten Sie bei der Montage die Anweisungen des entsprechenden Herstellers.

Die Hydraulikmutter befestigen und sichern, BEVOR Öl eingespritzt wird!

5. Öl einspritzen, bis der gewünschte Druck erreicht ist oder Öl zwischen den Kontaktflächen austritt.
6. Mit Hilfe der Montagewerkzeuge die Nabe in die richtige, axiale Position bringen. Hierbei weiterhin Öl einspritzen.
7. Öldruck ablassen.
8. Ölpumpe und Montagezubehör entfernen.
9. Wenn vorgesehen, Sicherungsscheibe und Sicherungsmutter am Wellenende montieren.

## Wellenausrichtung
Die Achsen der treibenden und der getriebenen Wellen wie folgt ausrichten:
1. Antriebsaggregat und Arbeitsmaschine aufstellen.
2. Beide Maschinen grob ausrichten und anschließend die Arbeitsmaschine fixieren.
3. Den Abstand zwischen den Wellenenden (DBSE) durch Verschieben des Antriebsaggregats nach Zeichnung herstellen.

WICHTIG! Für die Ausrichtung muss der Abstand zwischen den Innenflächen der Naben gemessen werden. Weiterhin kann, z.B. bei kegeligen Bohrungen, dieses Abstandsmaß von dem eigentlichen Wellenabstand abweichen. Siehe Abb. 4.

Dieses Verfahren kann mitverschiedenen Meßinstrumenten durchgeführt werden. Je genauer die Ausrichtung der Maschinen erfolgt, umso größer ist die Lebensdauer der Kupplung. Wir empfehlen daher die Verwendung von einem lasergestützten Meßsystem, z.B. das John Crane Wellenausrichtungssystem "Lase-A-Lign".
5. Nach dem Ausrichten der Maschinen, ist der Abstand zwischen den Nabenflanschen nochmals zu kontrollieren und gegebenenfalls korrekt einzustellen.
6. Bei der Verwendung von Kegelverbindungen kann es zu Schwierigkeiten beim Einstellen des genauen Wellenabstandes kommen. In diesem Fall können auf Anfrage axiale Ausgleichsbleche geliefert werden. Die notwendige Stärke der Ausgleichsbleche kann wie folgt ermittelt werden. [Gesamtstärke der Ausgleichsbleche] = [Abstand zwischen den Nabenflanschen] - [Gesamtlänge der Transmissionseinheit *]

## Testing Time and Extent
- **Time of Testing**: Defined by component design, fabricating steps, standards, or customer requirements.
- **Extent of Testing**: Defined by design, standards, or customer requirements.

## Implementation
### Surface Preparation and Pre-Cleaning
- Test parts must be cleaned from grease, oil, or other contaminants with a solvent-based cleaner.
- All surfaces must be dry when penetrant is applied.

### Applying the Penetrant
- Apply by spraying or brushing.
- Penetrant must be liquid on all areas during dwell time; drying not allowed.

### Dwell Time of Penetrant
- Ranges from 10 to 30 minutes, depending on surface condition and material.
- Parts must be turned to avoid pooling.

### Surface Temperature
- Environmental and surface temperature range: 10 to 50 °C.

### Removing Excess Penetrant
- Clean with a jet of water (not sharp, max. 3 bar, temp. 10-38°C), distance min. 30 cm.
- Do not wash off the work piece with water.

### Drying
- Dry immediately after cleaning with a clean lint-free cloth at room temperature.

### Developing
- Apply developer by spraying a thin and smooth film on the workpiece.
- Metallic bright spots must still be visible; spray can to be shaken during application.
- Layer thickness too big requires cleaning and redoing tests.

### Developer Dwell Time
- Starts immediately after drying, varies from 10 min to 30 min. Special dwell times possible (e.g., > 30 min).

## Inspection and Eye Examination
- Developer forms a reasonably uniform thin white coating.
- Surface discontinuities indicated by bleed-out of penetrant (deep red color).
- Light pink color may indicate excessive cleaning.
- Minimum light intensity: 1000 lx (100 fc) required for adequate sensitivity.

## Evaluation of Indications
- Evaluate during developer dwell time with conditions of sec. 8.3.
- Acceptable indications based on specifications; unacceptable require retest and decision by level II examiner.

## Documentation, Test Report
- Document test results on part papers or separate report acc. EN 10204-3.1.
- Report must include: designation, surface preparation/condition, penetration system, implementation description, minimum light intensity, test temperature, indications, test location/date, performance demonstration (if required), and testing personnel name and qualification.

## Post-Cleaning
- Clean workpiece with jet of water and clean, lint-free cloth after penetration testing.
