# 4521-HDD-AEE-39-70-0016-GAR Rev.01 Pneumatic automatic valves

## Resumen

- Document pertains to pneumatic automatic valves for the Solana project.
- Factory tests are mandatory as marked in the document.
- Delivery includes final quality dossier, design, and manufacturing dossiers.
- Inspections include hydrostatic pressure test, non-destructive testing, and tightness leakage.
- Document number: 4521-HDD-AEE-39-70-0016-GAR_Rev.01
- Valves for pneumatic automatic control with specifications on flow rate, pressure, and temperature
- Materials used include stainless steel, carbon steel, and flexible graphite gaskets
- Options include limit switches and a hand wheel
- Actuator orientation is vertical up and instrument air supply pressure ranges from 3 to 15 psig
- Document contains specifications for pneumatic automatic valves.
- Valves are designed for molten salt and thermal oil applications.
- Hand wheel operation, 1/2" NPT connection, and 24 Vdc power supply.

# Pneumatic Automatic Valves

## Document Information
- **Document No.**: Not specified
- **Sheet**: 22 and 23
- **Rev. Date**: Mar/28/2017

## Specifications
### General
- **Fluid**: Thermal Oil (Liquid) / Nitrogen (gas)
- **Service Frequency**: Once per month / Once a year
- **Electrical Classification**: Class 1, Division 2, Group D

### Design Parameters
- **Design Pressure**: 50 psig
- **Design Temperature**: 750 ºF
- **Body/ Bonnet Material**: A216 Gr. WCB Carbon steel / ASTM A-234 Grade WPB
- **Insulation Thickness**: 3" / 4"

### Valve Characteristics
- **Type**: Pneumatic automatic valves
- **Connection**: 1/2" NPT / 3" BW
- **Power Supply**: 24 Vdc (Low Consumption)
- **Actuator Type**: Diaphragm Simple Acting
- **Shut-off Pressure**: 50 psig
- **Control Action**: On-Off
- **Inherent Valve Characteristic**: Quick Opening
- **Leakage Level Class**: V

### Materials
- **Plug/ Seat**: 13 Cr + Stellite / 13 Cr + Stellite
- **Cage/ Stem**: 13 Cr / 13 Cr
- **Gasket**: Flexible graphite / Thermiculite
- **Packing Type/ Material**: Standard Flexible graphite / Standard Thermiculite

### Options and Features
- **Limit Switches**: SPDT 0.5 A (125 Vcc)
- **Hand Wheel**: Yes
- **Positioner**: N/A
- **Air Set**: Air Filter Regulator and Gauges / 1/4" NPTF
- **Bonnet Type**: Welded / Flanged Extension Bonnet
- **Inlet/ Outlet End Connection**: 1" SW / 3" BW

### Process Data
- **Flow Rate**: Not specified
- **Inlet Pressure**: Not specified
- **Pressure Drop (∆P)**: Not specified
- **Temperature**: Not specified

## Notes
- The valve must be compatible with molten salts.
- Process data fluid according to nitrogen flow.
