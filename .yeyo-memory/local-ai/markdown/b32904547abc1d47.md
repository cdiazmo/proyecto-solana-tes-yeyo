- All critical dimensions inspected.

## Reinforced Concrete Special Inspection
- Witnessed concrete pouring.
- Slump test performed; all values < 8 inches.
- Samples sent for third-party testing (Speedie and Associates).
- OSHA best practices observed during pouring.
- Weather conditions normal, no high winds recorded.
- Excessive overnight runoff caused issues on F26 and F18B footings.
- Postponed pouring due to plastic clay near rebar cage.
- Cage lifting and removal of plastic clay recommended.
