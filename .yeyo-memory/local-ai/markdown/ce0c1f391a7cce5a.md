# 4521-ESP-ANC-68-64-V380

## Resumen

- Document pertains to engineering standards and practices for material and installation in solar plants.
- Includes specifications for gate valves, globe valves, check valves, and nitrate salt service valves.
- Gate valves are available in Class 300 Buttweld with various bore sizes.
- Globe valves are also available in Class 300 Buttweld with varying bore sizes.
- Check valves include swing and piston types for different applications.
- Nitrate salt service valves feature stellited gate and seats with specific sealing requirements.

**EC-94 – Solana Specific Standard Form (Rev. 08/2010):**

**Valves for MS (nitrate salt) service:**

**TA58V TA58V TA58V TA58V**
- **Class:** Class 300 Buttweld
- **Bore Sizes:** 2” (Sch 80), 3” – 18” (Std. Wt.)
- **Material:** 316 Stainless steel gate, bolted and extended bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, 13 CR flexible or solid wedge disc, stellited gate and seats
- **Primary Stem Seal:** Bellows seal, replaceable with valve in position and with a minimum fatigue life of 10,000 cycles
- **Secondary Stem Seal:** Flexitallic brand “Thermiculite” processed vermiculite mineral

**Globe Valves:*

**FB58V FB58V FB58V FB58V**
- **Class:** Class 300 Buttweld
- **Bore Sizes:** 2” (Sch 80), 3” – 18” (Std. Wt.), 20” – 30” (X-Stg.)
- **Material:** Steel globe, bolted and extended bonnet, OS & Y, rising stem, 13 CR Plug disc, stellited seat and disc
- **Primary Stem Seal:** Bellows seal, replaceable with valve in position and with a minimum fatigue life of 10,000 cycles
- **Secondary Stem Seal:** Alternating layers of Teflon washers and graphite impregnated stainless steel braid, stem length Flexitallic brand “Thermiculite” processed vermiculite mineral
