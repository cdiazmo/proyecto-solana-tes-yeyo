# 19500201-INF-008 Monthly Report - 2020 October

## Resumen

- Monthly report for LOINTEK's activities until October 16, 2020.
- Project involves HEX replacement TES Solana at Arizona Solar One.
- Manufacturing progress of equipment units varies from 60% to 93%.
- Open issue due to Force Majeure Situation COVID-19.
- Invoices include advance payment, material arrival, inspection, and practical completion.
- Manufacturing activities from January to December 2020
- Multiple units (1 to 6) with various stages of assembly and testing
- Final works, PWHT, NDTs, helium tests, and hydrostatic tests conducted throughout the year
- Delivery scheduled for October 2020
- Project: SOLANA SOLAR POWER PLANT in Arizona, USA.
- Documents include progress reports, manufacturing plans, and inspection procedures.
- Purchase orders for tubes, tubesheets, and plates from suppliers like Taewoong and Voest Alpine.

## Observaciones
