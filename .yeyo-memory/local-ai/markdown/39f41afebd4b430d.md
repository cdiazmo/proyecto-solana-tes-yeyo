## Specifications
### Application
This specification is for chemical feed piping (ferric chloride to Clarifier) in Hastelloy-C276 or Hastelloy-C22 materials.

### Service Range
- **Pressure / Temperature:** 30 PSIG / 125 °F

### Construction
- **Pipe Material:** Hastelloy-C276 or -C22, Schedule 80 (PE), fusion welded per UNS ASTM N10276, B-474, B-626, B-619.
- **Fittings:** Buttweld Hastelloy-C276 or -C22, Wall Thickness to match ANSI B16.9.
- **Branch Connections:** Use fittings; for reduction use insert Stub End Type “A” Hastelloy-C276 or -C22.
- **Flanges:** Weld Neck RF CL150 per ASTM B-462 ANSI B16.5 (use weld neck flange on BW fittings) or use CL150 CS A105 backup flange with stub-end.

### Bolting
- **Bolts:** Alloy steel stud bolts ASTM A-193 Grade B7, heavy hex nuts ASTM A-194 Grade 2H.

### Gaskets
- **Gasket Material:** 1/8” Thick Garlock Gylon 3546 PTFE Class 150 or approved equal.

### Valves
- **Gate Valve:** NA41 – CL150 Gate RF Flanged, NA33 – CL150 Gate SW.
- **Globe Valve:** NB42 – CL150 Globe RF Flanged.
- **Check Valve:** NC40 – CL150 Check RF Flanged.

### Notes
1. **Welding Electrodes:** Bare – ER Ni Cr Mo-4, UNS W80276.
2. **Socketweld Gate Valves:** For low point drains or high point vents only if required.
3. **Sockets:** Preferred for tie-in locations due to difficulty in realignment after cutting.
