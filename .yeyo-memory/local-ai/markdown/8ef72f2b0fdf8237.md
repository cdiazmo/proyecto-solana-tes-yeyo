# Operational Limits

## Aim
The purpose of this document is to define the Operational limits of the Oil to Salt Heat Exchanger of Solana Thermosolar Power Plant (Arizona, USA).

## Design Condition
**Oil side (tube side):**
- Temperature: 399 ºC 750.2 ºF
- Pressure: 22 & FV barg 320 & FV psig

**Salt side (shell side):**
- Temperature: 399 ºC 750.2 ºF
- Pressure: 22 & FV barg 320 & FV psig

## Variation Ratio in Operation Conditions
- **Thermal shock:** +/- 126 ºF (70 ºC)
- **Maximum heating/cooling ramp:** 18 ºF/min (10 ºC/min)

## Operation Area
**A. Temperatures:**
- **No flow of two fluids:** |ΔT| < 126 ºF
- **Prolonged stand-by:** Max molten salts flow ≤ 5% design flow
- **Flow of one fluid:** |T1 – T4| < 216 ºF
- **Both fluids in circulation:** Operation temperature between 500 ºF (260 ºC) and 752 ºF (400 ºC)

**B. Mass flow:**
- HTF flow: > 435.600 lb/hr (55 kg/s)
- SALT flow: > 674.600 lb/hr (85 kg/s)
- Max SALT mass flow: 7.284.705 lb/hr (920 kg/s)
- Max HTF mass flow: 4.516.173 lb/hr (570 kg/s)

## Others
- **Drain with N2:** No special temperature limitation except for the 18 ºF/min (10 ºC/min) temperature ramp.
- **Pressure differences between fluids:** No limitation

The above mentioned limitations will also apply for temperature limits for initial filling, cold start and heat tracing heating ramp.
