## 1. - SCOPE
This plan provides general requirements for the organization of LOINTEK, S.L. and defines the responsibility and authority with the organizational assignments in order to assure the scope of supply of ENGINEERING / OIL TO SALT HEAT EXCHANGERS.

## 2. - CUSTOMER REQUIREMENTS
The technical and quality requirements are included in the following contractual documents:
- Order: 4500865375.
- Shell & Tube HEX Technical Specification : 4521-SPE-SGT-039-0062-0001 Rev.1
- HTF-MS Heat Exchanger data Sheet: 4521-HDD-AEPC-075-062-0001 Rev 0
- Project Design Criteria: 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0001
- Shell & Tube HEX Maximum Allowable Nozzle Loads: 4521-ESP-ANC-039-0062-0004

## 3. - APPLICABLE STANDARDS
The design and fabrication will be carried out according with the following Codes and Standards:
- Shell & Tube HEX Technical Specification : 4521-SPE-SGT-039-0062-0001 Rev.1
- ASME Code, Section VIII Div. 2 - Pressure Vessels
- ASME Code, Section II Material Specificarions and Properties
- ASME Code, Section V Non-Destructive Testing
- ASME Code, Section IX Welding and Brazing Qualifications

## 4. - ORGANIZATION
LOINTEK, S.L. planned the following organization for carrying out this order:
See Document :MC-SECC 04 Organization Chart (attached)

Each Manager may delegate the performance of any of his duties to personnel immediately under his authority. It is emphasized however, that the responsibility of these duties cannot be delegated and remains with the Manager himself.

A Manager may perform the duties of personnel under his responsib ility, if he is qualified for the duties.

The following clauses explain the duties of the people involved with the Quality Management System.

### General Manager
- Overall responsibility for managing all the activities carried out at LOINTEK, S.L.

### Commercial Manager
- Preparing the quotations for the new projects.
- Contacting with the customer for the contract development.
- Marketing activities.
- Receiving the orders from the customers.

### Purchasing Manager
- Marketing research and analysis in order to ascertain the availability of the material strictly in compliance with Code requirements and with company scheduling besides the economical requirements.
- Preparation of the Purchase Orders.
- Distribution of Purchase Orders

### Industrial Manager
- Overall responsibility for managing all the manufacturing activities carried out at LOINTEK, S.L. These activities include the supervision of all the departments involved with Mechanical Engineering, Planning, Manufacturing and Welding of Code Items.

### Engineering Manager
- Development of drawings and calculations.
- Approval of calculations
- Issuing and controlling drawings valid for fabrication.
- Approval Material / Welding Material Purchase Requisition List.
- Preparation of Manufacturer’s Data Report.

### Production Manager
- Scheduling, coordinating and performing of all activities concerning the shop works having under his direct authority in the plant.
- Storage of materials involved in “ASME” construction.
- Shipment of completed units or parts, and for all maintenance activities.

### Welding Engineer
- Preparing and approving of the Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS).
- Conducting the qualification tests and reporting them on Procedure Qualification Records (PQR). He certifies the PQRs
- Managing the performance qualifications of Welders and Welding Operators and documenting and certifying them on WPQ's/WOPQ’s.
- Maintaining the Welders log up-to date.
- Assingment of welder’s identification symbols

### Project Manager
- Coordinate the different departments involved in this project.
- Planning actualization.
- Communications with the customer .

### Workshop Manager
- Instructing and assigning qualified Welders and Welding Operators to Code jobs.

### Quality Manager
- Reports directly to the General Manager and has the following functions:
- Preparation, approval, distribution, implementation, revision and control of the Quality Manual
- Implementation of specific ations and procedures used to assure conformance of the product to the Code requirements.
- Concurrence and approval of the resolution of the non-conformities.
- Calibration of measuring and test equipment.
- Review and approval of Material Test Reports.
