# Executive Summary: TES 6 Exchanger Tank Inspection

**Date:** January 2, 2019
**To:** Official Having Jurisdiction
**From:** Dr. Al Gonzalez, PhD, PE
**CC:** P. Pajares, Bill Mcvicar, Richard Campbell, Records
**Ref:** Special Inspections Solana, December 21, 2018

## Rebar and Forms Special Re-Inspection

1) All critical dimensions were inspected, and the installation of the steel cage was done on two pedestals. The Bill Mcvair Team diligently made necessary corrections: four sets of dowels were de-assembled and re-installed, and vertical rebar was placed inside the dowels as required by code.

- Forms and cage prior to pouring of reinforced concrete meet the required applicable sections of ACI318-14.
- Emphasis was given to anchor locations and alignment in all six towers. All anchors were installed per blueprint.
- Pre-pouring process was done without delays, corrections were made expeditiously.

## Reinforced Concrete Special Inspection

2) Witnessed the pouring of concrete; slump test performed on site. All registered values were less than 8 inches; samples were taken for additional testing by a third party representing Speedie and Associates (3331 East Wood Street, Phoenix Arizona 85040).

- OSHA best practices observed during the pouring of reinforced concrete.
- Entire process went flawless. Bill Mcvair exercised leadership during the final process of pouring concrete to secure anchor alignment.

## Conclusion

Based on experience, education, and as a registered Engineer in the State of Arizona, I approve the cage assembly and partial pouring for the TES 6 Exchanger tank performed at Solana on December 21, 2018.
