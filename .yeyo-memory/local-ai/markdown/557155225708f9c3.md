## Construction information
- Tank will be N₂ blanketed during stand-by periods.
- Pressurized N₂ will be used to empty the tank during drainage operation.
- Manhole to be located on top. If there is not enough space, manhole will not be installed and access to the tank will be through the pump hole.

## Notes
- To be confirmed by pump supplier
- The weld for transportation caps has to be lower that the use for the hydraulic test and has to protect the nozzle mechanized according ASME 16/25
- The tank can suffer temperature step changes close to 212º F up to a maximum of 718º F.
- Tank will suffer 12 thermal cycles per year, from 29º F to 536º F and from 536º F to 750º F
- Tank will include a pit to give submergence to the pump
- TAG number TBC by UTE during manufacturing process.
- Trace content of salts can remain in the tank after draining after cooling
