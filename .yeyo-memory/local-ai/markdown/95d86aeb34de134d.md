# SS37 Specifications

**Material and Installation Standards**
- **Job No.**: 120006
- **Project**: Solana
- **Material**: Molten Salt 300# (304 SS)
- **Pipe Size**: 2, 4, 6, 24, 30 inches
- **Pipe Schedule**: Sch. 40S (BE), Sch. 80S (BE)
- **Welding**: 304 fusion welded SS in accordance with ASTM A-312, Grade TP304
- **Fittings**: BW fittings, CL 300 BW valves
- **Bolting**: Alloy steel stud bolts ASTM A-193 Grade B7 w/ heavy hex nuts ASTM A-194 Grade 2H
- **Gasket**: 304L SS, ring joint, CL 300 RTJ, ASME B16.20, oval section
- **Gate Valve**: TA58V - CL 300 BW

**Notes**
1. Welding Electrodes: Bare - AWS A5.9 Class ER 308, Coated - AWS A5.4 Class E 308-16, Carbon steel/stainless steel, Bare - AWS A5.9 Class ER 309.
2. Deleted
3. Weldolets are preferred for use at tie-in locations regardless of size change due to difficulty of realignment of piping after cutting.
4. Pressure / temperature allowed in this spec. are limited by F304 flange material. Do not substitute F304L, which has lower allowable pressure / temperature.
5. Gasket material shall be 304L SS only. Do not substitute with 304SS.
