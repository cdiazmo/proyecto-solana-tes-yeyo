# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0004-GAR Rev00 - PT

## Resumen

- Document describes liquid penetrant examination procedure for visible dye penetrant using solvent removable process.
- Applicable to non-porous metallic material, welds, and temporary attachments.
- Requires qualified personnel according to Lointek S.L. and ISO standards.
- Products include ARDROX 996PB, ARDROX 9 PR5, and ARDROX 9 D1B.
- Surface must be clean and dry before applying penetrant.
- Penetrant dwell time depends on temperature with a minimum of 15 minutes.
- Drying time after removing excess penetrant and before applying developer should be between 5 to 30 minutes.
- Developer application must occur within 5 minutes of penetrant removal and immediately after removing excess penetrant.
- Final interpretation begins once the wet developer coating is dry, with a minimum of 10 minutes and maximum of 60 minutes.
- Post-cleaning is necessary when residual products could interfere with subsequent processing or service requirements.
- Time limits for steps in the penetrant examination procedure are detailed in Table 1.
- Indications larger than 1.5mm are considered relevant, with specific criteria for linear and rounded indications.

## Liquid Penetrant Examination General Procedure
### 1.0 Object
This procedure describes the method and requirements for visible dye penetrant examination using the solvent removable process.

### 2.0 Scope
As specified in the particular Inspection Program of the component.

### 4.0 Responsibilities and Authority
#### 4.1 Quality Manager
He has the authority to determine the items that must be checked using this procedure.

#### 4.2 Qualified Personnel
The personnel who will perform the liquid penetrant examinations must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR05. In those cases where the tests are required to meet the EC Pressure Equipment Directive, personnel must be qualified as per ISO 9712/2012 (EN-473) specifies.

### 5.0 Products
Preferably the products to use will be the following ones:
- Dye Penetrant ARDROX 996PB
- Remover ARDROX 9 PR5
- Developer ARDROX 9 D1B
Another similar product could be used with the approval of the Quality Control Manager and they will be demonstrated to the A.I.

### 6.0 Surface Condition
#### 6.1 Cleaning
The areas to be examined and all adjacent areas within at least 1 inch (25 mm.) will be clean and dry before applying the penetrant. The cleaning will be made applying solvent: ARDROX 9PR5.

#### 7.2 Penetrant Dwell Time
The appropriate time of penetration for the range of temperatures indicated on paragraph 6.2.0 of this procedure is at least 15 minutes.

## Drying Time
Drying time after removal of excess penetrant and prior to the application of the developer shall be held to a minimum of 5 minutes and a maximum of 30 minutes.

## DEVELOP ING
The developer shall be applied within 5 minutes after penetrant removal. The developer shall be applied immediately after excess penetrant removal. Prior to applying wet developer to the surface, the developer spray must be thoroughly agitated to ensure adequate mixture. The developer shall be applied on the examined surface getting a fine layer free of local accumulations. The developer shall be applied within 15 minutes after penetrant removal.

## INTERPRETATION
Developing time for final interpretation begins as soon as wet developer coating is dry. Final interpretation shall be made not less than 10 minutes, nor more than 60 minutes. If the surface to be examined is large enough to preclude complete examination, the examination shall be performed in increments. The surface must be observed during the application of the developer to follow the behaviour of the indications and during the test.

## POST-CLEANING
Post cleaning is necessary when residual products could interfere with subsequent processing or with service requirements. When post cleaning is required, it should be conducted as soon as practical after evaluation and documentation with approved products.

## TIME LIMITS FOR STEPS IN PENETRANT EXAMINATION PROCEDURE
| Procedure Step | Time Minimum | Maximum |
|----------------|--------------|---------|
| Drying After Preparation (6.1.0) | 5 minutes - | - |
| Penetrant dwell (7.2.0) | 15 minutes (5ºC to 52ºC) | 20 minutes (5ºC to 10ºC) | Recommended by the manufacture or 2 hours |
| Drying after penetrant removal (7.4.0) | 5 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Developer application (7.5.0) | - | 5 minutes |
| Developing and interpretation time (7.6.0) | 10 minutes | 60 minutes |

## EVALUATION
An indication is the evidence of a mechanical discontinuity. An indication of an imperfection may be larger than the imperfection that causes it; however, the size of the indication is the basis for acceptance evaluation. Only indications with major dimensions greater than 1,5 mm shall be considered relevant.

- Linear indications are those having a length exceeding three times the width of the indication.
- Rounded indications are those which are circular or elliptical and have a length of less than three times the width of the indication.
- Any doubtful or questionable indication will be checked again to verify if it is relevant. Any indication that is believed to be non-relevant shall be regarded as a defect until the indication is eliminated by surface conditioning or evaluated by other non-destructive means and demonstrated to be non-relevant.

When two adjacent indications are separated by a lower distance than the bigger dimension of the smallest one, they should be evaluated as a single continuous indication. The final evaluation of the dimension of the indication should be carried out after the minimum time of development has lapsed and before the indication has decayed until the point of not representing the origin of the imperfection.

## ACCEPT / REJECT CRITERIA
All surfaces to be examined shall be free of:
- Cracks like indications detected irrespective of surface conditions (ASME VIII Div 2 only)
- Relevant linear indication.
- Relevant rounded indications greater than 5 mm.
- Four or more relevant rounded indications in a line separated by 1,5 mm. or less (edge to edge).

According with ASME B31.1:
- Any cracks or linear indications
- Rounded indications greater than 5 mm
- Four or more relevant rounded indications in a line separated by 2 mm. or less (edge to edge)
- Ten or more rounded indications in any 3870mm² of surface with the major dimension of this area not to exceed 150mm with the area taken in the most unfavorable location relative to the indications being evaluated

Only indications with major dimensions greater than 1,5 mm shall be considered relevant.

## FINAL REPORT
The result of the exam will be documented with the corresponding report that will include as minimum the following data:
- Job number
- Drawings

- Procedure and revision

- NDE operator qualifications
- Map or record of indications
- Material and thickness
- Lighting equipment
- Date of examination
- Evaluation
- Acceptance criteria
