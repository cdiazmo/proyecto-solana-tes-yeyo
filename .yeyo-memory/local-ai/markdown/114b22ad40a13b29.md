# Reinforcing Splice Notes

**Spacing**
- **C = COVER**: Cover of the reinforcing bars.
- **db = BAR DIAMETER**: Diameter of the reinforcing bars.
- **Lcc = LENGTH OF CONFINED CORE SEISMIC FRAME COLUMN OR SHEAR WALL BOUNDARY**: Length of confined core seismic frame column or shear wall boundary.

**Bundled Bars**
1. Bundled bars of more than 2 bars (including splice bar) in the same plane are not permitted.
2. Use bar diameter derived from equivalent total area to determine applicable splice type for bundled bars. See schedule below.
3. Do not splice more than one bar in a bundle. See splice diagram for acceptable bundle bar splice diagram.

**Additional Bundled Bar Notes**
- **CS**: Clear spacing.
- Minimum CS: 1".
- **3-BAR BUNDLE**: Splice bar, non-bundled bar, 4-bar bundled.
- **SPLICE LENGTH**: Maximum length of staggered splice: 1:6.

**Bar Location**
- Top bars: Horizontal bars with more than 12" of concrete cast below the bars.
- Other bars: All vertical bars and horizontal bars with less than 12" of concrete cast below bars.

**Development Type**
- Typical: D1 - C ≥ 2db AND S ≥ 4db, D2 - all other conditions, D3 - C < db OR S < 2db.
- Seismic: Provide "Seismic" splice for all seismic splices located outside frame columns and shearwall boundary elements including drags, chords, diaphragm reinforcing and distributed shearwall reinforcing.

**Reinforcing Grade**
- Reinforcing splice length is for grade and reinforcing. For grade 75 reinforcing, multiply the splice lengths in schedule by 1.25.

**Concrete**
- Reinforcing splice length schedule is for normal weight concrete. For lightweight concrete, multiply lengths in schedule by 1.3.

**Notes**
1. All deformed reinforcing bars and threaded rod noted as epoxy shall be installed per manufacturers recommendations. See schedule for minimum embedment lengths.
2. Only non-rebar-cutting drill bits shall be used to drill holes in existing concrete. Care is to be taken when drilling holes so as not to cut any existing reinforcing.
3. Drilled holes shall be cleaned of concrete dust and debris using either a long brush and a vacuum, or a nylon brush and oil-free compressed air. A blow-out bulb may be used if a vacuum or compressed air is not available.
4. Special inspection is required for installation of all adhesive anchors. Inspector to verify and document embedment length and hole preparation and cleanliness. Inspector to verify correct implementation of the manufacturer's instructions for installation.
5. Drill & epoxy to be used only where indicated on drawings.

**Seismic Bar at a Joint**
- Legend: Crosstie, Seismic stirrup, Hoopseismic crosstie, Single-piece hoopseismic stirrup, Seismic hook.
- 90° and 135° angles.
- Minimum CS ≥ 3in.
