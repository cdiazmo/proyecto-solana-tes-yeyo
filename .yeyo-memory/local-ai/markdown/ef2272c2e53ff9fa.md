**Job No:** 120006
**Project:** Solana

## Material and Installation Standards
- **Class 300 Carbon Steel – Piping System**
- **Pipe Sizes:** 22, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 30
- **Wall Thickness:** 0.625”
- **Fittings:** XXS Sch 160, Sch 120, XS Sch 40
- **Bolts:** Alloy steel stud bolts ASTM A-193 Grade B7 with heavy hex nuts
- **Gaskets:** Low carbon steel/soft iron, ring joint, CL 300 RTJ

## Notes
1. All piping shall comply with ANSI B31.3 basic code.
2. Sockolets or weldolets are preferred for tie-in locations.
3. Minimum pipe size in nitrate salt service is 2 inches.
4. Internal cleaning per spec 4521-ESP-ANC-00-61-PS010.
5. Shop and field root pass welds using TIG welding process.
6. All pipe shall be installed clean.
