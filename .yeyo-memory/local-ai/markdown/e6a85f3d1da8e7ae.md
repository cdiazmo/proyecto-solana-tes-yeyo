# 4521-INS-LOI-039-062-0005-GAR Rev.0 Liquid Penetrant Examination Procedure

## Resumen

- Document title: Liquid Penetrant Examination Procedure
- Client: Arizona Solar One, Project: HEX replacement TES Solana
- Prepared by Joana Teixeira, Reviewed by Alex Martin, Approved by Jon Baranda
- Procedure applicable for detecting discontinuities in non-porous metallic materials and welds
- Products include ARDROX 996PB, ARDROX 9 PR5, and ARDROX 9 D1B
- Surface cleaning with ARDROX 9PR5, drying by evaporation or forced air
- Penetrant dwell time varies based on temperature
- Excess penetrant removed using organic solvent, then cleaned with moistened rags
- Drying time after penetrant removal: 5-30 minutes.
- Developer application within 5 minutes after penetrant removal.
- Final interpretation between 10-60 minutes.
- Post-cleaning required for residual products interference.

## Liquid Penetrant Examination General Procedure

**Revision:** 0
**Issued by:** Q.D.
**Supplier Code:** 195001-PLP-01
**Date:** 05/12/16

### 1.0 Object
This procedure describes the method and requirements for visible dye penetrant examination using the solvent removable process. This procedure is applicable for the detection of discontinuities that are open to the surface in components of non-porous metallic material, welds, and temporary attachments.

### 2.0 Scope
As specified in the particular Inspection Program of the component.

### 3.0 Codes
- ASME Code, Section V – Non Destructive Examination (2015 Edition)
- ASME Code, Section VIII Div 1 y 2 – Pressure Vessels (2015 Edition)
- ASME Section I, Power Boilers (2015 Edition)
- ASME B 31.1 Power Piping (2012 Edition)
- WRITTEN PRACTICE ASME. QCM.PR05
- SNT-TC-1A (2006 Edition)

### 4.0 Responsibilities and Authority
#### 4.1 Quality Manager
He has the authority to determine the items that must be checked using this procedure.

#### 4.2 Qualified Personnel
The personnel who will perform the liquid penetrant examinations must be qualified according to Lointek S.L. Written Practice ASME.QCM.PR05. In those cases where the tests are required to meet the EC Pressure Equipment Directive, personnel must be qualified as per ISO 9712/2012 (EN-473) specifies.

The results must be interpreted by a Level II or III qualified and certified personnel.

A Change of a requirement in Table 2 (attached) identified as an essential variable shall require requalification of the written procedure by demonstration. All changes off essential or non essential variables from those specified within this written procedure shall require revision of the written procedure.

### 5.0 Products
Preferably the products to use will be the following ones:
- Dye Penetrant ARDROX 996PB
- Remover ARDROX 9 PR5
- Developer ARDROX 9 D1B
Another similar product could be used with the approval of the Quality Control Manager and they will be demonstrated to the A.I.

The products must be compatible with the material to examine and with the final use of the examined piece. It is not allowed to mix different makers' products.

The products used for the examination in Nickel base alloys, austenitic or duplex stainless steel and titanium must be according with Appendix II Article 6 Section V of the ASME CODE.

### 6.0 Surface Condition
#### 6.1 Cleaning
The areas to be examined and all adjacent areas within at least 1 inch (25 mm.) will be clean (free of dust, oxide, welding splatter, oils, machining marks or any other that can interfere with the exam or the interpretation) and will be dry before applying the penetrant. The cleaning will be made applying solvent: ARDROX 9PR5. Drying after cleaning of the surface to be examined shall be accomplished by normal evaporation or with forced hot or cold air. After all visible traces of the cleaner have been removed, a minimum of five minutes drying time shall be established prior to application of the penetrant.

#### 6.2 Temperature
The temperature of the penetrant and the examined surface will be between the 5ºC and 52ºC throughout the examination period. Local heating or cooling is permitted.

#### 6.3 Lighting
For visible penetrant, the minimum light intensity will be 1000 lux. The light intensity shall be measured with light meter prior to the evaluation of indications.

Light meters shall be calibrated at least once a year or whenever the meter has been repaired. If meters have not been in use for one year or more, calibration shall be done before being used.

### 7.0 Description of the Process
#### 7.1 Application of the Penetrant
The liquid penetrant examination shall be performed in accordance with minimum and maximum listed in Table 1 (attached). The examined area must be fully covered with the penetrant. This application will be done using the following methods: dipping, brushing, or spraying.

#### 7.2 Penetrant Dwell Time
The appropriate time of penetration for the range of temperatures indicated on paragraph 6.2.0 of this procedure is at least of 15 minutes. During this period the penetrant liquid shall not be allowed to dry, and shall be applied several times if required. For temperatures from 5ºC to 10ºC, minimum time shall be 20 minutes. The maximum penetrant dwell time shall not exceed the maximum recommended by the manufacture or 2 hours.

#### 7.3 Excess Penetrant Removal
The removal method depends on the product that is used.
- Organic solvent: First the excess penetrant shall be removed by wiping with cloth or absorbent paper, repeating the operation until most traces of penetrant have been removed. Later the cleaning process continues with rags moistened with solvent until the examined area and the adjacent ones will be fully cleaned. It is not allowed to apply the solvent directly on the examined area during the cleaning previous to the developer application.

#### 7.4 Drying After Excess Penetrant Removal
The examined surfaces must be totally dry, before applying the developer. The surface may be dried by normal evaporation, blotting, wiping or forced air.

# LIQUID PENETRANT EXAMINATION GENERAL PROCEDURE

**Drying Time**: After removal of excess penetrant and prior to developer application, hold time between 5-30 minutes.

## DEVELOPMENT
Developer should be applied within 5 minutes after penetrant removal. Thoroughly agitate wet developer before applying to surface for adequate mixture. Apply on examined surface with a fine layer free of local accumulations. Developer must be applied within 15 minutes after penetrant removal.

## INTERPRETATION
Developing time begins as soon as wet developer coating is dry. Final interpretation should be made between 10-60 minutes. Large surfaces may require incremental examination. Surface observation during developer application is necessary to follow indication behavior and test results.

## POST-CLEANING
Post-cleaning is required when residual products could interfere with subsequent processing or service requirements. Conduct post-cleaning as soon as practical after evaluation and documentation with approved products.

## TIME LIMITS FOR STEPS IN PENETRANT EXAMINATION PROCEDURE
| Procedure Step | Time Minimum | Maximum |
|----------------|--------------|---------|
| Drying After Preparation | 5 minutes | - |
| Penetrant Dwell | 15 minutes (5°C to 52°C) | 20 minutes (5°C to 10°C) or recommended by manufacturer |
| Drying After Penetrant Removal | 5 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Developer Application | - | 5 minutes |
| Developing and Interpretation Time | 10 minutes | 60 minutes |

## EVALUATION
An indication is evidence of a mechanical discontinuity. Only indications with major dimensions greater than 1.5 mm are considered relevant. Linear and rounded indications have specific criteria for relevance. Any doubtful or questionable indication should be rechecked.

## ACCEPT / REJECT CRITERIA
All surfaces to be examined must be free of specified defects according to ASME I, ASME VIII Div.1 & 2, and ASME B31.1 standards. Only indications with major dimensions greater than 1.5 mm are considered relevant for acceptance criteria.

## FINAL REPORT
The result of the examination will be documented in a report including job number, drawings, piece description, equipment and products used, procedure and revision, type of penetrant, remover, developer, NDE operator qualifications, map or record of indications, material and thickness, lighting equipment, date of examination, evaluation, and acceptance criteria.
