## Check Valves Specification
- **FC86 (Sch. 120 bore):** Class 1500 buttweld, 3” - 12”, cast or forged carbon steel lift check, short pattern, ASTM A216 GR WCB or ASTM A105 pressure seal cap, 13 CR lift disc, stellited seat, cap bolting per ANSI B16.34, dimensions per ANSI B16.10, pressure tested per MSS SP-61 or API Std. 598.
- **FC86 (Sch. 140 bore):** Similar to FC86 (Sch. 120 bore).
- **FC86 (Sch. 160 bore):** Similar to FC86 (Sch. 120 bore).
- **FC120:** Class 1500 socketweld, ¼” - 2”, forged carbon steel lift check, bolted cap, OS & Y, satellite faced 13 CR lift disc, stellited seat, spiral wound 304 SS/flexible graphite, soft iron, confined graphite or metal inserted flexible graphite cap gasket. Cap bolting per ANSI B16.34 socketwelding ends per ANSI B16.11, pressure tested per MSS SP-61 or API Std. 598.

## Ball Valves Specification
- **DF1 (Class 300 threaded):** ½” - 2”, ductile iron ball, non-inline maintenance, hard chrome plated stem, electrically grounded ball, reinforced TFE seats, teflon seals.
- **DF41 (Class 150 RF flanged):** 3” - 14” (6” and larger gear operated), ductile iron ball, non-inline maintenance, hard chrome plated stem, electrically grounded ball, reinforced TFE seats, teflon seals.
- **FF3 (Class 300 socketweld):** ½” - 2”, steel ball, inline maintenance, SS steam and electrically grounded ball, teflon seats, flexible graphite stem packing and body gaskets, fire-resistant trim.

## Gas Cock/Plug Valves Specification
- **EK1 (Class 200 threaded):** ½” - 2”, iron cock, all iron body, plug, washer and integral seats.
- **EK2 (Class 200 FF flanged):** 3” - 4”, iron cock, all iron body, plug and stem. Integral seats.
- **FD61 (Class 600 RF flanged):** 1” - 4”, cast carbon steel body, ASTM A216 GR WC B, CST iron plug, molybdisulfide coated; 400 SS stem, PTFE coated, stem packing-graphite/TFE, pressure energized. Valve to be per API6D, internally grounded and rated fire safe per API607 and RP; wrench type operator.
- **FD62 (Class 600 RF flanged):** 6” - 10”, cast carbon steel body, ASTM A216 GR WC B, CST iron plug, molybdisulfide coated; alloy steel stem, PTFE coated, stem packing-graphite/TFE, pressure energized. Valve to be per API6D, internally grounded and rated fire safe per API607 and RP6F; gear type operator.
