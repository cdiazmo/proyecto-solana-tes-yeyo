# 4521-ESP-ANC-00-60-N180

## Resumen

- This document is a specification for job number 120006, covering surface preparation and painting of various steel fabrications.
- The contract allows alternate billing cycles other than thirty days upon request from the owner's designated agent.
- All materials must be used as specified in the standards or approved by the Purchaser in writing.
- Painting work must comply with the requirements of the paint manufacturer and follow specific application conditions.
- Contract allows alternate billing cycles other than thirty days.
- Confidential information must not be shared without permission.
- Surface preparation materials include sand and steel grit for carbon steel, with specific requirements for stainless steel.
- Shop painting procedures are governed by Steel Structures Painting Council Specification SSPC-PA-1-64.
- Surfaces like sight glasses and equipment identification nameplates should not be painted.
- Machined surfaces must be masked during shipment to protect against abrasive cleaning.
- Fabricated items must be supported properly before painting to prevent damage.
- Paint application must follow vendor instructions for mixing, thinning, curing, etc.

## Scope

## Surface Preparation Materials
### Carbon Steel
Materials used in abrasive blast cleaning shall be 16 - 30 mesh sand, 40 - 50 mesh steel grit. Particulate sizes larger or smaller than the ranges designated may be used with the permission of the Purchaser provided they produce the required profile depth. Surface preparation details will be per paint standard or specification.

### Stainless Steel
Care must be taken to ensure mechanical cleaning methods do not contaminate stainless steel surfaces.
- Where sand blasting is applicable; (a) sandblast equipment must be cleaned before use to assure that all old iron contaminated sand has been removed, (b) sand and mineral grit must be iron and zinc free, and (c) sandblast material must be new and not recovered/re-used.
- Cleaning brushes and tools used must not contain iron and they must not have been previously used on iron containing materials.
- Do not use chlorinated solvents on stainless steel surfaces.

## Procedures and Workmanship
### Paint Vendor's Instructions
The paint vendor’s instructions and the applicable sections of Steel Structures Painting Council Specification SSPC-PA-1-64 shall govern shop painting procedures and workmanship.

### Shop Painting
Shop painting shall consist of the proper number of coats of paint material as noted in Paragraph 3. Method of application and dry film thickness (DFT) of each coat (in mils) shall be as shown in standards or specifications.

### Surfaces Not to Be Painted
The following surfaces shall not be painted: sight glasses and gauges, equipment identification nameplates, valve stems, plastic, rubber, copper, aluminum, galvanized steel, pre-finished metal furniture, glass and ceramic surfaces.

## Masking
### Machined Surfaces
Machined surfaces of fabricated items shall be masked with durable material that will protect the surface against abrasive blast cleaning, paint, and contamination. Unless other shipping protection is specified, masking shall remain during shipment.

### Masked Flange Faces and Pipe Lengths
Mask cleaned flange faces and cleaned pipe lengths, and structural members with a durable material, one inch from each end or edge. Masking shall remain during shipment.

## Surface Preparation
### Fabricated Items
Surfaces of all uncoated fabricated items shall be prepared for painting as specified on paint system standards or specifications (refer to Par. 3.1 for standards to be used). Blasted surfaces shall be painted on the same day before rust bloom occurs.

### Placement of Work
Fabricated items to be painted shall be supported to minimize contact with the painted surfaces. Freshly blast-cleaned steel shall be supported on clean supports and when painted shall be high enough to eliminate the possibility of abrasive being blown into the paint. Dirty bucks shall be covered with polyethylene or other clean, suitable material. Vessels may be supported on unpainted leg or lug surfaces.

### Application of Paint
Follow the paint vendor's instructions for mixing, thinning, pot-life, application, curing, and recoating. Remove all residues from cleaned surfaces with a clean brush or with dry, oil free compressed air.

### 4.6 Quality Control
- Unless otherwise agreed, the Purchaser, or the inspector as his representative, will observe work in progress and will inspect completed work for compliance with specified end conditions.
- The end condition of blast cleaning described in Par. 4.5 will be judged by visual comparison with profile panels such as Clemtex, Ltd. surface profile gauges and cleanliness standards such as NACE Standard TM 01-70.

### 4.7 Painted Surfaces
- Measurement of paint dry film thickness will be made with a calibrated Mikrotest gauge. Failure to meet specified thickness requirements is cause for rejection of the work.
- Work may be rejected if there is evidence of inadequate drying or curing, or of poor workmanship. Acceptable finished work must be free of detrimental dust and dirt inclusions, abrasions, over-spray, mud cracks, pinholes, runs, and sags, and must be uniform in color and even and continuous in appearance.

### 4.8 Correction of Defects
- The Supplier will be advised as quickly as possible of any defective or unacceptable work so that corrections can be made with a minimum of delay.
- Inadequate surface preparation and rejected portions of the paint shall be corrected as agreed upon by the Purchaser and the Supplier.

### 4.9 Handling of Painted Items
- Painted steel shall not be handled or moved until paint is sufficiently dry and cured to minimize abrasion and scarring.
- Steel cables or chains are not to be used for lifting or loading. All handling, lifting and loading shall be done with belt slings.
- Painted steel loaded for shipment shall be protected from direct steel-to-steel contact with wood or other suitable spacers.
- Cable, strap, or chain used to tie-down and secure steel loaded for shipment is separated from painted steel surfaces suitable wood or rubber spacers or mats.

### 4.10 Inspection
Purchaser has the authority and privilege of inspection of all work to assure conformance with specifications. The supplier responsible for surface preparation and painting shall notify Purchaser one week prior to the date the material will be ready for painting.
