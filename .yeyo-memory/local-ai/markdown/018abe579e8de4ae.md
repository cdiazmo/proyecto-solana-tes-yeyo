# Drain Back Tank Pit Design

## Overview
The Drain Back tank pit of Solana Generating Station is designed.

## Description
- **Shape and Dimensions**: The pit has dimensions of 24’11”x 15’9”, with an inner height of 10’6” and a slab thickness of 3’0”.
- **Support**: Supported over subbase by filling with suitable material (type A1 or A3 compacted 95% according to AASTHO T1-180).
- **Steel Structure**: Anchored by several post-installed bolts on the slab.
