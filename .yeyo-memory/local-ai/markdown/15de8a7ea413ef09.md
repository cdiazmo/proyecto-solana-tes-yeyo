# Engineering Standards and Practices
## Material and Installation Standards
### Underground HDPE – Piping System Project: Solana
#### Notice of Alternate Billing Cycle
This contract allows the owner to require billings or estimates in billing cycles other than thirty days. Written descriptions are available from the owner or agent.
#### Notice of Extended Payment Provision
Owner can make payment within 180 days after certification and approval of billings and estimates.
#### Notice of Extended Certification and Approval Period Provision
Owner can certify and approve billings and estimates within 30 days after receipt.
### Specifications
- **Service Range (nps 3 – 36):**
  - 202 PSIG @ 73°F
  - 158 PSIG @ 90°F
  - 127 PSIG @ 110°F
  - 101 PSIG @ 140°F
- **Service Range (nps 42 – 48):**
  - 126 PSIG @ 73°F
  - 98 PSIG @ 100°F
  - 79 PSIG @ 120°F
  - 63 PSIG @ 140°F
### Construction
- **Pipe:** High-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe from Performance Pipe or approved equal.
- **Fittings:** Butt fusion, flange adapters, and convoluted back-up rings. Controlled by AWWA C901 for 3” & smaller, AWWA C906, DIPS for 4 ” & larger.
### Materials
- **High-density polyethylene (HDPE):** PE3408/PE4710 per ASTM D-1248 and cell Class PE445474C per ASTM D-3350 with Dimension Ratio = DR 11, DR 17, or DR 26.
### Fittings
- **AWWA C901:** Butt fusion fittings for branch connections; mechanical fittings not allowed without approval.
- **Flanges:** HDPE DIPS flange adapter with epoxy coated convoluted ductile iron back-up ring or HDPE MJ adapter with stiffener as appropriate.
- **Bolting:** Carbon steel stud bolts A-307 Grade B w/heavy hex nuts.
- **Gasket:** 1/8” thick, full-face CL 150 drilling, butyl rubber: 50 - 70 Shore A durometer hardness.
### Valves
- **Gate Valve:** CA40 –CL 125 FF flanged.
- **Butterfly Valve:** DH48 – CL 150 FF (lugged body).
### Notes
1. Backfill must be in full contact with the pipe and compacted to over 85% of Standard Proctor density. Installation per ASTM D-2321, ASTM D-2774, and Plastic Pipe Institute Report TR-31/9-79.
2. All underground flanged connections must be tar coated for protection.
3. All underground valves must be installed with a valve box.
4. Piping must be marked with BLUE stripe for water service, GREEN for wastewater, drain or sewer service.
