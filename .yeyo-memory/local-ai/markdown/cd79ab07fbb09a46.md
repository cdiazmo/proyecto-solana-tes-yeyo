# 4521-ESP-ANC-00-62-0003 Mineral fiber insulation

## Resumen

- The document specifies standards for mineral fiber insulation.
- Insulation can be used on surfaces up to 1200 °F (649 °C).
- Available sizes include blankets and boards with specific dimensions and densities.
- Contract allows alternate billing cycles and extended payment/provision periods.
- Insulation must meet specific ASTM requirements for loose fill and preformed pipe insulation.
- Available sizes range from 36" to 42" in various thicknesses.
- Larger piping requires mineral fiber blanket as specified.
- Only approved vendors are allowed: Fibrex, Rock Wool, IIG, Lewco.

## General
This specification sets forth acceptable standards of materials for mineral fiber insulation.

## Temperature Range
This specified insulation, all forms, can be used on surfaces having operating temperatures up to a maximum of 1200 °F (649 °C).

## Materials
### Mineral Fiber Blanket (Flexible)
- **Insulation:** Conforms to ASTM Specification C 55 3, Type VII with additional requirements:
  - Nominal density: 8 lbs/ft³ (128 kg/m³)
  - Facing: None
  - Cumulative shot content per ASTM C 612: <30%
  - Meet requirements of ASTM specification: C 795 (“Thermal Insulation For Use In Contact With Austenitic Stainless Steel”)
- **Available Sizes:**
  - Length: 24”, 36”, 48”, 96” (600 mm, 900 mm, 1200 mm, 2400 mm)
  - Width: 12”, 24”, 36”, 48” (300 mm, 600 mm, 900 mm, 1200 mm)
  - Thickness: 1”- 6” in ½” increments (25 mm - 150 mm in 12 mm increments)

### Mineral Fiber Board and Block
- **Insulation:** Conforms to ASTM Specification C 612, Type IV with additional requirements:
  - Nominal density (Board): 8 lbs/ft³ (128 kg/m³)
  - Nominal density (Block): 12 lbs/ft³ (192 kg/m³)
  - Facing: None
  - Cumulative shot content per ASTM C 612: <30%
  - Compressive Strength-Block @ 10% Deform: 120 lb/ft² (0.06 kg/cm²)
  - Meet requirements of ASTM specification: C 795 (“Thermal Insulation For Use In Contact With Austenitic Stainless Steel”)
- **Available Sizes:**
  - Length: 36”, 48” (900 mm, 1200 mm)
  - Width: 24”, 36”, 48” (600 mm, 900 mm, 1200 mm)
  - Thickness: 1” - 5” in ½” increments (25 mm - 125 mm in 12 mm increments)

## Specifications
### Loose Fill Mineral Fiber
- Conforms to ASTM C764, Type II (poured application)
- Nominal density: 8 - 16 lbs/ft³ (128 - 256 kg/m³)
- Meets requirements of ASTM C795
- Packaging: Manufacturer’s standard containers

### Preformed Mineral Fiber Pipe Insulation
- Conforms to ASTM C547, Type II
- Nominal density: 8 lbs/ft³ (128 kg/m³)
- Facing: None
- Cumulative shot content per ASTM C612: <30%
- Meets requirements of ASTM C795

### Available Sizes
- Length: 36” (900 mm)
- Width: Pipe diameters ½” thru 42”
- Thickness: ½” - 6” in ½” increments (12 mm - 150 mm in 12 mm increments)

### Insulation for Piping Larger than 42"
- Mineral fiber blanket as specified in section 3.1
