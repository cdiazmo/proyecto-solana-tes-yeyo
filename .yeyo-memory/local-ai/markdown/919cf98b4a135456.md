# General Notes
1. The complete definition and codification of the supports for construction are included in "0000-LIS-AEE-000-063-YYYY_Rev00_Support List".
2. General pipe parameters as diameter, loads, orientation and displacements, required for support calculation or its validation, are included in the Support List.
3. Please check "comments" in the "typical support" sheet inside the mentioned support list for clarifications or special considerations about typical support.
4. If not exist a special definition of the material, all elements of this catalogue will be carbon steel S 275JR G2 or similar. For shoes to be mounted in alloy steel pipe, these shoes will be fabricated in alloy steel P11 or P22 to value by the supplier. For a different material considered in this standard, will be included a note in comments. The auxiliaries structures of the supports will take a cover hot galvanized with a minimum 85 microns.
5. Dimensions in inches.
6. All the diameters are in inches according ASME B36.10.
7. The position and orientation of the support will be defined by the reference point indicated bracket on the isometric.
8. For items that must be designed by the supplier, the modification by the supplier of the design parameters should be approved by the responsible of EPC.
9. The pipes shall be temporarily supported during installation until the final support has been installed.
10. All springs must remain blocked until the hydrostatic test has been realized.
11. All shoes will fabricated for the installation of non-rotating trunnion but only non-rotating trunnion will be installed when the support is not simple support (see typical P12).
12. Indications weld thickness:
12.2. Welding and heat treatment of elements supports directly welded pipes are fabricated according to ASME B31.1 Chapter V.
